\begin{abstract}
We audit the manuscript \emph{A Dependency-Complete Mathematical Reconstruction of the IUT--ABC Argument}, version 6.0, and the source papers on which its decisive numerical step depends.  The audit has a definite outcome: version 6.0 does not prove the $abc$ conjecture.  It proves a conditional implication whose antecedent contains a concrete global theta-values inequality and an IUT IV componentwise upper estimate.  Neither antecedent is derived in that manuscript from independently established theorems.

We then separate the valid parts from the unresolved ones.  We give complete proofs of the local Tate inertia matrix, the generation of $\SL_2(\F_\ell)$ by two nonparallel transvections, and a prime-escape theorem that chooses an auxiliary prime avoiding the complete finite set of bad residue characteristics and all Tate orders.  This improves the auxiliary-prime stage: if the forbidden logarithmic mass is $O(\log c)$, then one may choose $\log\ell=O(\log\log c)=o(\log c)$.  We also prove a local counterexample showing that a single mod-$\ell$ representation is blind to the magnitude of a Tate order modulo $\ell$ and therefore cannot by itself yield the height--radical inequality.

For the IUT/Arithmetic-Teichmuller route, we formulate the exact missing theorem as a normed, weight-preserving Rosetta comparison.  It must simultaneously preserve the concrete $j^2$-weighted theta values, the tensor cross norm, the procession weights, and the possible-image locus under Ind1--Ind3.  A set-theoretic or categorical correspondence is insufficient.  We explain why the global theorem claimed in Arithmetic Teichmuller Spaces III reduces to a local prototype plus this normed comparison, and why the Scholze--Stix objection directly targets the missing compatibility.

Finally, we examine possible bypasses through modularity, torsion fields, $S$-unit equations, and Mason--Stothers-type differentiation.  Each route is reduced to a precise theorem at least as strong as a known open form of Szpiro or $abc$; none presently closes the argument.  Consequently this paper neither proves nor disproves $abc$.  It supplies a rigorous rejection of the version-6 candidate proof, preserves the unconditional mathematics, and isolates a falsifiable list of exact lemmas that any future proof must establish.
\end{abstract}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!6,colframe=black!70,title=Main verdict]
The version-6 manuscript is \emph{not} an unconditional proof of $abc$.  Its central theorem has the logical form
\[
  H_{\Theta}\ \wedge\ H_{\mathrm{upper}}\ \wedge\ H_{\mathrm{chart}}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad abc,
\]
where $H_{\Theta}$ is explicitly introduced there as a ``Source Hypothesis''.  The present audit does not establish these three hypotheses from previously verified theorems.  Therefore no theorem of the form
\[
  \textnormal{Established mathematics}\Longrightarrow abc
\]
has been obtained.
\end{tcolorbox}
