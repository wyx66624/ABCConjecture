# Galois-averaged cancellation of fixed torsion packets

At a split Tate place, let `C` be the canonical cyclic order-`ell` line and put

\[
 A_\ell=(\ell-1)/12,
 \qquad
 B_\ell=-(\ell-1)/(12\ell).
\]

For any fixed weights `w_D` on `P^1(F_ell)`, define

\[
 S_C(w)=A_\ell w_C+B_\ell\sum_{D\ne C}w_D.
\]

Then

\[
 \frac1{\ell+1}\sum_C S_C(w)=0,
\]

because every line is canonical once and noncanonical `ell` times, and
`A_ell+ell B_ell=0`.

Hence, under transitive projective Galois image, the normalized product formula
over the field of definition of any fixed place-independent packet averages
away its entire q-slope. This is a strict no-go theorem for the naive fixed
three-line determinant in the first programme draft.

The corrected classical route must use locally adaptive canonical-line
filtrations and prove an adelic slope/selection inequality. The cyclic-line
field different bound remains useful for the discrepancy, but does not itself
avoid the cancellation.
