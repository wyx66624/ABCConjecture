# Local `Ism` actions preserve the normalized Tate radius

**Author:** ChatGPT  
**Status:** mathematical proof and Lean formalization of the finite-dimensional
core completed.  No IUT IV component estimate or abc statement is assumed.

## 1. Why this is the next source-level input

The complete-packet and direct-summand theorems show that Ind2 must be modeled
fiberwise over each fixed theta label.  To pass from the formal radial shadow
to the actual local `Ism` actions of IUT III, one must justify that a local
field isomorphism does not alter the normalized nonarchimedean radius.

For finite extensions of a local base field this is not an additional
conjecture.  It follows from invariance of the algebraic field norm under base-
field automorphisms.

## 2. Algebraic field norm as a determinant

Let `K/F` be a finite extension of degree `d`, and let

\[
 m_x:K\longrightarrow K,\qquad y\longmapsto xy
\]

be multiplication by `x`, regarded as an `F`-linear map.  The algebraic field
norm is

\[
 N_{K/F}(x)=\det_F(m_x).                          \tag{2.1}
\]

Let `\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_F(K)`.  For every `y\in K`,

\[
 (\sigma\circ m_x\circ\sigma^{-1})(y)
 =\sigma(x\sigma^{-1}(y))
 =\sigma(x)y
 =m_{\sigma(x)}(y).
\]

Thus

\[
 m_{\sigma(x)}=\sigma\,m_x\,\sigma^{-1}.         \tag{2.2}
\]

Choose an `F`-basis of `K`.  If `P` and `Q=P^{-1}` are the matrices of
`\sigma` and `\sigma^{-1}`, and `A_x` is the multiplication matrix of `x`,
then

\[
 A_{\sigma(x)}=P A_x Q,
 \qquad PQ=I.                                    \tag{2.3}
\]

Taking determinants gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 N_{K/F}(\sigma(x))
 &=\det(PA_xQ)\\
 &=\det(P)\det(A_x)\det(Q)\\
 &=\det(A_x)\det(PQ)\\
 &=N_{K/F}(x).
 \end{aligned}                                   \tag{2.4}
\]

This proof requires no continuity or analytic approximation.

## 3. Normalized local absolute value

For a finite extension of nonarchimedean local fields, the normalized absolute
value can be written in field-norm form

\[
 |x|_K
 =|N_{K/F}(x)|_F^{1/d},                         \tag{3.1}
\]

with the conventional normalization on the base field.  Combining (2.4) and
(3.1) yields

\[
 \boxed{|\sigma(x)|_K=|x|_K.}                    \tag{3.2}
\]

In particular,

\[
 |\sigma(q)|_K=|q|_K.                            \tag{3.3}
\]

Hence for every natural exponent `m`, the two principal regions generated by
`q^m` and `\sigma(q)^m` have the same radius:

\[
 \boxed{
   \sigma(q)^m\mathcal O_K=q^m\mathcal O_K
 }
                                                        \tag{3.4}
\]

when principal regions are normalized by radius, as in the repository's Tate-
power-region construction.  Thus an actual local `Ism` action cannot change
the squared-label radial contribution.
