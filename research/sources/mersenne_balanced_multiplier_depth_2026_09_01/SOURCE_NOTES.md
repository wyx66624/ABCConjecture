# Source notes: balanced Mersenne multiplier/depth localization

Access date: 2026-09-01.

This directory archives the two primary papers newly needed by
`research/ABC_MERSENNE_BALANCED_MULTIPLIER_DEPTH_LOCALIZATION_2026_09_01.md`.
The earlier fixed-order, cyclotomic, and Wieferich sources remain archived in
`research/sources/mersenne_prime_layer_radical_2026_09_01/`.

## Yamada (2010)

- Archived files: `Yamada_2006_p_adic_Fermat_quotient_bound.pdf` and its
  `pypdf` text extraction.
- Author: Tomohiro Yamada.
- Title: *A note on the paper by Bugeaud and Laurent “Minoration effective de
  la distance p-adique entre puissances de nombres algébriques”*.
- Journal: *Journal of Number Theory* **130** (2010), 1889--1897.
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2010.02.018>.
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607072>.
- Archived PDF endpoint: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0607072>.
- Exact evidence: Theorem 1.2, equation (7), states for every prime `p`
  \[
  v_p(2^{p-1}-1)
  \le
  \left\lfloor
  283(p-1)\frac{\log3}{\log p}\frac{\log6}{\log p}
  \right\rfloor+4.
  \]
  This is unconditional and pointwise.  It is a valuation bound, not an
  upper-density theorem for Wieferich primes.  The Mersenne report combines
  it with the exact identity
  `v_p(2^(p-1)-1)=v_p(2^ord_p(2)-1)` and multiplier injectivity.

## Li--Zhao (2026)

- Archived files: `Li_Zhao_2026_higher_Wieferich_prime_ideals.pdf` and its
  `pypdf` text extraction.
- Authors: Ruofan Li and Jiuzhou Zhao.
- Title: *Non-Wieferich property of prime ideals and a conjecture of Erdös*.
- Primary record: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12753v1>.
- Archived PDF endpoint: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.12753>.
- Exact evidence: Definition 1.1 calls a prime ideal `alpha`-Wieferich at
  level `r` when the cyclic groups generated by `alpha` modulo the two
  successive prime powers have the same size.  Theorem 1.1 fixes one prime
  ideal and one non-torsion `alpha`, then produces an integer `v` such that
  the successive kernel size is periodic for every `r>v`; for an unramified
  prime ideal it is never one beyond that threshold.
- Quantifier warning: `v` depends on the fixed prime ideal and on `alpha`.
  In `K=Q`, this describes the familiar stabilization of order growth after
  the initial valuation.  It does **not** give a threshold uniform over
  varying rational primes and does not bound the localized sum of initial
  base-two super-Wieferich depths.

## Other primary papers checked

- P. Erdős and M. Ram Murty, *On the order of a modulo p* (1999): for each
  prescribed positive function `epsilon(p) -> 0`, the exceptional set is
  `o(x/log x)`; the theorem is global and unweighted.
- M. Ram Murty and François Séguin, *Prime divisors of sparse values of
  cyclotomic polynomials and Wieferich primes* (2019): exact-order valuation
  dictionary and weighted Brun--Titchmarsh input, but no high-multiplier
  depth estimate.
- Igor E. Shparlinski, *Fermat quotients: Exponential sums, value set and
  primitive roots* (2011), <https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.3909>: the large-sieve
  estimates average over intervals in the base variable; they do not bound
  the fixed base `2` exact-order/Wieferich intersection.
- Nic Fellini and M. Ram Murty, *Wieferich primes in number fields and the
  conjectures of Ankeny--Artin--Chowla and Mordell* (2026): the quantitative
  non-Wieferich results use Masser's number-field abc conjecture or finiteness
  of super-Wieferich primes.

The archived copies of the first, second, and fourth papers in this list are
in `research/sources/mersenne_prime_layer_radical_2026_09_01/` and are not
duplicated here.

## Extraction and integrity

The `.txt` files were generated with `pypdf 6.10.0` for searchability.  The
PDF is authoritative where mathematical typography matters.  `SHA256SUMS`
records every file in this directory except itself.

Run `RETRIEVE_SOURCES.ps1 -Destination <path>` from the repository root to
download fresh PDF copies and print their SHA-256 hashes.  A publisher or
arXiv byte-level change does not by itself imply a change in mathematical
content; compare the theorem statements as well as the hashes.
