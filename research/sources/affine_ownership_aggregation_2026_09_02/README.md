# Primary-source seal for affine ownership aggregation

This directory records the primary literature checked for the affine
ownership maximal-intersection report dated 2026-09-02. SOURCES.json
distinguishes theorem inputs from comparison-only sources.

Only the Rosser--Schoenfeld Mertens product estimate is used as an external
analytic input, through its coarse logarithmic product consequence. The
divisor estimate is the standard elementary divisor bound, and the report
proves its square-divisor excess estimate directly.

The hypergraph-container papers, the Szemeredi--Trotter paper, and the
Bateman--Grosswald and Golomb powerful-number papers are background
comparisons. No container hypothesis or incidence theorem is asserted or
used. The official Bateman--Grosswald endpoint was bot-blocked and the
Golomb article was paywalled during retrieval, so their DOI metadata is
sealed without pretending that a PDF was archived.

Verify every archived file against SHA256SUMS.txt before reuse.
