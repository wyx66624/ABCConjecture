# Latest abc-facing primary-source audit

Retrieved on 2026-09-02 from the versioned arXiv PDF endpoints listed in
`source-metadata.json`.  The adjacent UTF-8 text files were extracted with
`pypdf` for local search; the PDFs are the primary artifacts.

- Letendre proposes a stronger abc-type conjecture involving
  `H(n)=rad(n)/(log rad(n))^omega(n)`.  Its short-interval omega theorems are
  conditional on that new conjecture.  It is a source of testable interfaces,
  not an unconditional abc result.
- Zhou announces explicit rational abc-type inequalities by applying a
  modified IUT construction.  Proposition 1.9 explicitly invokes the
  mu-six version of IUT III, Corollary 3.12.  This source therefore does not
  remove the source-faithful comparison obligation isolated by the repository.
- Falk--Harrington--Jones characterize monogenicity of a trinomial family by
  generalized Wieferich/Lucas congruences.  Their results give exact local
  congruence structure, not a density or critical-count upper bound for the
  base-two depth-three packet.

No theorem in these files is silently introduced as a Lean axiom.  Preprint
claims are attributed to their authors and kept distinct from repository
proofs.
