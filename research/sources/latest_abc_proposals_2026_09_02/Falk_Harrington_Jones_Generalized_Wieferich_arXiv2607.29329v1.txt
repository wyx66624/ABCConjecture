AND MONOGENIC TRINOMIALS
AMY FALK, JOSHUA HARRINGTON, AND LENNY JONES
Abstract.Letb≥2 be an integer and letp≥3 be a prime. We say thatp
is ageneralized Wieferich prime baseb, or more succinctly, abase-bWieferich
prime,ifb p−1 ≡1 (modp 2). Whenb= 2,pis also known simply as a
Wieferich prime. Letf(x)∈Z[x] be a monic polynomial of degreeN≥2. We
say thatf(x) is monogenic iff(x) is irreducible overQand{1, θ, θ 2, . . . , θN−1 }
is a basis for the ring of integers ofQ(θ), wheref(θ) = 0. Recently, the third
author proved thatx 2p+2x p+2 is monogenic if and only ifpis not a Wieferich
prime. In this article, we generalize this result tox 2n +bx n +bwith certain
restrictions onb≥2 andn≥3.
1.Introduction
For an integerb≥2 and a primep≥3, we say thatpis ageneralized Wieferich
prime baseb, or more succinctly, abase-bWieferich prime, ifb p−1 ≡1 (modp 2) [2].
Whenb= 2, the primepis also known simply as aWieferich prime. Base-b
Wieferich primes are quite scarce. For example, the only known Wieferich primes
are 1093 and 3511, while there are no known base-47 Wieferich primes [2,7].

monogeniciff(x) is irreducible overQand{1, θ, θ 2, . . . , θN−1 }is a basis for the
ring of integers ofQ(θ), wheref(θ) = 0. It is well known [1] that

Recently, the third author [4] proved that
(1.2)x 2p + 2xp + 2 is not monogenic if and only if 2 p−1 ≡1 (modp 2).

trinomials of the formx 2p +bx p +b, whereb≥3 is an integer andpis an odd

A standard tool used to determine the monogenicity of a monic irreducible polyno-
