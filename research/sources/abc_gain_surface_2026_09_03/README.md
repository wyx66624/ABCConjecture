# Source capsule: canonical gain surface (2026-09-03)

This capsule fixes the primary source used for the canonical gain-surface
checkpoint.

* Karsten Müller and Michael Taktikos, *From ABC to Effective Roth and
  Ridout Constants for Cubic Roots*, arXiv:2601.11376v2 (4 February 2026).
* Official abstract: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11376v2>
* Official PDF: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.11376v2>
* Official experimental HTML: <https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11376v2>

`Mueller_Taktikos_2601.11376v2.txt` is a page-delimited extraction made with
`pypdf`; it contains one NUL emitted by the PDF text layer.
`Mueller_Taktikos_2601.11376v2.normalized.txt` removes exactly that NUL and is
the searchable audit copy.  Neither text file is treated as more authoritative
than the frozen PDF.

The source is used critically.  In particular, the checkpoint distinguishes
the exact factorization of a quality ratio from the stronger claim that fixed
separate upper bounds on its two factors would prove the standard abc
conjecture.
