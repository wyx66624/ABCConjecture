# Scalar norm rigidity on the thrice-punctured line

Let `L/k(t)` be a finite separable extension from a finite cover of

\[
 U=P^1\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}.
\]

If `u in L^x` has divisor supported above the boundary, then the valuation
formula for field norms shows that `N_{L/k(t)}(u)` has divisor supported on the
same three points. Every such rational function is

\[
 c\,t^m(1-t)^n,
 \qquad c\in k^\times,
 \quad m,n\in\mathbb Z.
\]

At `t=a/c`, its specialization is only a fixed constant times a monomial in
`a,b,c`. Thus a scalar norm of a boundary-supported torsion, level or theta
unit creates no new independent abc relation.

This eliminates norm-only scalar amplification. Vector packets, projective
oscillation, discriminants, traces of sums, and constructions with controlled
interior divisors remain active.
