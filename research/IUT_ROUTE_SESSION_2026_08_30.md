## 2. Exact determinant criterion for a reachable hull

Let `K` be a nonarchimedean local field, `O` its valuation ring, `pi` a
uniformizer, `k = O/pi O` its residue field of cardinality `q > 1`, and
`v(pi)=1`. Fix `N >= 1`, put `L=O^N`, and normalize additive Haar measure by
`mu(L)=1`. Let `R` be any subset of `L`; in an intended application it must be
the actual collection of reachable output vectors. Define

```text
delta(R) = min v(det[r1 ... rN]),     ri in R, det[r1 ... rN] != 0,
```

if a nonsingular tuple exists, and `delta(R)=infinity` otherwise. Let `H(R)`
be the topological closure of the `O`-linear span of `R` in `K^N`.

**Theorem 2.1.** One has

```text
mu(H(R)) = q^(-delta(R)),
```

where `q^(-infinity)=0`. If `delta(R)=d<infinity`, there exist `N` members of
`R` whose column matrix `A` satisfies

```text
H(R) = A O^N,       v(det A)=d.
```

In particular, the whole positive-volume hull has a finite witness drawn from
the original reachable set; arbitrary replacement vectors are unnecessary.

**Proof.** Suppose first that a nonsingular tuple exists. The nonnegative
integer valuations of its nonzero determinants have a least element `d`.
Choose a tuple with column matrix `A` attaining `d`. For `r in R`, write
`r=A c`, `c in K^N`. By Cramer's rule, `c_i=det(A_i(r))/det(A)`, where
`A_i(r)` replaces the `i`th column by `r`. Every column of `A_i(r)` belongs to
`R`. If its determinant vanishes then `c_i=0`; otherwise minimality gives
`v(det A_i(r)) >= d`, so `v(c_i)>=0`. Thus `c in O^N` and `R subset A O^N`.
The reverse inclusion of spans follows because the columns of `A` belong to
`R`. The span is exactly `A O^N`. Since `O^N` is compact and `A` continuous,
this span is compact and closed, hence equals `H(R)`.

Integral row and column elimination over the DVR gives
`U A V = diag(pi^a1,...,pi^aN)` with `U,V in GL_N(O)` and `ai>=0`.
For completeness, choose a nonzero entry of minimal valuation, exchange it
into the first position, and use its divisibility of every entry to clear its
row and column by integral elementary operations. Repeat on the remaining
block, absorbing units into the invertible matrices. The resulting exponents
sum to `v(det A)=d`. Consequently `L/A L` has cardinality
`product q^ai=q^d`. Its `q^d` cosets partition `L` and have equal Haar
measure, so `mu(A L)=q^(-d)`.

If every `N`-tuple is singular, the `K`-linear span of `R` is a proper
subspace. Choose a nonzero linear functional annihilating it. After scaling,
its coefficient vector lies in `O^N` and has a unit coefficient. The
resulting map `ell:L -> O` is surjective. Its kernel is closed and contains
`H(R)`. For every integer `m>=1`, the subgroup
`ell^(-1)(pi^m O)` has index `q^m` in `L`, hence measure `q^(-m)`.
Therefore `0 <= mu(H(R)) <= q^(-m)` for every `m`, forcing measure zero.
This proves both cases. QED.

**Corollary 2.2 (residue-field test).** The following are equivalent:

1. `H(R)=L`;
2. `delta(R)=0`;
3. the reductions of `R` span `k^N`;
4. some `N` members of `R` have determinant a unit of `O`.

**Proof.** A determinant is a unit precisely when its reduction is nonzero.
A spanning set of the finite-dimensional vector space `k^N` contains a basis.
Theorem 2.1 identifies the hull of a minimizing tuple. Conversely, if
`H(R)=L`, its measure is one, so Theorem 2.1 gives `delta(R)=0`. QED.

**Corollary 2.3 (one witnessed lower bound).** If a measurable `O`-submodule
`Theta` contains `R` and some reachable tuple has determinant valuation `d`,
then

```text
mu(Theta) >= q^(-d).
```

**Proof.** `Theta` contains the `O`-span `A O^N` of those columns. Its measure
is `q^(-d)` by the elimination argument above; apply measure monotonicity.
No assumption `Theta subset L` is necessary. QED.

The hypothesis that the target contains the linear span is essential. A set
of tensor images is not automatically an `O`-submodule. Passing to an
appropriate hull is legitimate only after its relationship to the actual
theta region has been proved.

## 3. A positive result from independent factor actions

Let `L_j=O^(d_j)`, with `d_j>=1`, and
`T=L_1 tensor_O ... tensor_O L_s`, of rank `N=product d_j`. Assume `s>=1`.
For nonzero `x_j in L_j`, write

```text
x_j=pi^(m_j) u_j,
```

where `m_j>=0` is the minimum valuation of its coordinates and `u_j` is
primitive, meaning at least one coordinate is a unit. Let
`Gamma=product_j GL_(d_j)(O)` act independently on the factors.

**Theorem 3.1.** The `O`-span of the orbit of `x_1 tensor ... tensor x_s` is

```text
pi^(sum_j m_j) T.
```

If `mu_T(T)=1`, its measure and dimension-normalized log measure are

```text
mu_T(orbit span) = q^(-N sum_j m_j),
(1/N) log mu_T(orbit span) = -(sum_j m_j) log q.
```

**Proof.** Elementary integral row operations carry a primitive vector to
the first standard basis vector: exchange a unit coordinate into the first
position, multiply it by its inverse, and clear the remaining coordinates.
Further permutations carry it to any standard basis vector. For every
multi-index `(a_1,...,a_s)`, independently choose `g_j` sending `u_j` to
`e_(j,a_j)`. The orbit therefore contains
`pi^(sum m_j) e_(1,a1) tensor ... tensor e_(s,as)`.
These vectors are a basis of the displayed scaled lattice. Conversely every
`g_j` preserves `L_j`, so every orbit vector belongs to that lattice. Taking
the span proves equality. The quotient of `T` by its scaling has cardinality
`q^(N sum m_j)`, giving the two formulas. QED.

This offers an actual constructive route to the local lower bound: exhibit
the appropriate independent actions on genuine outputs, prove that their
integral content is controlled, and prove that the actual theta hull contains
their span. Independent arbitrary integral automorphisms must not be added
as a hypothesis and then described as already supplied by IUT.

## 4. A strict counterexample for simultaneous tensor actions

The distinction in Theorem 3.1 cannot be removed. Over **any** commutative
ring `O`, identify `O^2 tensor_O O^2` with `Mat_2(O)` in the standard tensor
basis. Put

```text
R_diag = {v tensor v : v in O^2}.
```

**Theorem 4.1.** Its `O`-linear span is exactly

```text
Sym_2(O) = {[[a,b],[b,c]] : a,b,c in O}.
```

**Proof.** Every `v tensor v` has equal off-diagonal entries; this equality
is preserved by addition and scalar multiplication. For the reverse
inclusion, put `e1=(1,0)`, `e2=(0,1)`. The three vectors

```text
e1 tensor e1,
e2 tensor e2,
(e1+e2) tensor (e1+e2) - e1 tensor e1 - e2 tensor e2
```

are respectively `E11`, `E22`, and `E12+E21`. They generate every symmetric
matrix. No division by two is used, so this argument includes residue
characteristic two. QED.

If `O` is nontrivial then `E12` is not in this span: the functional
`M -> M12-M21` vanishes on it but takes value one on `E12`.
For a nonarchimedean local field, `Sym_2(O)` is compact, hence already
closed, and has zero Haar measure in `Mat_2(K)` normalized on `Mat_2(O)`.
Indeed, modulo `pi^m` the condition `M12=M21` leaves precisely `q^(3m)`
possibilities out of `q^(4m)`; the containing congruence locus has measure
`q^(-m)` for every `m`. Thus the measure is zero.

Moreover, `R_diag` is stable under every simultaneous action `g tensor g`,
`g in GL_2(O)`. Hence even stability under the *full* simultaneous integral
linear group does not supply the independent-action conclusion.

This is a counterexample to the explicit abstract implication

```text
all simultaneous pure-tensor outputs are present
  + take their closed O-linear hull
  => obtain the complete tensor lattice.
```

It is not a counterexample to IUT, to all arithmetic Teichmuller constructions,
or to ABC. No identification of the actual outputs with `R_diag` is asserted.

## 5. Source-interface qualification and a remaining numerical target

Part III Proposition 9.7.5.1 does allow collation using all specified
topological isomorphisms. Therefore a blanket statement that its construction
has no automorphism enlargement would be inaccurate. Theorem-Definition
9.8.1.1(2)-(3) describes simultaneous Galois and Frobenius actions; (4)-(6)
takes a convex closure in a product and then a tensor image. Theorem 9.11.1
subsequently needs a tensor lattice. One must determine which isomorphisms
may be chosen independently while respecting the source constraints, and
prove the required target hull contains their integral span. Theorem 4.1
shows why simultaneous symmetry alone cannot settle that question. These
statements refer to the [actual displayed construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.13508v4#S9.SS8.SSS1.E1),
not to an assertion that its full output set is diagonal.

Likewise, the LANA eta equation compares maps from one oriented real line
into a volume quotient. The repository already proves that equality can be
tested at the pilot. The new determinant criterion does not prove that a
suitable IUT integral structure exists. It quantifies one concrete piece of
information such a structure would have to supply in a finite lattice model.

For example, suppose a source-faithful finite packet produces local sets
`R_v subset O_v^(N_v)`, and genuine measurable local target regions
`Theta_v` contain `H(R_v)`, with finite positive Haar measures. Let positive
weights `lambda_v` define the two packet log-volumes on these same local
carriers and with the same normalizations by

```text
V_H = sum_v (lambda_v/N_v) log mu_v(H(R_v))
    = -sum_v (lambda_v/N_v) delta(R_v) log(q_v),
V_Theta = sum_v (lambda_v/N_v) log mu_v(Theta_v).
```

Suppose every `delta(R_v)` is finite. If the native q-pilot log-volume is
`V_q`, a **sufficient** numerical comparison is

```text
sum_v (lambda_v/N_v) delta(R_v) log(q_v) <= -V_q.
```

It implies `V_q <= V_H <= V_Theta` by Theorem 2.1 and local measure
monotonicity, followed by monotonicity of the logarithm and the positive
weighted sum. For a non-product global region with a different volume
functional, this step must instead be supplied as a separate proved
monotonicity interface; product containment alone does not establish it.
Thus an alternative proof of the numerical corollary can target a one-sided
determinant bound; exact equality of eta maps is stronger than this numerical
sufficient condition. This observation neither constructs the required
packet nor supplies its measure normalization.

Before using this target for ABC, all of the following remain to be proved:

1. The vectors and hulls are generated by the authentic cross-label operations,
   with admissibility and independence proved, not stipulated.
2. The coefficient lattices, Haar measures, root normalization and arithmetic
   residue-degree weights agree with the intended IUT objects.
3. The local determinant defects obey the displayed global bound; archimedean
   contributions and infinite-packet convergence are handled separately.
4. A source-derived IUT IV estimate for the same selected q-divisor has the
   quantifier order `for every epsilon>0, there is a source selection and
   constants uniform for all ABC inputs`.

No item in this list is introduced as an axiom. The new counterexample rejects
only the false simultaneous-action substitute. The broader routes remain
open research routes.
