# Next global research targets

## Priority 1: Legendre parabolic specialization

Close the remaining archimedean/maximal-slope estimate after the good finite
places have been removed. Compute the exact theta/determinant metric at the
three cusps and prove the level-prime error is `O(ell log ell)` before
normalization.

## Priority 2: varying diagonal cubic

Use the cube-core reduction to study

`A x^3 + B y^3 = C z^3`

with cube-free coefficients and one polynomially large cubic coordinate.
Separate local solubility, 3-Selmer rank, and integral-height estimates; seek a
uniform theorem depending on the radical rather than coefficient magnitude.

## Priority 3: nontrivial exceptional amplification

The exact Belyi and rational-isogeny variants are eliminated. Test varying
maps, Galois-orbit norms, and polynomial identities against the precise
`beta > gamma + kappa*alpha` criterion.

## Priority 4: IUT/ATS source

Attempt the normed, weight-preserving source comparison directly. Every local
power scaling, tensor cross norm, Haar Jacobian, procession weight, and
Ind1--Ind3 inclusion must be explicit.

## Disproof track

Search for a fixed `epsilon_0>0` and an infinite primitive family with

`c / rad(abc)^(1+epsilon_0) -> infinity`.

Finite high-quality examples or failure of a proposed proof mechanism are not
a disproof.
