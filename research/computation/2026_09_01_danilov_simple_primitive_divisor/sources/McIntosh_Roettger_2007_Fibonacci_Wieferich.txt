prime p is regular if and only if p does not divide the numerators of the Bernoulli
numbers B2,B 4,...,B p−3. For all such primes the algebraic proof of Fermat’s Last

The Wolstenholme primes are those irregular primes wherep divides the numer-

387] in his search up to 2×108. Using (iii) we extended the search to 109 and found
no new Wolstenholme primes, and thus no new primes p divide the numerator of
