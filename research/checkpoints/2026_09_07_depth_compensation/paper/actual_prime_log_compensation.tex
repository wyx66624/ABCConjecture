\section{Actual prime logarithms and a finite compensation theorem in Lean}
\label{apl-section}

The earlier integer-weight ledger has a direct arithmetic realization.
This section uses the prime factorization of positive natural numbers
and real logarithms. Its finite height hypotheses are discharged from
integer input and output bounds. It supplies no asymptotic membership
theorem for an arbitrary orbit.

For a natural cutoff $Y$ and a positive integer $N$, define
\begin{align*}
 S_Y(N)&=\sum_{\substack{p\mid N\\p>Y}}v_p(N)\log p,&
 R_Y(N)&=\sum_{\substack{p\mid N\\p>Y}}\log p,\\
 L_Y(N)&=\sum_{\substack{p\mid N\\p\le Y}}v_p(N)\log p,&
 E_{h,Y}(N)&=\sum_{\substack{p\mid N\\p>Y}}(v_p(N)-h)_+\log p.
\end{align*}
Here $p$ always denotes a rational prime, $h$ is a natural number,
and the positive part agrees with natural-number subtraction. Write
$\mathcal S_Y(N)=S_Y(N)-3R_Y(N)$ for the signed logarithmic cost.
It is the logarithm of the corresponding multiplicative cost in the
preceding sections, not that multiplicative cost itself.

\begin{lemma}[The actual factorization ledger]\label{apl-ledger}
For positive integers $U,V,N$,
\begin{gather*}
 S_Y(N),L_Y(N)\ge0,\qquad S_Y(N)+L_Y(N)=\log N,\\
 S_Y(N)\le hR_Y(N)+E_{h,Y}(N),\\
 S_Y(UV)=S_Y(U)+S_Y(V),\qquad
 L_Y(UV)=L_Y(U)+L_Y(V),\\
 \mathcal S_Y(UV)\le\mathcal S_Y(V)+\log U.
\end{gather*}
If $U,V$ are coprime, then $R_Y(UV)=R_Y(U)+R_Y(V)$
and their signed costs are additive. If all prime depths of $N$
above $Y$ are at most $h$, then $E_{h,Y}(N)=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The prime factorization of $N$ and the logarithm of a finite product
give the partition identity. Every prime logarithm is nonnegative.
At a supported prime of depth $e$, the inequality
$e\le h+(e-h)_+$ gives the depth-cap estimate after multiplication
by $\log p$ and summation. Valuations add under multiplication,
which proves additivity of both full masses. Every prime divisor
of $V$ divides $UV$, so $R_Y(UV)\ge R_Y(V)$. Therefore
\[
 \mathcal S_Y(UV)\le S_Y(U)+\mathcal S_Y(V)
                       \le\log U+\mathcal S_Y(V).
\]
For coprime factors their prime supports are disjoint; radical mass,
and hence signed cost, then adds exactly. Under the stated cap every
summand in the excess is zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite compensation from integer arm data]
\label{apl-finite-compensation}
Let $a,b,d,A,B,C,H,H_1$ be positive integers. Suppose $A,B,C$
are pairwise coprime and
\[
 aA\le H,\quad bB\le H,\quad dC\le H,
 \qquad a,b\le H_1.
\]
Put $U=abd$, $T=(aA)(bB)(dC)$ and
$\Delta=3\log H-\log T$. Then
\begin{align}
 \mathcal S_Y(T)\le{}&\Delta+\log H_1+\log U+\tfrac12 L_Y(T)
 \label{apl-finite-bound}\\
 &+\tfrac32\bigl(E_{2,Y}(A)+E_{2,Y}(B)\bigr)-3R_Y(C).\notag
\end{align}
No coprimality between $U$ and $ABC$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Abbreviate the masses of $A,B,C$ by $S_A,S_B,S_C$, and similarly
for $R,L,E$. The depth-two estimates give
$R_A\ge(S_A-E_A)/2$ and $R_B\ge(S_B-E_B)/2$.
Additivity of signed cost on the three coprime quotients yields
\begin{equation}\label{apl-two-arm-step}
 \mathcal S_Y(ABC)\le S_C-\tfrac12(S_A+S_B)
                         +\tfrac32(E_A+E_B)-3R_C.
\end{equation}
Since the other two output logarithms are at most $\log H$,
\[
 \log(aA)\ge\log T-2\log H=\log H-\Delta.
\]
Subtracting $\log a\le\log H_1$ and using the partition identity gives
$S_A\ge\log H-\Delta-\log H_1-L_A$; the same argument applies
to $B$. Also $C\le dC\le H$, so $S_C\le\log H$.
Substitution in \eqref{apl-two-arm-step} gives
\[
 \mathcal S_Y(ABC)\le\Delta+\log H_1+\tfrac12(L_A+L_B)
                              +\tfrac32(E_A+E_B)-3R_C.
\]
The identity $T=UABC$ and nonnegativity and additivity of $L_Y$
imply $L_A+L_B\le L_Y(T)$. Apply the overlap estimate of
Lemma~\ref{apl-ledger} to adjoin $U$, proving the theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A squarefree-arm finite interface]\label{apl-squarefree}
Suppose $A,B,C$ are positive pairwise coprime integers, and every
prime depth of $A$ above $Y$ is at most one. For real $t,\delta,l$,
assume $S_Y(A)\ge t-\delta-l$ and $S_Y(B),S_Y(C)\le t$.
Then
\[
 \mathcal S_Y(ABC)\le2\delta+2l-3\bigl(R_Y(B)+R_Y(C)\bigr).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The depth-one estimate has zero excess, so $R_Y(A)\ge S_Y(A)$.
Additivity gives
$\mathcal S_Y(ABC)\le-2S_Y(A)+S_Y(B)+S_Y(C)-3R_Y(B)-3R_Y(C)$.
Insert the assumed mass bounds.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Formal scope.}
The modules \texttt{ActualPrimeLogCompensation} and
\texttt{ActualPrimeLogHeight} contain respectively 17 and 7 proved
declarations, using \texttt{Nat.factorization}, \texttt{Nat.primeFactors}
and \texttt{Real.log}. They include the finite inequalities above,
the actual mass partition, product and overlap laws, logarithmic
monotonicity, and the derivation of the height hypotheses from the
displayed natural-number data. The second module's final theorem is
\texttt{finite\_actual\_arm\_compensation}. The natural cutoff is part
of the formal statement; no real-cutoff asymptotic limit is inferred
from it without an additional argument.

Both modules were freshly compiled with Lean 4.32.0 and warnings as
errors against the pinned Mathlib revision
\texttt{81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997}.
The pinned dependency cache was reused; this is not a fresh build of
all Mathlib. Every new declaration has an explicit axiom query, with
only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}
in the union. No ordinary number-theoretic claim is added as an axiom.
The sources and their mathematical scope were independently reviewed.

In the orbit application, $a,b,d$ and $A,B,C$ are the absolute input
arms and actual quotients of Section~\ref{sac-section}. The ordinary
primitivity and quotient theorems supply their reconstruction and
pairwise coprimality. Those orbit facts, the logarithmic-form input,
and arbitrary-root membership in a sufficient signed-compensation
class are still outside these two formal modules. The finite
factorization bridge therefore narrows the formal gap without
closing the remaining number-theoretic one.
