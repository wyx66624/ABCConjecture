\section{The tenth continuation: distinct remaining proof obligations}
\label{depth-status-section}

The present continuation gives reviewed results on three different
obstructions. The reciprocal-depth theorem permits unequal caps and
retains radical credit from every unrestricted arm. Its five maximal
cap patterns are sufficient but do not exhaust actual roots: the
three-arm private-depth family disproves that precise automatic
membership assertion. Its selected deep primes have a vanishing
normalized height cost, so the same family does not disprove the net
compensation criterion. Controlling the full excess-minus-credit
quantity at each actual moving root remains the nearest analytic
gate; an average full-mass result does not supply that control.

The independent block theorem locates almost all full multiplicity
mass above a growing prime threshold for most roots of a specified
block. Its elementary replacement removes the archimedean and
fixed-prime logarithmic-form inputs on those blocks. It retains the
established Brun--Titchmarsh estimate and an exceptional set. This
chain remains useful for identifying where a missing signed estimate
must act; it proves neither the sign of the remaining cost nor a
pointwise result on the exceptional roots.

The geometric route retains both the generic mixed Kummer construction
and the stronger construction using the two actual Eisenstein norms.
The latter has degree $hg$, genus $1+2hg-g-2h$, and a sparse coefficient
budget independent of the exponents. Its smooth rational descent
exhibits the same compatibility through two explicit rational maps.
The expanded rational coefficient budget is different. Neither a
small coefficient height nor a large genus bounds the actual point
height uniformly in this family. A suitable height upper bound, or
an actual sequence violating it while meeting the norm requirements,
remains to be established.

The new formal inventory has 41 proved declarations: 17 in the
integer reciprocal-depth module and 24 in the actual prime-logarithm
modules. The first fresh scoped build also recompiled 71 dependency
declarations. The prime-logarithm build reused pinned Mathlib compiled
dependencies. The geometric constructions, infinite private-depth
family and analytic block estimates remain ordinary reviewed proofs,
not Lean theorems. The two exact finite replays provide separately
identified arithmetic evidence. None of these distinctions is
removed by publishing the files together.

Thus no complete proof or disproof of $abc$ is claimed. The earlier
unrefuted routes remain available. Only the particular automatic-cap
claim has been excluded by the actual counterfamily; difficulty,
lack of a ready formal library, and disagreement with another route
are not reasons to discard a direction.
