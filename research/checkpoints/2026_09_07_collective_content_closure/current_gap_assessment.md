# Current distance to a complete ABC proof

Snapshot for the user's status question, 2026-09-07. This is a research
assessment, not a new theorem or a sealed manuscript release. The live local
main HEAD at inspection was 66d4323bdc90f1283aa0da24900058bd6280f5c1.
The seventeenth/eighteenth-round additions discussed here are still local.
The standard ABC conjecture remains unproved and undisproved in this project.

## What completion requires

For every epsilon > 0, prove a constant C_epsilon independent of the positive
primitive triple a+b=c such that

    log(c) - (1+epsilon) log(rad(abc)) <= C_epsilon.

A disproof instead needs one fixed positive epsilon and an unbounded
sequence of primitive triples for which the left side is unbounded above.
Large absolute defect, a finite high-quality example, or a counterexample to
an auxiliary sufficient condition does not meet this requirement.

The remaining work cannot be assigned a meaningful completion percentage.
An implication from a new uniform estimate to ABC can be short while the
estimate itself retains the original mathematical difficulty. Existing Lean
declaration counts measure verified scope, not progress toward the missing
global inequality.

## Principal arithmetic route

The current actual power block has prime n>324, B=n^4, w_k=3k+zeta and a
multiplicatively independent subset of the block. There are full bounds at
each individual large prime, finite-window sums, actual signed-product
rigidity, and combined-precision bounds for common-hit rectangles. These do
not bound the union of different-prime owners: different primes can select
different roots. Joint precision currently gives a product target of size
n^s for s primes, without the missing saving for singleton owners.

The central open statement is a suitable uniform bound for the complete
signed boundary ledger

    J(T) = sum_(p|T) (v_p(T)-3) log(p),    T>0,

at the original roots, with the negative depth-one and depth-two terms
retained. An appropriate normalized positive part must tend to zero in the
structured family, with all exceptional roots accounted for. Even the
necessary averaged or sparse-exception successor estimates remain open.

After that structured gate, the proof still needs the complement of the
independent domain, bounded and general exponents, arbitrary input roots,
nonunit residuals, and a reduction covering every primitive ABC triple.
These are separate obligations, not consequences of a bound on the current
special block.

Useful next targets are an actual weighted relation between different-prime
owners, with prime credits counted only once, or an arithmetic transfer
theorem from a controlled new output to the original signed ledger. A
smaller per-prime numerical constant alone does not supply either target.

## Collective products and mixed maps

The reviewed complement theorem proves that the common-product/complement
construction returns precisely the original primitive boundary after gcd
normalization. Its radical and signed ledger are unchanged. The reviewed
primitive-profile theorem proves that the specified full interval product
cannot produce the required small-residual norm compression: on a fixed
macroscopic interval, every extraction satisfies

    max(1,log(V))/g >= (delta log(7)/6) B

for sufficiently large B. These are rigorous barriers for specific
constructions, not exclusions of the parent arithmetic program.

The new affine-selector ordinary argument preserves an arbitrary fixed
finite collection of exact owner depths, including their negative credits.
For fixed admissible data, a positive-density family of new primitive
outputs has squarefree support outside a fixed set, and hence

    J(T_new) = -2 log(T_new) + O_fixed_data(1).

The output is generally no longer an original n-th-power point. Its constants
and threshold are not uniform for growing prescribed packets. Two concrete
open targets are a controlled intersection with the power/small-residual
family, and a uniform moving-packet threshold together with an actual
signed-ledger transfer. Neither target has been proved. Increasing the
number of coefficients in a map landing in Z[zeta] does not by itself
increase its two-dimensional output rank; the explicit finite-difference
kernel must be excluded or genuinely richer output data retained.

## Independent geometry

For a particular surviving fixed-exponent, unit-residual branch, ordinary
proofs establish usable elliptic/genus-two quotients, a finite quadratic
Chabauty container, the required five-adic closure, and the correct global
height/model normalization. The new exhaustive away-from-five local-height
value-set argument has now passed both independent ordinary/source reviews.
Its exact target is Omega={-(4/3) log_5(3)} for this fixed curve. The source
hash is 05572c7e4740badfe093aad999f719fcbca892bda8a47252feb6173df6cad22a.
This is not yet a Lean theorem or a complete analytic zero certificate.

The next finite branch milestones are explicit analytic functions on all
twelve five-adic residue disks, certified tail
bounds and all zero multiplicities, a Mordell--Weil sieve, and the inverse
test that both required squares come from the same original source.
No complete rational-point classification has yet been established.

Even such a classification would settle only this branch. Global ABC still
requires moving exponents and residuals, integral/content and ramification
conditions, and complete source coverage. The decisive geometric bridge is
a point-height estimate uniform over the actual varying family and strong
enough to contradict its established height separation. Fixed-curve
finiteness does not supply that uniform statement.

## Actual divisor allocation and FCRT

The exact optimization for actual balanced two-source endpoints is known on
important classes. A certified example with e=64 and f=41 has positive scalar
defect D<1 but divisor-partition penalty Gamma>52D. It refutes the proposed
universal Gamma=0 and Gamma<=D strengthenings. It does not refute a parent
estimate allowing an arbitrary constant C_epsilon, or ABC.

A proposed infinite large-prime-barrier family would separate the old
allocation gate from ABC, but its required prime-value infinitude is
unproved. The next positive target is a new actual admissible certificate
that covers the known large-prime class without charging a prime twice.
If the allocation rules are unchanged, their exact barrier must instead be
controlled by a uniform arithmetic estimate. The medium-largest-prime class
also supplies a concrete next test for real central divisor gaps.

## Other retained routes

* Frey--Szpiro, Arakelov--Vojta, and effective integral points have conditional
  transfers and local invariants. They still need a noncircular estimate of
  sufficient strength with constants uniform in the curve and prime support.
* The IUT route still needs the actual all-place tensor/integral-order
  constructions, Ind1--Ind3 and pointed same-pilot transport, followed by the
  global height comparison. Abstract interfaces do not discharge them.
* Mason/function-field specialization needs uniform control of coefficient
  height and bad specialization primes while preserving integer radicals.
* S-unit, smooth-neighbour, Pell/Lucas and Mersenne methods need varying-support
  or exact-order/depth distribution estimates, beyond fixed data or finite
  computations.
* Canonical defect flags, five-term fillings, incidence complexes, and
  ownership catalogues retain explicit uniform cost or sparsity gates. None
  has an unconditional construction satisfying its full ABC-strength gate.

The full FLT conclusion and selected modular ingredients can be reused only
at their actual hypotheses. FLT's equal-exponent equation does not provide
the varying-support Szpiro estimate or convert arbitrary ABC triples into
Fermat triples. Upstream theorem availability and successful local import
are separate facts; the repository's full upstream import is not yet
verified locally.
