\section{Formal general Lucas identities and actual bad-prime support}
\label{sec:formal-lucas-support}

The module \texttt{GeneralLucasBoundary.lean} contains ten declarations
using the existing actual Eisenstein pair multiplication, norm and
boundary. For arbitrary $w=a+b\zeta$ it defines
\[
 \tau(w)=2a^3+3a^2b-3ab^2-2b^3,\qquad R(w)=N(w)^3.
\]
The kernel checks the cubic trace and discriminant identities and the
general shifted recurrence
\[
 \tau(w)^2+27P(w)^2=4N(w)^3,\qquad
 P(w^2z)+R(w)P(z)=\tau(w)P(wz).
\]
These are actual polynomial identities for all integer pairs $w,z$,
with no primitivity or nonzero assumption. Ordinarily, the recurrence
follows by multiplying the characteristic equation of $w^3$ by $z^3$
and taking its difference with its conjugate. The formal proof directly
checks the integer polynomial identity.

For arbitrary integer parameters $t,r$, put $U_0=0$, $U_1=1$ and
$U_{n+2}=tU_{n+1}-rU_n$. A general formal recurrence theorem proves
\[
 f(n+1)=U_{n+1}f(1)-rU_nf(0)
\]
for every integer sequence with that recurrence. Applied to the actual
Eisenstein powers, it gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 P(w^{n+1}z)&=U_{n+1}P(wz)-N(w)^3U_nP(z),\\
 P(w^n)&=P(w)U_n,\qquad P(w)\mid P(w^n),
 \end{split}
 \quad (t,r)=(\tau(w),N(w)^3).
\]
The zero exponent and zero-boundary cases are included. Norm
multiplicativity is also lifted to every actual power. This is a general
actual Lucas bridge; the whole cyclotomic factorization and its rank
valuation classification are not silently inserted into its signature.

The separate module \texttt{QuarticSupportArithmetic.lean} contains
eleven declarations. It imports the actual second norm
$F(a,b)=N(ab,N(a,b))$ from the preceding continuation, proves polynomial
congruence transfer for arbitrary integer coordinates, and checks
\[
 \gcd(a,b)=1\ \Longrightarrow\ F(a,b)\equiv1\pmod3,
\]
\[
 \gcd(a,b)=1,\quad 13\mid F(a,b)
 \ \Longrightarrow\quad 169\nmid F(a,b).
\]
The proof uses finite residue tables reduced in the kernel, transfers
them to all coprime integers, and applies the exact identity
\[
 F(b+13t,b)=13b^4+
 169\bigl(2b^3t+20b^2t^2+91bt^3+169t^4\bigr).
\]
It then proves that, whenever $g\ge2$ and
$F(a,b)=VQ^g$ is an actual primitive integer profile, one has
$13\nmid Q$. Thus thirteen cannot be absorbed into a high-exponent
extraction root. No positivity restriction is needed in these formal
integer conclusions.

A fresh standalone Lake build with Lean~4.32.0 and warnings treated as
errors accepted all twenty-one new declarations and recompiled forty-four
unchanged dependency declarations. Each declaration has a printed-axiom
inventory; only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound} occur. The exact source hashes and independent
signature review are recorded in the checkpoint. This verifies neither
the complete cyclotomic and geometric sections nor standard ABC, and is
not a full repository build.
