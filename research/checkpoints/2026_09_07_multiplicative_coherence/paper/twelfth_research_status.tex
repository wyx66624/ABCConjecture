\section{The twelfth continuation: coherence, private support and quotient curves}
\label{coherence-status-section}

The analytic route now uses multiplication of actual root ratios.
At sufficiently deep prime powers, the height of an integer determinant
is smaller than the modulus. Congruent pair products then have exactly
equal rational phase, and their multiplicity is bounded by actual
divisors of a quadratic integer. This gives a simultaneous cap-three
window beyond the root block on a set of relative size tending to one.
It controls the signed contribution of the window without asserting
that its full prime mass is small.

A distinct refinement uses primes of the root norms. At least half of
the block asymptotically has a simple norm prime larger than $12B$;
that prime occurs in no other root norm in the block. The corresponding
oriented valuations prove multiplicative independence on this actual
subset. Higher ordered moments then have only permutation fibers.
For every fixed moment order this controls positive excess in a still
larger bounded interval, on a set of relative size at least $1/2-o(1)$.
The constants depend on the fixed moment order. The unbounded farther
signed contribution and the complementary roots remain open.

The exact phase analysis also records the limits of stronger counting
claims. Divisors obey a precise congruence modulo three times the
reduced denominator. Large denominators give an injective unordered
subclass; small denominators admit actual repeated fibers, including
three distinct unordered pairs in one fiber. These are counterexamples
to the stated uniform fiber bounds, not counterexamples to deep-prime
membership or $abc$. Multiple prime marks retain the product target
size and an explicit low-denominator remainder.

The geometric branch remains separate. The positive rational locus
on the odd biquadratic cover has an exact integral inverse. Every
positive rational point on the first Eisenstein curve has a unique
Gaussian lift. Thus that additional equation does not exclude such
points. Nevertheless the resulting unramified cover exhibits a divisor
class of exact order $g$ on a varying Jacobian. The first curve is also
a rational biquadratic cover with three hyperelliptic quotients of
genus $g-1$. Their Jacobians are isogenous in product to its Jacobian,
and the odd torsion class survives in the product. At $g=3$ these are
three explicit genus-two curves. Their full rational-point sets and
ranks have not been computed here. Uniform exponent and height control,
ramified first norms and moving residual coefficients remain live targets.

The formal contribution is the 21-declaration actual phase and finite
counting core, with 29 freshly checked unchanged dependency declarations.
The finite-group size and modular divisibility are explicit inputs.
The asymptotic density and sieve estimates, ideal-factorization argument
and geometric constructions have ordinary proofs with independent
internal review; they have not all been formalized. Finite replays
check only their stated bounded domains. None of these results supplies
a complete proof or disproof of $abc$, and no unrefuted parent route
is removed because its remaining tools or bounds are not yet available.
