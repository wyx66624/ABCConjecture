# Actual cyclotomic rank packets and their totient budget

Date: 2026-09-07. Fifth-round ordinary proof, independently reviewed.
The fourth-round signed-entropy files are ready for integration and remain frozen.

## 1. Actual homogeneous orbit and integer cyclotomic factors

Work in O=Z[zeta], zeta^2-zeta+1=0. Let w be a primitive unramified
Eisenstein integer of norm Q>=7; thus its two integer coordinates are
coprime and 3 does not divide Q. Write

    P(a+b*zeta)=ab(a+b),    eta=w^3,    eta_bar=bar(w)^3,
    T_n=|P(w^n)|,          n>=1.

The powers w^n are primitive. All their boundary values are nonzero.
These facts follow equally from oriented factorization: at any split
prime only one of the conjugate factors can occur, no inert rational
prime divides a primitive element, and the ramified prime is excluded.

**CP1 (exact actual cyclotomic factorization).** Let Phi_d(X,Y) be the
homogeneous d-th cyclotomic polynomial. Define

    C_1=P(w),       C_d=Phi_d(eta,eta_bar)       (d>=2).

Every C_d is a nonzero rational integer, and

    P(w^n)=prod_{d|n} C_d.                                      (CP1a)

Moreover, for d>=2,

    log|C_d| <= phi(d)(1.5 log Q+log 2),                         (CP1b)

while log|C_1|<=1.5 log Q. In particular, for every d>=1,

    log|C_d|<=2 phi(d)log Q.                                    (CP1c)

Proof. The ideals generated by w and bar(w) are coprime. Indeed a
common prime ideal away from three would divide their difference
b*sqrt(-3), hence the integer b and then the integer a. Its rational
prime would divide both coordinates. A common prime above three is
excluded by 3 not dividing Q. If eta/eta_bar were a root of unity, its
principal ideal would be the unit ideal, so (w)^3=(bar(w))^3. Unique
ideal factorization and coprimality would force both ideals to be units,
contradicting Q>=7. Thus eta/eta_bar is not a root of unity.

For d>=2, Phi_d(X,Y) is symmetric in X,Y by reciprocity. This includes
d=2, when it is X+Y and phi(2)=1. Its value at eta,eta_bar is an
algebraic integer fixed by conjugation in Q(sqrt(-3)), and so lies in Z.
It is nonzero by the preceding non-root-of-unity argument.

Direct expansion gives

    (a+b*zeta)^3-(a+b*bar(zeta))^3
       =3*sqrt(-3)*ab(a+b).

Apply the homogeneous cyclotomic identity to eta^n-eta_bar^n, and
cancel its first factor eta-eta_bar=3*sqrt(-3)*P(w). This proves
(CP1a), including its signs. Each complex linear factor of Phi_d has
absolute value at most 2 Q^(3/2), proving (CP1b). The same difference
identity gives |P(w)|<=2 Q^(3/2)/(3sqrt(3))<=Q^(3/2).
Finally log 2 <=(log Q)/2 for Q>=7, giving (CP1c).

This is a factorization of the actual integer boundary. It does not
replace the orbit by an arbitrary collection of independent factors.

## 2. Exact rank valuations of each integer factor

For every prime p>3 with p not dividing Q, let

    d_p=ord((w/bar(w))^3 mod p),      s_p=v_p(T_{d_p}).

The order is in the norm-one group of O/pO. Its group order is p-chi_p,
where chi_p is 1 in the split case and -1 in the inert case. Thus
d_p divides p-chi_p, in particular p does not divide d_p. The earlier
homogeneous LTE theorem gives the exact boundary law

    v_p(T_n)=0                         if d_p does not divide n,
    v_p(T_n)=s_p+v_p(n)                 if d_p divides n.           (CP2a)

**CP2 (first occurrence and the only later cyclotomic occurrences).**
For every integer n>=1,

    v_p(C_n)=s_p           if n=d_p,
    v_p(C_n)=1             if n=d_p*p^j for some j>=1,
    v_p(C_n)=0             otherwise.                            (CP2b)

Proof. Equation (CP1a) says that v_p(T_n) is the divisor sum of
v_p(C_d). The proposed right side of (CP2b) has divisor sum zero if
d_p does not divide n. Otherwise that sum is s_p plus the number of
j>=1 for which d_p*p^j divides n; since p does not divide d_p, this
number is v_p(n). It therefore has precisely the divisor sum (CP2a).
Uniqueness of divisor inversion (or induction on n) proves (CP2b).

Equivalently, at the first rank d_p the Mobius product for Phi_{d_p}
has only one factor with positive p-adic valuation: the index d_p
itself. The later repeats belong only to the p-power tower of that
rank, and have depth one in their individual cyclotomic factors.

## 3. A totient bound for the actual first-depth packet

For a real cutoff Y>=5, define finite products

    B_d(Y;w)=prod_{p>Y, d_p=d} p^{s_p},
    R_d(Y;w)=prod_{p>Y, d_p=d} p,
    A_d(Y;w)=B_d(Y;w)/R_d(Y;w)^3.

Every prime in these products divides T_d, so the products are finite.
Primes dividing Q never divide T_n: if a primitive pair has a boundary
arm zero modulo such a prime, its norm being zero would force both
coordinates to vanish. Thus no supported prime above the cutoff has
been omitted from the rank notation.

**CP3 (actual packet divisibility and exact signed assembly).** One has

    B_d(Y;w) divides |C_d|,
    log B_d(Y;w)<=2 phi(d)log Q.                                (CP3a)

For every n>=1 the signed boundary is exactly

    log W_Y(T_n)=sum_{d|n}log A_d(Y;w)+ell_{n,Y},
    ell_{n,Y}=sum_{p>Y,p|T_n}v_p(n)log p,    0<=ell_{n,Y}<=log n.  (CP3b)

Proof. The primes of rank d have valuation s_p in C_d by (CP2b), so
their product with those powers divides that integer. Apply (CP1c).
For every supported prime, (CP2a) gives
v_p(T_n)-3=(s_p-3)+v_p(n). Partition the first terms by their
unique rank and sum. The remaining product is a divisor of n.

In contrast to a bound by log T_d, (CP3a) has phi(d), not d.
The exact identity sum_{d|n}phi(d)=n means that the total full packet
budget is at most 2n log Q, with no sigma(n) or divisor-count loss.
This remains linear in height and does not by itself prove the needed
sublinear signed bound.

## 4. A proved sparse-rank subclass

Let E(n,w,Y) consist of the divisors d of n for which the NET signed
packet log A_d(Y;w) is positive. Put

    m_phi(n,w,Y)=(1/n)sum_{d in E(n,w,Y)}phi(d).

**CP4 (quantitative sparse-totient-mass bound).** Let c_n be the largest
side of the primitive positive triple obtained from the three boundary
arms of w^n, and put t_n=log c_n. Then

    log W_Y(T_n)/t_n
       <=4 m_phi(n,w,Y)+2 log n/(n log Q).                       (CP4a)

In particular, along any sequence with n tending to infinity, Q>=7,
and m_phi tending to zero, the positive part of this signed ratio tends
to zero, uniformly in the moving roots and cutoffs. If every positive
signed packet has rank at most an integer D>=0, then

    log W_Y(T_n)/t_n
       <=2D(D+1)/n+2 log n/(n log Q).                            (CP4b)

Thus D=o(sqrt(n)) is a sufficient explicit subclass condition. More
generally, at most K exceptional ranks, all at most D, suffice whenever
KD=o(n). The statement is a theorem for the specified class; it does
not assert that arbitrary small-lambda orbits belong to that class.

Proof. Discard only the nonpositive WHOLE packets in (CP3b), and bound
the others by (CP3a). The norm of w^n is Q^n and is at most c_n^2,
so t_n>=(n/2)log Q. This gives (CP4a).
Since phi(d)<=d, the sum over ranks at most D is at most D(D+1)/2;
alternatively it is at most KD for K ranks at most D. These give the
assertions. The bound is nonnegative and tends to zero, so it implies
vanishing positive part, not two-sided convergence of the signed cost.

For the small-lambda homogeneous class the previously proved analytic
reduction uses lambda=1/n and cutoff Y=n^(1/6). At this cutoff, any
sequence in the CP4 subclass has the required one-sided signed-tail
bound, which combines with the established low-prime and archimedean
estimates. Nothing here asserts control of the bounded-exponent class
with arbitrarily moving roots, or global membership in CP4.

The exceptional set E above is not the larger set E_plus of ranks
having at least one first-depth prime s_p>=4. Such a prime may be
outweighed by depth-one or depth-two primes in the same rank packet.
E_plus sparsity would suffice but is stronger. In particular a single
rank near n can have totient mass comparable to n, so cardinality of
the rank set alone cannot replace m_phi. The independent adversarial
route is constructing actual high-rank depth-four families to test
that stronger shortcut; it does not thereby refute E's net signed gate.

## 5. A rankwise saving condition without divisor-count inflation

**CP5 (totient-normalized packet saving).** Consider any sequence of
actual data (n,w,Y), n tending to infinity, Q>=7. Suppose a function
epsilon(d)>=0, tending to zero as d tends to infinity, satisfies

    log A_d(Y;w)<=epsilon(d) phi(d)log Q                       (CP5a)

at every divisor d of every actual n in the sequence. Then

    limsup log W_Y(T_n)/t_n<=0.                                 (CP5b)

Proof. Given delta>0 choose D so epsilon(d)<=delta for d>D. Bound the
finitely many smaller ranks by (CP3a), and use sum_{d|n}phi(d)=n on
the others. Then

    log W_Y(T_n)<=2log Q sum_{d<=D}phi(d)+delta n log Q+log n.

After division by t_n>=(n/2)log Q, the limsup is at most 2delta.
Let delta decrease to zero. All constants for the small ranks are
uniform in the moving root Q and the actual cutoff Y.

The old homogeneous polynomial packet hypothesis
A_d(Y;w)<=(Qd)^kappa implies (CP5a). Indeed
phi(d)>=sqrt(d/2): in phi(d)^2/d each odd prime-power factor is at
least one, and the sole possible factor below one is 1/2 from 2^1.
Consequently
kappa(1+log d/log 7)/phi(d) tends to zero. The new sufficient condition
allows much larger packet upper budgets, for example log Q*sqrt(phi(d));
this is a comparison of hypotheses, not an exhibited arithmetic family
violating the old hypothesis.

For a prime exponent n, the exact formula has only ranks one and n.
The rank-one packet has O(log Q) cost and the lifting term has O(log n)
cost. Thus the remaining upper-bound issue is precisely the primitive
top-layer signed packet log A_n(Y;w) on the scale n log Q. Neither the
cyclotomic full-mass bound nor the entropy reformulation proves a
radical saving on that layer.

## 6. Dependencies and review scope

CP1 uses elementary Eisenstein ideals, homogeneous cyclotomic
factorization and the complex absolute value. CP2 uses the already
independently established homogeneous rank/LTE law and exact divisor
inversion. CP3--CP5 use these ordinary results and elementary totient
identities. No distributional assumption, squarefree-value heuristic,
or assertion that most actual primes have simple depth enters them.

The parent and adversarial agents independently read the complete CP1--CP5
proof and approved the identities, bounds, quantifiers and scope. There is no
new Lean claim in this note and no claim of resolving ABC. The new
unconditional arithmetic progress is the actual integer factorization
and its totient packet budget; the saving and membership conditions
are explicitly separate remaining hypotheses.
