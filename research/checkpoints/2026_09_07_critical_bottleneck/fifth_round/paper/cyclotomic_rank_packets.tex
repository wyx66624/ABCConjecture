\section{Actual cyclotomic rank packets and the totient budget}
\label{cp-section}
The support-conditioned entropy criterion retains signed credit but
does not itself estimate the arithmetic allowance. We now use an
integer factorization of the actual homogeneous orbit. Its first-depth
packets have a $\varphi(d)$ budget, whose sum over the divisors of the
exponent is exactly the exponent. This proves a sparse-rank subclass
while retaining the primitive top-layer saving as an open step.

Let $\mathcal O=\mathbb Z[\zeta]$, $\zeta^2-\zeta+1=0$, and let $w$
be primitive and unramified, with $Q=N(w)\ge7$ and $3\nmid Q$.
Put $\eta=w^3$, $\bar\eta=\bar w^3$, and, for $n\ge1$, let
$T_n=|P(w^n)|$, where $P(a+b\zeta)=ab(a+b)$.
Oriented factorization shows that all $w^n$ remain primitive.

\subsection{An integer factorization with exact rank valuations}
\begin{theorem}[Actual cyclotomic integer factors]\label{cp-integers}
For the homogeneous cyclotomic polynomials, define
\[
 C_1=P(w),\qquad C_d=\Phi_d(\eta,\bar\eta)\quad(d\ge2).
\]
Every $C_d$ is a nonzero rational integer, and
\[
 P(w^n)=\prod_{d\mid n}C_d.
\]
For $d\ge2$,
\[
 \log|C_d|\le\varphi(d)\bigl(\tfrac32\log Q+\log2\bigr),
\]
while $\log|C_1|\le\tfrac32\log Q$. In particular,
$\log|C_d|\le2\varphi(d)\log Q$ for every $d\ge1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The ideals $(w)$ and $(\bar w)$ are coprime. A common prime away from
three would divide $b\sqrt{-3}$ and then both integer coordinates;
a prime above three is excluded by $3\nmid Q$. If
$\eta/\bar\eta$ were a root of unity, unique ideal factorization
would give $(w)^3=(\bar w)^3$ and hence force $w$ to be a unit,
contrary to $Q\ge7$.

For $d\ge2$, reciprocity gives
$\Phi_d(X,Y)=\Phi_d(Y,X)$, including $\Phi_2(X,Y)=X+Y$.
Its value is an algebraic integer fixed by conjugation in
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$, so it lies in $\mathbb Z$; nonvanishing
follows from the non-root-of-unity ratio. Direct expansion gives
\[
 (a+b\zeta)^3-(a+b\bar\zeta)^3
       =3\sqrt{-3}\,ab(a+b).
\]
Apply the homogeneous cyclotomic factorization to
$\eta^n-\bar\eta^n$ and cancel its first factor
$3\sqrt{-3}P(w)$. This proves the exact signed integer product.
Each complex linear factor of $\Phi_d(\eta,\bar\eta)$ has modulus
at most $2Q^{3/2}$. The same cubic identity gives
$|P(w)|\le2Q^{3/2}/(3\sqrt3)\le Q^{3/2}$.
Finally $\log2\le\tfrac12\log Q$ for $Q\ge7$.
\end{proof}

For a prime $p>3$, $p\nmid Q$, let
\[
 d_p=\operatorname{ord}\bigl((w/\bar w)^3\bmod p\bigr),
 \qquad s_p=v_p(T_{d_p}).
\]
The norm-one group has order $p-\chi_p$, where $\chi_p=1$ in the
split case and $-1$ in the inert case. Thus $p\nmid d_p$.
The previously proved homogeneous rank and LTE law is
\[
 v_p(T_n)=\begin{cases}
 0,&d_p\nmid n,\\
 s_p+v_p(n),&d_p\mid n.
 \end{cases}
\]

\begin{theorem}[The only cyclotomic occurrences of a prime]
\label{cp-valuations}
For every $n\ge1$,
\[
 v_p(C_n)=\begin{cases}
 s_p,&n=d_p,\\
 1,&n=d_pp^j\text{ for some }j\ge1,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The integer factorization says that $v_p(T_n)$ is the divisor sum
of $v_p(C_d)$. The proposed right side has divisor sum zero if
$d_p\nmid n$. Otherwise its divisor sum is $s_p$ plus the number
of positive $j$ for which $d_pp^j\mid n$, which is $s_p+v_p(n)$
because $p\nmid d_p$. Uniqueness of divisor inversion proves the
claim. In particular, at the first rank the M\"obius product has
only one term of positive valuation, and all later occurrences lie
on that rank's $p$-power tower with depth one per factor.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Totient-sized packets and exact signed assembly}
For $Y\ge5$ put
\[
 B_d=\prod_{\substack{p>Y\\d_p=d}}p^{s_p},\qquad
 R_d=\prod_{\substack{p>Y\\d_p=d}}p,\qquad A_d=B_d/R_d^3.
\]
These finite products depend on $Y,w$. Every prime in them divides
$T_d$. A prime dividing $Q$ cannot divide a primitive boundary:
one zero boundary arm together with zero norm would force both
coordinates to vanish. Thus no supported prime above the cutoff
is missing from this rank notation.

\begin{theorem}[Actual packet budget and signed assembly]
\label{cp-packets}
For every $d\ge1$,
\[
 B_d\mid |C_d|,\qquad \log B_d\le2\varphi(d)\log Q.
\]
For every $n\ge1$,
\[
 \log W_Y(T_n)=\sum_{d\mid n}\log A_d+\ell_{n,Y},
 \qquad
 \ell_{n,Y}=\sum_{\substack{p>Y\\p\mid T_n}}v_p(n)\log p,
 \quad 0\le\ell_{n,Y}\le\log n.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The primes of rank $d$ occur to exact depth $s_p$ in $C_d$, so their
full product divides that integer. Apply Theorem~\ref{cp-integers}.
For every supported prime the rank law gives
$v_p(T_n)-3=(s_p-3)+v_p(n)$. Partition the first terms by their
unique rank. The product represented by the remaining sum divides $n$.
\end{proof}

The identity $\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(d)=n$ bounds the total full
packet mass by $2n\log Q$. There is no $\sigma(n)$ or divisor-count
loss. This is still a linear budget and supplies no sublinear
radical saving by itself.

Let $E(n,w,Y)$ be the divisors $d\mid n$ for which the \emph{net}
signed packet $\log A_d$ is positive, and write
\[
 m_\varphi(n,w,Y)=\frac1n\sum_{d\in E(n,w,Y)}\varphi(d).
\]
\begin{theorem}[A quantitative sparse-rank subclass]\label{cp-sparse}
Let $c_n$ be the largest side of the primitive positive triple from
the three boundary arms of $w^n$, and $t_n=\log c_n$. Then
\[
 \frac{\log W_Y(T_n)}{t_n}
 \le4m_\varphi(n,w,Y)+\frac{2\log n}{n\log Q}.
\]
If every positive net packet has rank at most an integer $D\ge0$, the right side
may instead be bounded by
\[
 \frac{2D(D+1)}n+\frac{2\log n}{n\log Q}.
\]
Thus $m_\varphi\to0$, or the sufficient condition $D=o(\sqrt n)$,
gives a vanishing positive part of the signed ratio as $n\to\infty$,
uniformly in moving $Q\ge7$ and the cutoff. At most $K$ exceptional
ranks, all at most $D$, also suffice when $KD=o(n)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Discard only the nonpositive whole rank packets and apply their
full-mass bounds. The norm satisfies $Q^n\le c_n^2$, so
$t_n\ge(n/2)\log Q$. This proves the first inequality.
Use $\varphi(d)\le d$ and
$\sum_{d\le D}d=D(D+1)/2$, or bound the sum for $K$ ranks by $KD$.
The resulting nonnegative upper bounds give the positive-part
conclusion, not two-sided convergence of a signed quantity.
\end{proof}

At the established small-$\lambda$ homogeneous cutoff
$Y=n^{1/6}$, $\lambda=1/n$, this supplies the required one-sided
signed tail on the stated subclass. It combines with the earlier
low-prime and archimedean estimates along sequences with $n\to\infty$.
It does not control bounded exponents with arbitrarily moving roots,
and membership of a general actual orbit in this subclass is unproved.

\subsection{A saving condition with no divisor-count inflation}
\begin{theorem}[Totient-normalized rank saving]\label{cp-saving}
Consider any sequence of actual data $(n,w,Y)$ with $n\to\infty$ and
$Q\ge7$. Suppose a function $\epsilon(d)\ge0$, tending to zero,
satisfies
\[
 \log A_d(Y;w)\le\epsilon(d)\varphi(d)\log Q
\]
at every divisor $d$ of each actual $n$ in the sequence. Then
$\limsup\log W_Y(T_n)/t_n\le0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Given $\delta>0$, choose $D$ so that $\epsilon(d)\le\delta$ for
$d>D$. Bound the finitely many smaller ranks by the unconditional
packet bound. The exact totient sum gives
\[
 \log W_Y(T_n)\le2\log Q\sum_{d\le D}\varphi(d)
                     +\delta n\log Q+\log n.
\]
Divide by $t_n\ge(n/2)\log Q$, first let $n\to\infty$ and then
$\delta\downarrow0$. The small-rank bound is uniform in $Q$ and $Y$.
\end{proof}
The older hypothesis $A_d\le(Qd)^\kappa$ implies this saving condition.
Indeed $\varphi(d)\ge\sqrt{d/2}$: in $\varphi(d)^2/d$, all odd
prime-power factors are at least one, while the only factor below one
is $1/2$ from $2^1$. Thus
$\kappa(1+\log d/\log7)/\varphi(d)\to0$.
The new hypothesis permits larger upper budgets, such as
$\log Q\sqrt{\varphi(d)}$. This compares hypotheses; it does not
exhibit an arithmetic family violating the earlier hypothesis.

It is essential to distinguish $E$ from the larger set of ranks
containing one prime with $s_p\ge4$. Other primes at the same rank
may cancel that prime's positive contribution. Also one rank near
$n$ can carry totient mass comparable to $n$; rank cardinality alone
is insufficient. For prime $n$, the factorization has only ranks one
and $n$. The first rank costs $O(\log Q)$ and the lifting term costs
$O(\log n)$. The unproved issue is precisely the net primitive
top-layer packet on the scale $n\log Q$.

These ordinary statements use actual Eisenstein ideals, cyclotomic
factorization, the previously established homogeneous LTE law, and
elementary divisor identities. No assertion that actual large primes
usually have simple depth enters them. Neither these packet budgets
nor their finite arithmetic checks prove the unrestricted signed-tail
gate or ABC, and no new Lean coverage is asserted for this section.
