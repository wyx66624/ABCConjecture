\section{Signed depth entropy with the actual support retained}
\label{se-section}
The positive-excess observable of Section~\ref{om-section} discards the
negative contributions of primes of depths one and two. We now retain
these contributions and condition on the actual supported primes. An
elementary height count bounds the remaining conditional Shannon entropy.
The result is an exact signed allowance criterion, not an estimate of
that allowance on a specified arithmetic orbit.

For a positive integer $A$ and a real $Y\ge5$, put
\[
 S_Y(A)=\log W_Y(A)=\sum_{\substack{p>Y\\p\mid A}}
          (v_p(A)-3)\log p,
 \qquad R_Y(A)=\prod_{\substack{p>Y\\p\mid A}}p.
\]
The sum is signed. For the finite primitive-pair reference measure
modulo $p^E$, $p>3$, write $\nu$ for the capped boundary valuation.
The local count proved in Section~\ref{om-section} gives
\begin{align*}
 \Pr(\nu=0)&=\frac{p-2}{p+1},\\
 \Pr(\nu=e)&=\frac{3(p-1)}{(p+1)p^e}\quad(1\le e<E),&
 \Pr(\nu=E)&=\frac{3}{(p+1)p^{E-1}}.
\end{align*}
Indeed the primitive state count modulo $p^e$ is
$p^{2e-2}(p^2-1)$, and each of the three disjoint boundary arms has
$\varphi(p^e)$ such states. Multiplication by a fixed $H$ with
$p\nmid N(H)$ permutes the primitive states. Distinct primes have
independent reference laws by CRT. These are reference statements;
no uniformity of actual small-height residuals or power orbits is assumed.

\subsection{Signed moments before and after conditioning on support}
Define $j(0)=0$ and $j(e)=e-3$ for $e\ge1$.
\begin{theorem}[Signed local moments]\label{se-moment}
For $0<\theta<1$ the infinite-precision signed moment is
\[
 Z_p(\theta)=1-\frac3{p+1}
 +\frac{3(p-1)}{(p+1)p^{1+2\theta}(1-p^{\theta-1})}.
\]
The finite capped moment is at most $Z_p(\theta)$. For $p\ge5$,
\[
 Z_p(1/2)=1-\frac3{p+1}(1-p^{-1}-p^{-3/2})
 \le1-\frac2{p+1}.
\]
Thus for a finite set $J$ of retained primes and the signed reference
variable $S=\sum_{p\in J}j(\nu_p)\log p$,
\[
 \log\mathbb E_\mu e^{S/2}\le-2\sum_{p\in J}\frac1{p+1}.
\]
For every probability law $\nu$ on the same finite space,
\[
 \mathbb E_\nu S\le2D(\nu\Vert\mu)
                       -4\sum_{p\in J}\frac1{p+1}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sum the geometric series over the positive depths. Capping at a positive
integer $E$ decreases $j$ on the positive branch, and hence decreases
its exponential moment. At $\theta=1/2$, cancellation uses
$(1-p^{-1})/(1-p^{-1/2})=1+p^{-1/2}$.
The inequality $1-p^{-1}-p^{-3/2}\ge2/3$ holds at $p=5$ and its
left side increases. Multiply the local moments and use
$\log(1-x)\le-x$. Finally, the tilted measure
$\mu_\theta(x)=e^{\theta S(x)}\mu(x)/\mathbb E_\mu e^{\theta S}$
and $D(\nu\Vert\mu_\theta)\ge0$ give the stated entropy bound.
\end{proof}
The unconditioned negative term above is a reciprocal-prime sum.
Conditioning on the supported primes gives a radical-sized allowance.

\begin{theorem}[Support-conditioned signed moments]\label{se-conditioned}
Given a hit $\nu_p\ge1$, the infinite-precision reference law is
\[
 q_p(e)=(1-p^{-1})p^{-(e-1)}\quad(e\ge1).
\]
At finite precision $E$, this formula holds for $e<E$, with
$q_p(E)=p^{-(E-1)}$. In either case
\[
 \mathbb E[p^{\theta(\nu_p-3)}\mid\nu_p\ge1]
 \le p^{-2\theta}\frac{1-p^{-1}}{1-p^{\theta-1}}.
\]
In infinite precision equality holds, and at $\theta=1/2$ the value
is $p^{-1}(1+p^{-1/2})$. Fix a supported set $J$ and write
$R_J=\prod_{p\in J}p$. If $D_J$ is the relative entropy of an actual
depth law on this support against the conditional product reference,
then
\[
 \mathbb E S\le2D_J-2\log R_J
                      +2\sum_{p\in J}\log(1+p^{-1/2}).
\]
More generally, for $\theta=1-\delta\in(0,1)$,
\[
 \mathbb E S\le\frac{D_J}{\theta}-2\log R_J
 +\frac1\theta\sum_{p\in J}
       \bigl(\log(1-p^{-1})-\log(1-p^{-\delta})\bigr).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Divide the positive-depth probabilities by $3/(p+1)$, sum the resulting
geometric series, and apply the same tilted-measure inequality.
Capping only decreases the signed moment. For primes $p>Y$, the last
sum is at most
$|J|Y^{-\delta}/(1-Y^{-\delta})$.
For example, $\delta=1/\sqrt{\log Y}$ makes it $o(\log R_J)$,
uniformly over finite nonempty supports as $Y\to\infty$; the empty
support has zero error.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An exact identity with a uniform height error}
Fix $t>0$ and any probability distribution on finitely many positive
integers $A$ satisfying $\log A\le3t$. This is a common upper height
bound for the ensemble. It applies in particular to an ensemble of
actual primitive boundaries $A=abc$ with $\log c\le t$.
Let
\[
 J=\{p>Y:p\mid A\},\qquad e=(v_p(A))_{p\in J},
 \qquad L=\sum_{p\in J}e_p\log p,\quad r=\log R_Y(A).
\]
Keep the support law exactly as in the actual ensemble, and put
$H_{\rm depth}=H(e\mid J)$, with natural logarithms.
For each prime in the finite union of the actual supports choose an
integer $E_p>3t/\log p$. Given $J$, let $q_J$ be the product of the
finite capped geometric laws in Theorem~\ref{se-conditioned}, and set
\[
 D_{\rm depth}=\mathbb E_J
   D\bigl(\operatorname{Law}(e\mid J)\Vert q_J\bigr),
 \qquad c_J=\sum_{p\in J}-\log(1-p^{-1}).
\]
Every actual depth is strictly below its reference precision cap.
The terminal reference atoms are needed for normalization but do not
enter the cross entropy at actual states.

\begin{theorem}[Height-sensitive signed entropy identity]\label{se-height}
With the preceding common-height assumptions,
\[
 \mathbb E S_Y(A)=D_{\rm depth}-2\mathbb E r
                     +H_{\rm depth}-\mathbb E c_J.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 0\le H_{\rm depth}\le\frac{3(\log2)t}{\log Y},
 \qquad 0\le\mathbb E c_J\le\frac{3t}{(Y-1)\log Y}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 -\frac{3t}{(Y-1)\log Y}
 \le \mathbb E S_Y(A)-(D_{\rm depth}-2\mathbb E r)
 \le \frac{3(\log2)t}{\log Y}.
\]
These estimates are uniform in the ensemble size, actual prime supports,
common factor, and the chosen precisions satisfying the strict bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On every actual depth vector,
$-\log q_J(e)=\sum_{p\in J}(e_p-1)\log p+c_J=L-r+c_J$.
Thus $D_{\rm depth}=\mathbb E(L-r+c_J)-H_{\rm depth}$;
subtracting $2\mathbb E r$ proves the identity since $S_Y=L-3r$.

Put $M=\lfloor3t/\log Y\rfloor$. Since $p>Y$ and $L\le3t$,
every possible depth vector satisfies $\sum e_p\le M$.
For a fixed support of size $r_0$, stars and bars gives exactly
$\binom M{r_0}$ positive integer vectors with sum at most $M$.
The empty support contributes one vector. As
$\binom M{r_0}\le2^M$, its conditional entropy is at most
$M\log2$. Also $|J|\le3t/\log Y$ and
$-\log(1-p^{-1})\le1/(p-1)\le1/(Y-1)$.
These prove both nonnegative error bounds and the final estimate.
\end{proof}

For a sequence of such ensembles with $Y\to\infty$, the uniform
one-sided upper bound $\mathbb E S_Y(A)\le o(t)$ is therefore
equivalent to
\[
 D_{\rm depth}\le2\mathbb E\log R_Y(A)+o(t).
\]
This permits negative linear signed credit; it does not assert that
the signed cost converges to zero after division by height.
For a point mass the identity still holds, with $H_{\rm depth}=0$.
No distributional assumption has been introduced into that single seed.

\subsection{Arithmetic scope and the remaining allowance bound}
This conditional allowance retains credits that the positive-excess
observable loses. For a concrete retained-prime comparison, take primes
$p<q<2p$ and the actual primitive seed $(a,b)=(p^4,q)$ with common
factor $H=1$. At the specified primes $\{p,q\}$ its boundary has
depths four and one. Its retained positive excess is $\log p$, whereas
its retained signed cost is $\log p-2\log q<0$ and
$\log(a+b)\sim4\log p$. This comparison does not control the other
prime factors of the boundary and does not supply a nontrivial power
orbit with small $\lambda$.

The new allowance has not been bounded arithmetically. Neither data
processing nor a sparse set of actual states proves it. On a homogeneous
orbit, the exact rank law gives $e_p=s_p+v_p(g)$ at the supported ranks
$d_p\mid g$, with total lifting cost at most $\log g$. The unresolved
part is the actual joint first-depth and supported-prime budget.
Shifted residuals require their own shifted ranks and stopping towers;
the homogeneous divisibility law cannot be transferred unchanged.
The dependency chain here consists of the proved local count, finite
geometric series, the relative-entropy identity, and stars and bars.
These ordinary probability statements are not claimed as new Lean
formalizations or as a resolution of the signed-tail gate or ABC.
