\begin{abstract}
We study the correlation of high prime multiplicity, low-depth credit and
imbalance in the project's $abc$ program. An elementary completion theorem
prescribes exponents on both large arms of $1+b=c$, forces norm-exponent
content one, and proves an exact compensating radical formula. A second
completion theorem uses a prime-element estimate with its Siegel-zero term
retained to produce rational-prime auxiliary norms, moving valuation patterns,
and squarefree complementary support. Its constants are uniform in a stated
pseudopolynomial-modulus range, with at most one exceptional conductor
explicitly excluded. Growing primes and growing prescribed depth can occur
in these fully compensated completions. Separately, a sharp Jacobian
ramification argument bounds the boundary degree of any rational map forcing
an exact norm power, ruling out the corresponding degree-only radical
transfer. None of these results supplies the all-point correlation required
for standard $abc$, which remains unproved here. Mathematical proofs,
external analytic inputs and the narrower formal scope are distinguished.
\end{abstract}
