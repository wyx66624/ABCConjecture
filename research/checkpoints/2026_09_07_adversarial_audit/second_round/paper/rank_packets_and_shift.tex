\section{Signed rank packets and the residual-shift boundary}
\label{sec:aa2-rank-packets}
The shared-generator theorem leaves an arithmetic signed tail. The present
section gives an exact decomposition on homogeneous orbits, a sufficient
rankwise budget with its full quantifiers, and an actual content-one family
which explains why that decomposition cannot be transferred unchanged to a
residual orbit. The rankwise arithmetic budget remains unproved. No result in
this section proves or disproves standard $abc$.

For a positive integer $T$ and a real $Y\ge1$, write
\[
 W_Y(T)=\prod_{\substack{p\mid T\\p>Y}}p^{v_p(T)-3}.
\]
This is a positive rational number. In particular, primes of exponent one or
two contribute negative logarithmic credit; they are not silently removed.
For an actual representation $z=u(1+\zeta)^{e_0}vw^g$, the shared-generator
parameter is
\[
 \rho=\frac{\max(1,\log N(v))\log(4+g)}{g}.
\]
The previously proved sufficient class assumes small $\rho$ and a bound
$W_{\rho^{-1/6}}(abc)\le g^B$. Its exact height absorption also works under
the weaker conclusion $(\log W_{\rho^{-1/6}}(abc))_+=o(\log c)$, provided that
the little-oh estimate is uniform on the stated class. This observation is
not a proof of either arithmetic estimate.

\subsection{An exact homogeneous decomposition}
Let $w$ be a primitive nonunit Eisenstein integer with no ramified factor
and no conjugate pair of split factors. Set
\[
 Q=N(w)\ge7,\qquad T_n=|P(w^n)|\quad(n\ge1),
 \qquad P(x+y\zeta)=xy(x+y).
\]
Every $w^n$ remains primitive. Its boundary is nonzero, since a primitive
zero-boundary element is a unit. Rotate $w^n$ into the positive sector and
denote the largest entry of the resulting primitive triple by $c_n$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-height}
 \log c_n\ge\tfrac12 n\log Q.
\end{equation}

For $p>3$ with $p\nmid Q$, let $d_p$ be the residue order of
\[
 \alpha=(w/\bar w)^3
\]
at a place above $p$. At a split prime the two components give inverse
residues, so this order does not depend on the component. At an inert prime
the residue lies in the norm-one group of order $p+1$. In particular
$d_p\mid p-1$ or $d_p\mid p+1$, and $p\nmid d_p$. Put
\[
 s_p=v_p(T_{d_p}).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Exact homogeneous first-depth law]
\label{lem:aa2-rank-law}
For every such prime and every $n\ge1$,
\[
 p\mid T_n\ \Longleftrightarrow\ d_p\mid n,
 \qquad
 v_p(T_n)=s_p+v_p(n)\quad\text{if }d_p\mid n.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The cubic identity
\[
 w^{3n}-\bar w^{3n}=3\sqrt{-3}\,P(w^n)
\]
identifies the valuation of $\alpha^n-1$ with that of $T_n$: both $Q$ and
$3\sqrt{-3}$ are units at $p$. Reduction modulo $p$ proves the support
criterion. If $n=d_pm$, the usual odd-prime binomial argument in the
unramified local quadratic ring gives
\[
 v_p(\alpha^n-1)=v_p(\alpha^{d_p}-1)+v_p(m).
\]
Raising a principal unit to a $p$-coprime power preserves its valuation;
raising it to $p$ increases it by one, since the linear binomial term is
uniquely least. There is no ramification correction at $p>3$. Finally
$p\nmid d_p$, so $v_p(m)=v_p(n)$. At split primes, norm-one conjugacy gives
the same normalized valuation in both components.
\end{proof}

For $Y\ge3$ define the finite signed rank packet
\[
 A_d(Y;w)=\prod_{\substack{p>Y\\d_p=d}}p^{s_p-3},
 \qquad
 L_{n,Y}=\prod_{\substack{p>Y\\d_p\mid n}}p^{v_p(n)}.
\]
Every prime in $A_d$ divides the fixed nonzero integer $T_d$, so the product
is finite. Primes dividing $Q$ never divide $T_n$ and are not included.

\begin{proposition}[The signed packet identity]
\label{prop:aa2-signed-packet}
For every $n\ge1$ and $Y\ge3$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-signed-packet}
 \boxed{W_Y(T_n)=L_{n,Y}\prod_{d\mid n}A_d(Y;w),
 \qquad L_{n,Y}\mid n.}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each supported prime above $Y$, Lemma~\ref{lem:aa2-rank-law} separates
its exponent $v_p(T_n)-3$ into $(s_p-3)+v_p(n)$. Group the first terms by
their exact rank $d_p\mid n$. Each exponent in the second product is at most
the corresponding exponent in $n$, proving the divisibility assertion.
No positivity assumption on the first product is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A rankwise polynomial budget is sufficient}
For the homogeneous representation take
\[
 \rho_n=\frac{\log(4+n)}n,\qquad Y_n=\rho_n^{-1/6}.
\]
The next theorem restricts to $Y_n\ge3$, the domain of the rank packets;
this includes all sufficiently large $n$.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform absorption from rankwise budgets]
\label{thm:aa2-rank-budget}
Fix $\kappa\ge0$. Consider actual pairs $(w,n)$ as above, with $n\ge1$
and $Y_n\ge3$, satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-rank-premise}
 A_d(Y_n;w)\le(Qd)^\kappa\qquad\text{for every }d\mid n.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-rank-sum}
 \log W_{Y_n}(T_n)\le\log n+
 \kappa\tau(n)\log Q+\frac\kappa2\tau(n)\log n.
\end{equation}
Consequently $(\log W_{Y_n}(T_n))_+/\log c_n\to0$ uniformly on this class as
$n\to\infty$. For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a threshold depending
only on $\kappa,\varepsilon$ and the absolute constants in the established
shared-generator theorem such that every pair in this class above that
threshold satisfies
\[
 c_n\le\operatorname{rad}(T_n)^{1+\varepsilon}.
\]
The arithmetic hypothesis \eqref{eq:aa2-rank-premise} is not proved for
all roots or all ranks.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take logarithms in Proposition~\ref{prop:aa2-signed-packet}, use
$L_{n,Y_n}\le n$, and sum \eqref{eq:aa2-rank-premise}. The involution
$d\mapsto n/d$ of the divisors gives
\[
 \sum_{d\mid n}\log d=\tfrac12\tau(n)\log n,
\]
including a possible fixed divisor $\sqrt n$. This proves
\eqref{eq:aa2-rank-sum}. Pairing small and large divisors also gives the
elementary bound $\tau(n)\le2\sqrt n$. Divide by
\eqref{eq:aa2-height}, use $Q\ge7$, and obtain the explicit uniform estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-rank-uniform}
 \frac{\log W_{Y_n}(T_n)}{\log c_n}
 \le\frac{2\log n}{n\log7}+
      \frac{4\kappa}{\sqrt n}+
      \frac{2\kappa\log n}{\sqrt n\log7}\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
The one-column shared-generator theorem supplies a uniformly sublinear
angular penalty and full small-prime mass at $Y_n$. In the compensated cubic
height inequality, the remaining high-prime signed mass is bounded by
\eqref{eq:aa2-rank-uniform}. Its nonnegative right side also bounds the positive
part of the left side; no two-sided sublinear claim is made about a signed
quantity which may retain substantial negative credit. Choose the threshold at which their combined
ratio, including the constant $\log8$, is at most
$3\varepsilon/(1+\varepsilon)$. The standard absorption yields the asserted
inequality. No threshold depends on $w$ or its prime support.
\end{proof}

This theorem asks for polynomial dependence on both $Q$ and the individual
rank; it does not demand that their complete divisor product be polynomial
in $n$. That latter inference would be false without an additional arithmetic
restriction. For example, the abstract equality model $A_d=d^\kappa$ gives
\[
 \prod_{d\mid n}A_d=n^{\kappa\tau(n)/2},
\]
which exceeds any fixed power of $n$ for $\kappa>0$ along $n=2^j$.
This is a counterexample to that abstract aggregation inference, not an
arithmetically realized counterexample to a rank-packet theorem. Estimate
\eqref{eq:aa2-rank-uniform} shows why the false inference is unnecessary.
Also, a bound on a full signed packet at cutoff zero does not automatically
survive the removal of small primes carrying negative credit. The actual
cutoff $Y_n$ in \eqref{eq:aa2-rank-premise} is essential.

\subsection{A small-rho content-one residual counterfamily}
The divisibility $L_{n,Y}\mid n$ uses the homogeneous first-depth law.
The next construction refutes one precise attempted transplantation of that
law to $vw^g$ while preserving every structural hypothesis of the new
small-rho class.

Fix $w=2+\zeta$, with $N(w)=7$ and $w^2=3+5\zeta$. For each $k\ge1$ put
\[
 g_k=2^k,\qquad w^{g_k}=A_k+B_k\zeta.
\]
Modulo five, this is the nonzero scalar $3^{g_k/2}$. Hence $A_k+B_k$ is
invertible modulo $5^k$. By the modular inverse and the Chinese remainder
theorem, there is a unique $y_k\in[0,84\cdot5^k)$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aa2-crt}
 B_k+y_k(A_k+B_k)\equiv0\pmod{5^k},
 \qquad y_k\equiv42\pmod{84}.
\end{equation}
Set
\[
 v_k=1+y_k\zeta,\qquad V_k=1+y_k+y_k^2,
 \qquad z_k=v_kw^{g_k},\qquad T_k=|P(z_k)|.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Deep residual hits with total content one]
\label{thm:aa2-residual-family}
Every $z_k$ is primitive with nonzero boundary and gives a primitive positive
triple after unit rotation. Its complete split-norm exponent gcd equals one.
Its displayed shared representation satisfies
\[
 \rho_k=\frac{\log V_k\log(4+2^k)}{2^k}\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad v_5(T_k)\ge k,
 \qquad v_5(g_k)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pair $(1,y_k)$ is primitive. Since $y_k$ is divisible by three and
seven, neither prime divides $V_k$. Thus the factorization of $v_k$ has no
ramified factor, no conjugate pair, and no factor above seven. Multiplication
by $w^{g_k}$ preserves primitivity and the oriented factorization. Its norm
is greater than one, so its primitive boundary cannot vanish.

Since $y_k\equiv2\pmod4$, one has $V_k\equiv3\pmod4$. At least one of
its prime exponents is odd, since a factorization with all exponents even
would make $V_k$ an odd square. This prime is not seven. Its odd exponent
has gcd one with the norm exponent $2^k$ at seven, proving that the complete
split-exponent gcd is one.

The second coordinate of $z_k$ is $B_k+y_k(A_k+B_k)$, divisible by $5^k$.
Modulo five, $B_k=0$, $A_k\ne0$, and \eqref{eq:aa2-crt} gives $y_k=0$.
The first coordinate $A_k-y_kB_k$ and the sum of the coordinates are both
units modulo five. Hence $v_5(T_k)$ is exactly the valuation of the second
coordinate and is at least $k$. Clearly $5\nmid2^k$.

Finally $y_k<84\cdot5^k$ and $y_k\ge2$ give
\[
 \log V_k<\log(3\cdot84^2)+2k\log5,
 \qquad \log(4+2^k)\le(k+2)\log2.
\]
Thus $\rho_k=O(k^2/2^k)\to0$. This argument does not factor the growing
integer $V_k$.
\end{proof}

For the fixed step root $w=2+\zeta$, the homogeneous data at five are
$d_5=2$ and $s_5=v_5(P(w^2))=v_5(120)=1$. The displayed family therefore
refutes the exact proposed extension
\[
 v_5(P(vw^g))\le s_5+v_5(g)
\]
when $s_5$ is retained from the homogeneous step root: its proposed bound
is one while the actual depth is arbitrarily large. This failure persists
under small $\rho$ and complete exponent gcd one. A theorem defining new
residual-dependent first depths or shifted residue classes is a different
claim and is not refuted.

This counterfamily does not refute the signed-tail gate. Five eventually
lies below $\rho_k^{-1/6}$; the construction also provides no upper bound
on the entire boundary radical or on all its signed credits. In particular
it supplies no ABC counterexample. The proper successor is the shifted
residue lattice at each prime and depth, treated separately.

\subsection{Exact local replay and formal scope}
Independent modular exponentiation and CRT gave, for example,
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|c}
k&g_k&y_k&v_5(T_k)\\\hline
16&65536&7181029215570&16\\
18&262144&144280296684930&20\\
20&1048576&5276983743390870&21
\end{array}
\]
Each exact depth was checked by vanishing modulo $5^e$ and nonvanishing
modulo $5^{e+1}$, with the other boundary coordinates verified to be units.
The same procedure reaches $k=60$ without constructing its enormous full
boundary. These are local certificates; no complete factorization of those
boundary integers is claimed.

The homogeneous packet and residual-family ordinary proofs were independently
reviewed by the critical-bottleneck research agent. The arithmetic budget
\eqref{eq:aa2-rank-premise} remains open after that review.

The separate second-round module \texttt{ShiftedResidueCounts.lean} proves
eight scoped declarations using only \texttt{Std}. It proves the actual
residue-hit parameterization
\[
 n<N,\ n\bmod d=a
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists j<C(d,a,N),\ n=a+dj,
\]
its uniqueness, and the strict one-period counting error via explicit
Euclidean division. It also checks the actual infinite family
$(1+5\zeta)(1+50\zeta)^n$ whose boundary is $5\pmod{25}$ at every index,
while the step boundary has exact five-adic depth two. All eight declarations
were checked by Lean 4.32.0 with warnings as errors and axiom dependencies
limited to \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
This formal scope does not include the general rank law, the rankwise
arithmetic budget, the complete norm-one group classification, or the real
weighted global tail limit. No such stronger formal claim is made here.
