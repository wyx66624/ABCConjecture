I read the entire new workflow and its initializer, dependency-project preparer, formal verifier and finite verifier. I also read the fifth script, the manuscript builder, which is not a step of this CI job. The workflow has read-only repository permissions, initializes evidence immediately after checkout, replays finite inputs before dependency setup, installs the toolchain, prepares an isolated Mathlib-only project, requests the five actual import roots, compiles the canonical new source and always uploads the three explicit evidence artifacts.

Thus none of those paths can upload a prior successful result as the current run's PASS. This conclusion is from the complete control-flow read, not a claim of new executed failure tests.
