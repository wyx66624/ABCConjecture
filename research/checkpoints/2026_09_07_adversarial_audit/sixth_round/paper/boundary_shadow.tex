\section{A non-CM boundary curve and its actual local shadows}
\label{bs-section}

This section separates an exact boundary curve, its unbounded actual
local shadows, and a conditional obstruction to one specified modular
elimination procedure. It gives no global pure-power family and does
not identify compatible Galois representations from finitely many
matching traces.

Put $r^2=-3$, $K=\mathbb Q(r)$, $\zeta=(1+r)/2$, and
\[
 F(a,b)=a^4+3a^3b+5a^2b^2+3ab^3+b^4,
 \qquad x=a^2+b^2,\quad y=a+b.
\]
The actual Frey equation is
\[
 E_{a,b}:\quad Y^2=X^3+12yX^2+6(3y^2+xr)X.
\]
At the excluded boundary seed $(a,b)=(0,1)$ it specializes to
\[
 E_0:\quad Y^2=X^3+12X^2+6(3+r)X,
 \qquad \Delta(E_0)=2^{11}3^4(3+r).
\]
Since $N_{K/\mathbb Q}(3+r)=12$, this equation has good reduction
at every prime above a rational prime greater than three.

\begin{theorem}[The boundary curve has no geometric CM]
\label{bs-noncm}
The elliptic curve $E_0$ has no complex multiplication over
$\overline{\mathbb Q}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Full finite-field enumerations give the following exact data,
including the point at infinity:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|r|r|r}
 \text{residue field}&r&\#E_0&t&t^2-4q\\\hline
 \mathbb F_{25}&r^2=-3&34&-8&-36\\
 \mathbb F_7&2&12&-4&-12\\
 \mathbb F_7&5&4&4&-12
\end{array}
\]
Here $X^2+3$ is irreducible modulo five. The certificate enumerates
all $625$ affine pairs over $\mathbb F_{25}$ and all $49$ pairs in
each of the two prime-field reductions.

Use the auxiliary Tate-module prime $17$. If $E_0$ had geometric
CM, its geometric endomorphism algebra would be an imaginary
quadratic field $M$. The algebra $M\otimes\mathbb Q_{17}$ embeds
into $\operatorname{End}(V_{17}(E_0))$. All these endomorphisms are
defined over some finite number-field extension. Consequently their
action commutes with some positive power of Frobenius at each good
place. These are the usual injectivity and good-reduction properties
of Tate modules; see Milne, \emph{Elliptic Curves}, second edition,
Proposition~II.6.7 and Proposition~V.8.3, in the
\href{https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf}{author's text}.
The latter Frobenius argument applies over the completion of $K$
just as it does over a completion of $\mathbb Q$.

At norm $25$ the eigenvalues are $-4\pm3i$, and their ratio is
$(7-24i)/25$. Its real part $7/25$ excludes the roots of unity in
$\mathbb Q(i)$. At either norm-seven place the eigenvalues are
$2\pm r$ or $-2\pm r$, and their ratio has real part $1/7$.
This excludes the sixth roots of unity in $\mathbb Q(r)$. Thus every
positive power of either Frobenius still has distinct eigenvalues.
Its centralizer has dimension two and is the same as the centralizer
of the original Frobenius.

The two-dimensional algebra $M\otimes\mathbb Q_{17}$ must therefore
equal each centralizer. At norm $25$ the latter is
\[
 \mathbb Q_{17}[T]/(T^2+8T+25)
       \simeq\mathbb Q_{17}\times\mathbb Q_{17},
\]
since $-1$ is a square modulo $17$. At norm seven it is
$\mathbb Q_{17}[T]/(T^2\pm4T+7)$, a quadratic field: its
discriminant has nonsquare unit part $-3$ modulo $17$. A product
algebra with nontrivial idempotents is not a field. This contradiction
proves the theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Simultaneous actual positive local shadows]
\label{bs-local-shadows}
For every pair of integers $L\ge1$ and $H\ge0$, there are positive
coprime integers $a,b$ with $a+b>H$ such that
\[
 (a,b)\equiv(0,1)\pmod L,\qquad
 (x,y,F(a,b))\equiv(1,1,1)\pmod L,
 \qquad 2x-y^2=(a-b)^2=1.
\]
The same seed passes every direct congruence test that $F(a,b)$ be
a $g$-th power modulo every divisor of $L$, simultaneously for all
integers $g\ge1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a sufficiently large integer $k\ge1$ and put
$a=Lk$, $b=Lk+1$. Consecutiveness proves primitivity, positivity
and the last equality, and their sum tends to infinity with $k$.
The congruences follow by substitution. The residue $1$ is the
$g$-th power of $1$ for every positive $g$.
\end{proof}

The Frey coefficients are integer polynomials in $x,y$. Thus these
actual curves reduce to $E_0$ at every prime above any $q>3$ dividing
$L$. Arbitrary finitely many precisions can be prescribed by including
the required $q^e$ in $L$. This statement retains the exact inverse
square condition and positive primitive integer seeds. It does not
assert the integer equation $F(a,b)=Q^g$.

\begin{proposition}[A specified Mazur product has a zero factor]
\label{bs-conditional-mazur}
Suppose a descended eigenform $f_0$ has independently been identified
with the compatible representation of $E_0$ twisted by the fixed
splitting character. At an auxiliary $q>3$, consider an elimination
product over all original seed residue states admitted by positivity,
primitivity, the inverse-square condition and the direct $F$ power
residue condition. Suppose its factors are the differences of the
compatible candidate and curve Frobenius traces. This product is zero
at every such $q$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The admitted state $(0,1)$ yields the twisted trace of $E_0$, equal
by hypothesis to the candidate trace. Its factor is zero. Theorem
\ref{bs-local-shadows} additionally gives actual positive primitive
seeds shadowing these states simultaneously at any finite set of
auxiliary primes.
\end{proof}

The proposition applies to the specified product with its stated
state space. It does not restrict auxiliary global covers, additional
local conditions proved from those covers, deformation arguments, or
other modular information. An identification of $f_0$ is an explicit
premise, not a conclusion drawn from a few computed coefficients.

\begin{lemma}[The fixed character gives trace minus four at thirteen]
\label{bs-character-sign}
For the $d=3$ splitting character in
\cite[Table~3.3 and the following definition]{PacettiVillagra2022QCurves},
both twisted Frobenius traces of $E_0$ at primes above thirteen are
$-4$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The local character $\theta$ on $(\mathcal O_K/8)^\times$ has values
\[
 \theta(\zeta-1)=1,\quad \theta(r)=i,\quad
 \theta(3+2r)=1,\quad \theta(-1)=1.
\]
The four generators have orders $3,4,2,2$. Their $48$ products are
distinct. Indeed, reduction modulo two removes the order-three
factor. Modulo four the remaining exponent of $r$ must be even.
Now $r^2\equiv5\pmod8$, while $3+2r=1+4\zeta$; the $\zeta$
coefficient removes its exponent, and the remaining scalar factors
$5$ and $-1$ are independent modulo eight. This also verifies directly
that the prescribed values define a character on all units. It is
trivial on global units. Since $r^2\equiv1\pmod4$ has character
$-1$, its conductor is exactly eight.

For $\pi=3+\zeta$ and $\bar\pi=4-\zeta$, both of norm thirteen,
\[
 \pi\equiv(\zeta-1)(3+2r),\qquad
 \bar\pi\equiv(\zeta-1)^2r^2(3+2r)\pmod8.
\]
Their local character values are therefore $1,-1$. At the associated
primes $r$ has images $6,7$ respectively. Full point counts give
$18,10$ points, hence untwisted traces $-4,4$ respectively. Global
principality and the absence of other ramified finite places give
$\chi((\pi))=\theta(\pi)^{-1}$ and the analogous equality for
$\bar\pi$. Both twisted traces are consequently $-4$.
\end{proof}

An independent exact PARI/GP~2.15.4 newspace replay, with character
$\psi_3=(12/\cdot)$, gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|l|l}
 N&\dim S_2^{\mathrm{new}}(N,\psi_3)&
       \text{eigenorbit fields}&\text{CM discriminants}\\\hline
 36&2&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})&-4\\
 72&0&\text{none}&\text{none}\\
 144&2&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2})&-4\\
 288&4&\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)&0\\
 576&8&\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2}),\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2}),
          \mathbb Q(\zeta_8)&-4,-4,0
\end{array}
\]
Here zero is the software's non-CM return value. The field-degree
sums equal the dimensions, and the exact coefficients through $25$
are retained. The non-CM orbits at $288$ and $576$ have rational
$a_{13}=4$ and $-4$, respectively. Thus an independently justified
membership of the $E_0$ descent in this list would select the level
$576$ orbit. The positive-seed large-image argument cannot simply
be applied to $F(0,1)=1$, which has no multiplicative prime above
three. A separate general modularity or sufficiently-large-prime
argument for this fixed non-CM curve remains necessary for that
unconditional identification.

\paragraph{Verification scope.}
The ordinary non-CM and local-shadow proofs were independently
reviewed, and the point counts and character sign were separately
replayed. The certificate enumerates all $48$ local units, checks all
$2304$ multiplication pairs and both norm-thirteen reductions. The
newspace computation is an independently repeated exact software
calculation; it is not a Lean proof of modular-form algorithms or an
exclusion of the remaining eigenforms. The corresponding scripts,
canonical JSON files and exact hashes are recorded in the sixth-round
adversarial-audit checkpoint.
