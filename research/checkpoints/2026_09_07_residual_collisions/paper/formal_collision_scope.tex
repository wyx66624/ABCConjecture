\section{Formal cross-residual cancellation and annotated determinants}
\label{sec:formal-residual-collisions}

The preceding integer collision mechanism admits a direct formalization
using the repository's existing Eisenstein multiplication, norm, boundary,
and exact boundary gcd theorem.  The new module
\texttt{ResidualCollisions.lean} contains fifteen declarations.  Its central
statement is the actual integer equality
\[
 \gcd\bigl(P(v_1H),P(v_2H)\bigr)
 =\gcd\bigl(P(v_1H),P(v_2\overline{v_1})\bigr),
 \qquad v_1H\text{ primitive}.
\]
Only the first product needs to be primitive in this formal statement.
Integer gcd retains signs by taking the usual nonnegative gcd; zero second
boundary and the primes two and three are included.  The proof first
establishes coprimality of $N(v_1)$ with $P(v_1H)$, and then cancels the
actual scalar cube in
$(v_2\overline{v_1})(v_1H)=N(v_1)v_2H$.

The module also verifies the exact Gram identity and its integer norm
consequence.  For a specified boundary arm $\ell\in\{a,b,a+b\}$, it proves
\[
 \begin{gathered}
  q\mid\ell(vH),\qquad q\mid\ell(wH),\qquad
  \gcd(q,N(H))=1,\qquad \det(v,w)\ne0
  \\
  \Longrightarrow\qquad 3q^2\le4N(v)N(w).
 \end{gathered}
\]
Here $q$ is an arbitrary integer.  The chosen arm is the same for the entire
modulus.  The proof obtains divisibility of the determinant after
multiplication, cancels $N(H)$, and uses the nonzero integer quotient to
deduce $q^2\le\det(v,w)^2$.  Different arm choices at different primes,
the full CRT lattice index, and the first-minimum question are not silently
inserted into this formal signature.

The separate module \texttt{QuarticTwistArithmetic.lean} adds twelve
declarations supporting the independent elliptic descent.  Its actual
primitive integer statements exclude squares for the three dual quartics
with coefficients $(2,-4,8)$, $(2,-12,72)$, and $(6,-12,24)$ modulo sixteen,
and for the quartic with coefficients $(3,-12,48)$ modulo three.
Finite residue propositions are proved by kernel reduction, and a general
polynomial congruence lemma transfers them to arbitrary coprime integer
coordinates.  The module also proves the cleared-denominator identity
linking the two norms to the elliptic twist, and the exact example
$M_1(101,355)=192529^2$.  It does not formalize the elliptic group law,
isogeny descent, Mordell--Weil theorem, torsion classification, or the
complete combined-norm obstruction.

A fresh standalone Lake build with Lean~4.32.0 and warnings treated as
errors accepted these twenty-seven declarations and recompiled all fifty-two
declarations in the three unchanged local dependency modules.  Every
declaration was subjected to a printed-axiom audit; the union consists of
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
The saved inventory identifies the exact source bytes by SHA-256.
The full lcm envelope and finite-reference probability theorems remain
ordinary proofs.  No private-depth estimate, height-to-measure transfer,
or complete ABC proof is asserted by this scoped formal verification.
