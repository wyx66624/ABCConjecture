\section{Fifteenth continuation: signed rigidity and the remaining point problem}
\label{fifteenth-status}

The fixed-prime argument now uses actual signed products after a coherent
unit choice. Its target has at most $n$ elements. The complete positive
excess costs at most $(5/2)n\log(8B)$, and a single actual maximal-depth
index contains every sufficiently deep layer. Outside that index the
cost is at most $7n^{5/6}\log(8B)$. The stronger support threshold
excludes even depth-one hits at a second root of the independent domain.
These conclusions include arbitrarily large primes individually. They
still require a bound on the number and total weight of different prime
labels before summation over all primes can close the signed gate.
The strict private-domain density improvement from the preceding
continuation remains available; its complement remains a separate target.

On the surviving cubic geometric branch, explicit descents in
$\mathbb Q(i)$ and $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$ determine an isogeny quotient
of order three. They prove, by ordinary mathematics, that both displayed
elliptic curves have rank one and the first genus-two quotient has
Jacobian rank two. The Gaussian cube is automatic on every rational
point of the first elliptic curve. It supplies a reversible map and
does not by itself exclude any of those points. The simultaneous
quartic, its positive-domain conditions and the other quotient curves
remain live rational-point problems. Neither exact rank nor a finite
list of tested multiples is a complete rational-point determination.

The finite formal package consists of 55 new declarations: 22 signed
count and depth statements, 18 actual determinant/product/diamond
connections, and 15 integer and rational-function descent identities.
They were freshly compiled together with 56 unchanged local dependency
declarations. All 111 explicit axiom queries were checked; their union
is exactly \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. The compiler is Lean 4.32.0 and Mathlib is pinned
to \texttt{81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997}; its compiled
dependency cache was reused. The exact arithmetic replay independently
checks eleven rational-function identities, the nontrivial unit-class
witness and all 486 primitive residue hits of the specified model
modulo 27. Sampling is not used to prove the ordinary rank upper bound.

Local reuse of the official Fermat source has also advanced to a bounded
sample of eighteen unchanged dependency modules at the previously cited
upstream commit. The six named solution declarations and their six
exported wrappers have actual local axiom queries using only the standard
three axioms. This sample was compiled with the present compiler and
Mathlib cache, requesting only Lean object output. It is separate from
the 55 new declarations above. The complete upstream import remains
uncompiled locally; the full-source inventory contains 60,475 local
modules for its final check. Neither the sample nor the inventory
establishes compatibility of every untried source. The successful cusp-form
bridge and the official full-Fermat verification records keep their
distinct local and upstream provenance.

The next substantive obligations are joint arithmetic control across
different primes, the remaining independent-domain complement, complete
points on the simultaneous geometric locus, and uniform control of
moving exponents, heights and residual coefficients. A failed special
test does not exclude any of these parent routes. The research group
retains independent analytic, arithmetic, geometric and formal audits.
There is still no proof or disproof of $abc$, and no complete formal
proof of either conclusion.
