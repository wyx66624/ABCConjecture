# What has closed, and what still separates the project from ABC

The standard conjecture remains unproved and undisproved. For every
epsilon > 0 the target is a constant C_epsilon, independent of the positive
primitive triple a+b=c, bounding

    log c - (1+epsilon) log rad(abc).

A disproof requires one fixed positive epsilon and an unbounded sequence
on which that expression is unbounded above. Finite examples, auxiliary
counterexamples and the number of Lean declarations do not measure a
percentage of completion of this universal statement.

## Fixed geometry: one branch is now closed in ordinary mathematics

The complete local analytic proof and an exact five-primary sieve at
thirteen classify the six rational points of

    W^2 = z^6 - 27z^4 + 99z^2 - 9.

All twelve five-adic disks are covered; the four extra local candidates
are excluded by the finite sieve. The actual inverse and second square at
the same source give exactly twelve boundary points on D_(3,zeta), none
in the positive seed domain. This excludes that unit class for odd
exponents divisible by three. The identity class already has its earlier
three-adic obstruction. The zeta-squared class remains a separate problem.

This closes the fixed branch's previous analytic-tail, zero-count, rational
sieve and inverse bottlenecks. Its height/rank/analytic proof chain is
ordinary mathematics under internal independent review, not yet a complete
Lean formalization. For ABC the major missing geometric input is still a
height bound uniform in the actual varying exponents, residuals and source
parameters. Solving finitely many fixed curves does not provide it.

## Main arithmetic: different-prime owners and the full signed ledger

On the original block n > 324 prime, B=n^4 and w_k=3k+zeta, the missing
bound still concerns the full signed boundary sum

    J(T) = sum_(p|T) (v_p(T)-3) log p.

The negative contributions from depths one and two must be retained.
Per-prime bounds or simultaneous common-hit rectangles do not control
different primes choosing different actual owners.

The new actual coefficient-packet example disproves an absolute
fixed-power bound on lattice index based on just one short primitive
polynomial. A valid replacement is a second integer polynomial G, coprime
to the actual boundary polynomial F_n=T_n/3, meeting every adjusted owner
congruence. The resultant then gives the precise sufficient estimate

    J(T-packet) <= deg(G) log||F_n||_2 + (3n-1) log||G||_2
                  +delta_3 log 3 - 3 log rad(M_T).

Constructing G with the needed simultaneous degree and height saving is
open. The automatic product of owner factors merely recovers the original
owner-height budget. The exact correction at three is required; omitting
it was detected and repaired by independent review.

## Nonlinear selectors and actual divisor allocation

Square and cube selectors now preserve actual pure-power structure while
meeting any fixed admissible finite packet and producing positive-density
good outputs. The square case uses an elementary linear sieve; the cubic
case verifies the hypotheses of the existing Booker--Browning theorem.
The 24 new Lean theorems verify their concrete arithmetic, not these sieve
or density conclusions. Moving packets, growing exponents and a transfer
to every original root remain missing. The affine intersection theorem
also shows why a generic affine output cannot silently be called an
original power point.

For divisor allocation, actual paired packets give a constructive m-step
sufficient certificate with each prime used exactly once. The new medium
prime examples show that largest-prime size alone does not control the
central divisor gap. A uniform supply of suitable packets is still open;
the scalar-defect premise itself contains a restricted ABC problem.

## Research priorities

Concentrate the principal arithmetic work on a genuinely smaller coprime
resultant certificate, or another weighted relation connecting different
prime owners on the original block. Preserve every signed prime credit.
Continue the independent geometry with the remaining unit class and the
varying-family height dependency chain. Keep constructive packet supply
and nonlinear moving-data thresholds as separate live routes. A failed
specific inequality is recorded with its exact counterexample or repaired
statement; the parent direction is not discarded because progress is hard.

Even after the structured block gate, a complete ABC proof needs its
exceptional-root complement, arbitrary input roots, residuals, ramification
and all-triple coverage, followed by an independently audited complete
Lean chain. These are mathematical obligations, not routine final steps.
