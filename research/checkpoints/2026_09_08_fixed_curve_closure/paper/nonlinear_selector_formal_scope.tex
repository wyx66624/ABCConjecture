\section{The actual square and cube selector arithmetic in Lean}
\label{sec-nps18-formal-scope}

The new module
\texttt{Nonlinear\allowbreak{}Power\allowbreak{}Selectors.lean}
contains 24 theorems for the actual root-parameter families used in
NP1 and CU1. Its definitions multiply the integer coordinate pair
$(3k,1)$ literally. No output power representation or primitive
Bezout witness is assumed for either concrete family.

Write $\zeta^2=\zeta-1$, and for pairs of integers set
\[
 (a,b)(c,d)=(ac-bd,ad+bc+bd),\qquad
 N(a,b)=a^2+ab+b^2,\quad T(a,b)=ab(a+b).
\]
For every integer $k$, let $w=(3k,1)$, $z_2=w^2$, $z_3=w^3$,
and $N_k=9k^2+3k+1$. The implemented definitions of $z_2,z_3$
are respectively the product of $w$ with itself and that product
with $w$.

\paragraph{Exact coordinates and the complete boundaries.}
For all $k\in\mathbb Z$, the formal identities are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 N(w)&=N_k, & N_k&\equiv1\pmod3,\\
 z_2&=(9k^2-1,6k+1), & (z_2)_1+(z_2)_2&=3k(3k+2),\\
 z_3&=((3k)^3-3(3k)-1,\,3(3k)(3k+1)),\\
 (z_3)_1+(z_3)_2&=(3k)^3+3(3k)^2-1,\\
 N(z_2)&=N_k^2, & N(z_3)&=N_k^3.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The entire boundary products, with no removed prime factors, are
\[
 \begin{split}
 T(z_2)&=(3k-1)(3k+1)(6k+1)(3k)(3k+2),\\
 T(z_3)&=3(3k)(3k+1)\bigl((3k)^3-3(3k)-1\bigr)\\
       &\hspace{30mm}\cdot\bigl((3k)^3+3(3k)^2-1\bigr).
 \end{split}
\]
These are obtained directly by expanding the coordinate products;
the norm-power identities follow by substitution. The modulus-three
claim follows from $N_k=3(3k^2+k)+1$.

If $k\geq1$, both coordinates of each output, their sum and their
boundary are positive, and $N_k>1$. For the cubic first coordinate,
put $a=3k\geq3$; then $a^3-3a-1>0$, while the second coordinate
is $3a(a+1)>0$. The square and norm claims are immediate from their
displayed formulas. Positivity alone uses $k\geq1$; the polynomial
identities and coprimality below hold for every integer $k$.

\paragraph{Actual primitivity without an assumed witness.}
For the square coordinates $A=9k^2-1$, $C=6k+1$, the integer
identity
\[
 (-12k^2-1)A+3k^2(6k-1)C=1
\]
proves coprimality. For the cube put $a=3k$ and $F=a^3-3a-1$.
The three identities
\[
 \begin{split}
 -F+3(9k^3-3k)&=1,\\
 -F+a(a^2-3)&=1,\\
 F-(a+1)(a^2-a-2)&=1
 \end{split}
\]
show that $F$ is coprime to $3$, $a$ and $a+1$, hence to the
actual second coordinate $3a(a+1)$. The Lean proofs construct these
witnesses and use integer coprimality lemmas.

For any coprime integers $A,C$, if $uA+vC=1$ then
\[
 (u-v)A+v(A+C)=1,\qquad
 (v-u)C+u(A+C)=1.
\]
Thus all three pairs of arms have gcd one. The module proves the
corresponding actual equalities
$\operatorname{Int.gcd}(A,C)=
\operatorname{Int.gcd}(A,A+C)=
\operatorname{Int.gcd}(C,A+C)=1$
and applies them to both concrete outputs.

\paragraph{Every boundary prime leaves the root norm a unit.}
Over any field, the simultaneous equations
\[
 AC(A+C)=0,\qquad A^2+AC+C^2=0
\]
force $A=C=0$. If either coordinate is zero this follows from the
square of the other; if $A+C=0$, subtract $A(A+C)$ from the norm
equation to get $C^2=0$ and then $A=0$.
For a primitive integer pair, a prime divisor $q$ of its full
boundary therefore cannot divide its norm: reduction of a Bezout
identity would give $0=1$ in $\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$.

Apply this proved helper to $z_2$ and $z_3$, using their proved
coprimality. Since their norms are $N_k^2$ and $N_k^3$, a zero
root norm modulo $q$ would give a zero output norm, a contradiction.
Consequently the final concrete theorems assert, for every
$k\in\mathbb Z$ and natural prime $q$,
\[
 q\mid T(z_i)\ \Longrightarrow\
 N_k\in(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^\times,
 \qquad q\nmid N_k,\qquad i=2,3.
\]
They do not assume a marked prime or a lower cutoff; primes two and
three are included. The statements use the literal full boundary,
not a selected or prefactored part of it.

\paragraph{Exact verification inventory.}
The 24 theorem declarations consist of three root-norm statements;
six square and six cube statements (coordinates, sum, norm, boundary,
positivity, coprimality in each family); three arm-gcd statements;
two generic field/primitive-pair statements; and four concrete
root-norm unit and nondivisibility consequences. Definitions are
not counted as theorem declarations.
