# Proof dependency map and precise open joins

Every arrow below names a proved implication or a stated open obligation.
Ordinary theorem status is distinct from formal verification status.

## The forward signed-tail chain

1. Actual primitive unramified Eisenstein root and n coprime to 6.
2. Normalized integer arm quotients, reconstruction, additive relation
   and pairwise coprimality (SA1; formal coprimality exposes primitivity).
3. Local depth/radical inequality and old-boundary overlap bound.
4. SA finite signed inequality with the third arm's negative credit intact.
5. Previously proved two-place LR2, positive-sector normalization and
   cutoff Y=n^(1/6): archimedean and small-prime errors tend uniformly to zero.
6. Sufficient gate: for some pair, (E_i+E_j-2R_k)_+/log c tends to zero.
7. The full positive signed large-prime cost tends to zero.
8. The previously proved reduction gives the restricted ABC estimate
   for each fixed epsilon and uniformly sufficiently large n.

Steps 1--5 and 6=>7=>8 are proved in their exact domains. Arbitrary
actual membership in step 6 is OPEN. Step 6 is only sufficient; its
failure would not by itself refute step 7 or the parent research route.
Bounded n with moving roots is not covered by this asymptotic conclusion.

## The simultaneous-norm support chain

1. Actual positive primitive seed, M=R^n and F=Q^n.
2. Exact F-M^2=ab c^2 and four-ninths identity.
3. Full geometric sum gives 9n(Q-R^2)<=4R^2 for every n>=1;
   the actual integer connection is formalized, without a sum-bound axiom.
4. For n>1 odd, the top homogeneous cyclotomic factor has only the
   possible exceptional largest prime outside 1 mod n, of depth one.
5. Conjugate-root lengths give Phi_n(Q,R^2)>=Phi_n(1)R^(2phi(n)).
6. For gcd(n,6)=1, oriented actual first-root extraction plus SA arms
   allocates all extra inert depths to a single input arm.
7. Full valuation budget at 6n, retaining kappa=maxprime(n)/Phi_n(1).
8. If 2phi(n)>n+1, combine step 3 with the even exponent gap to force
   an actual seed prime q=1 mod 6n. Prime powers give the PP specialization.

All ordinary implications 1--8 are proved with the indicated hypotheses.
The new Lean code formalizes step 2, the actual integer conclusion in
step 3, and elementary rank/arm/product arithmetic used in steps 6--8.
It does not formalize the whole prime valuation, UFD, or complex-root proof.

OPEN joins toward the global goal: control high multiplicities in the
surviving support; treat ramification and moving nonunit residuals; handle
exponent pairs without suitable common divisors; obtain the required
uniform signed/radical estimate for every actual primitive triple.
The support chain supplies necessary restrictions and a height lower
bound, with no height upper bound and no nonexistence conclusion.

## Independent directions retained

The full private-tail question, mixed Kummer covers for relatively prime
exponents, moving residual levels and actual cover-point heights remain
separate live directions. The previously proved infinite quartic-square
family prevents treating that square class as empty; only exact claims
contradicted by complete-premise counterexamples may be retired. The
current common-exponent theorem's hypotheses do not retire any of these
complementary branches.
