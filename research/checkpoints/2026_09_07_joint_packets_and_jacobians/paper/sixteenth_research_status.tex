\section{Sixteenth continuation: verified scope and remaining dependencies}
\label{jpj-status}

Standard ABC remains unproved and undisproved. The present continuation
adds the complete ordinary joint-packet argument, the rational simplicity
and rank lower bounds for the two remaining genus-two factors, a finite
quadratic Chabauty container through the first quotient, and the actual
three-phase integer bridge. Their ordinary proofs preceded implementation
and received independent internal review. This is not external peer review.

The two new Lean modules contain 27 declarations: ten for actual phase
arithmetic and joint finite capacity, and seventeen for homogeneous curve
identities, complete finite square-fibre counts, integer trace obstructions
and the resulting numerical gcds. All 27 new declarations and 45 unchanged
local dependency declarations were freshly compiled with warnings treated
as errors. Every one of the 72 declarations has an explicit axiom query;
only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}
occur. The checked compiler is Lean 4.32.0 and the exact Mathlib source pin is
\texttt{81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997}. Its compiled dependency
cache was reused; the five listed local source modules were freshly built.

The formal statements retain their mathematical inputs. In particular,
the joint-capacity theorem assumes collision divisibility, actual pair
heights, phase separation and the finite target sizes. It does not prove
the construction of the arithmetic norm-one quotient or its torsion.
The finite curve counts use the displayed quadratic pair multiplication
and add two as the number of infinity points. Good reduction, the
interpretation of those pairs as the extension field, the curve/Jacobian
trace formula, rational simplicity, Mordell--Weil rank and torsion groups
are supplied by the separately reviewed ordinary proof. The Chabauty
criterion and the Neron--Severi arguments are also ordinary inputs, not
Lean theorems in these modules.

The mathematical dependency chain has two distinct open ends. For the
analytic route, combined precision on a common-hit rectangle still gives
the product target $n^{|S|}$. It does not control the nonrectangular
incidence of different primes with different roots, the full signed
far tail, exceptional individual roots, or global ABC coverage. For the
geometric route, the existence of a finite five-adic container has not
computed that container or determined its rational survivors. Even a
complete classification of the fixed cubic branch would leave changing
exponents and residual parameters, integral lifting and uniform point
heights unresolved. None of these gaps is replaced by a formal hypothesis
and then counted as a solved conjecture.

The full verification logs and exact source hashes are recorded in the
accompanying checkpoint. Its finite replay independently regenerates
all four small-field curve certificates. Subsequent research candidates
about collective input content, actual divisor partitions and local
Jacobian closure are kept in the next continuation until their own
proof, formal-scope and publication reviews are complete. No unrefuted
parent route is abandoned.
