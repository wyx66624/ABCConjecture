# EQ1--EQ3. An explicit elliptic gate for the nontrivial cubic unit

Status: complete ordinary proof, independently reviewed by both peers. New
thirteenth-round research; the preceding publication remains frozen.
No software rank output is used in the proofs below.

## EQ1. Two degree-two elliptic quotients

The first HG quotient for g=3 and u=zeta is the smooth projective genus-
two curve H with affine equation

    y^2=P(s),
    P(s)=s^6+3s^5-3s^4-11s^3-3s^2+3s+1.          (EQ1)

Indeed the identity-unit power coordinates are
A_0=s^3-3s-1, B_0=3s(s+1); multiplication by zeta gives
A=-B_0, B=A_0+B_0, so B(A+B)=A_0(A_0+B_0)=P.

There are degree-two morphisms from H to the elliptic curves

    E:  Y^2=X^3-9X-9,
    E': Y'^2=X'^3-189X'+999,                       (EQ2)

given on their finite charts by

    X=-(2s^2+5s+2)/(s+1)^2,  Y=y/(s+1)^3,
    X'=3(2s^2-7s+2)/(s-1)^2, Y'=-9y/(s-1)^3.     (EQ3)

Moreover Jac(H) is isogenous over Q to E times E'.

Proof. For s not equal to 1 put

    t=(s+1)/(s-1),       v=y(t-1)^3.

Direct expansion gives the birational even-sextic equation

    v^2=-9t^6+99t^4-27t^2+1.                     (EQ4)

The inverse is s=(t+1)/(t-1), y=v/(t-1)^3. These formulas are chart
identities; the isomorphism extends to the smooth projective models.
The two elliptic quotient coordinates in this chart are

    X=(1/t^2-9)/4,      Y=v/(8t^3),
    X'=(33-9t^2)/4,    Y'=-9v/8.

Substitution verifies (EQ2) and yields (EQ3). The two short
Weierstrass discriminants are 11664 and 944784, respectively, and
are nonzero. The second map is the quotient by (t,v)->(-t,v), and
the first by (t,v)->(-t,-v). Each has degree two: the function fields
are generated by t^2 and the indicated invariant, and the displayed
nontrivial involution fixes this subfield. In characteristic zero
the maps extend to morphisms of smooth projective curves.

For completeness, the isogeny is arithmetic over Q. Let sigma be
(t,v)->(-t,v), and let iota be the hyperelliptic involution. The two
quotients have involutions sigma and sigma*iota. On Jac(H), iota
acts as -1: its norm/pullback sum is the pullback of a divisor class
on its quotient P^1, hence is zero. If Phi is the pair of quotient
norms and Psi the sum of the two pullbacks, the divisor identity for
each double cover, including the ramified fibres, gives

    Psi Phi=(1+sigma)+(1+sigma*iota)=[2].         (EQ5)

Multiplication by 2 is surjective. Thus Psi is surjective, and both
sides have dimension two, so Psi is an isogeny over Q. This is the
same explicit norm/pullback argument used in HG; no complex-only
group-action decomposition is promoted to an arithmetic assertion.
QED.

## EQ2. The exact rational image in the first elliptic quotient

The image of H(Q) in E(Q) under the first map of (EQ3) is exactly

    {O} union { (X,Y) in E(Q): 4X+9 is a square in Q }.   (EQ6)

Proof. Clearing the X formula gives

    (X+2)s^2+(2X+5)s+(X+2)=0.                    (EQ7)

The discriminant in s is 4X+9. Hence every finite-chart preimage has
the stated square condition. The apparent chart exceptions are as
follows. Above s=-1, P(-1)=1, so the two rational points have y=+1
and y=-1 and both map to O. The two points at s=infinity are rational
because P has leading coefficient 1; they map to (-2,+1) and
(-2,-1), respectively. The points with s=0 give the same two elliptic
images. These all satisfy (EQ6).

Conversely, suppose (X,Y) is finite and 4X+9 is a rational square.
If X=-2 then Y=+1 or -1, and the points s=0, y=Y are preimages.
If X is not -2, the quadratic (EQ7) has a rational root s. It cannot
be -1, since substituting s=-1 in its left side gives -1. Define
y=Y(s+1)^3. The polynomial identity behind (EQ3) implies y^2=P(s),
so this is a rational point of H. The double-discriminant case
4X+9=0 does not occur on E(Q): at X=-9/4 the right side of its
equation is -9/64. Finally O already has the two rational preimages
described above. This proves the exact image, with no lost projective
or zero-denominator branch. QED.

This is still only one quotient gate for the actual seed problem.
An actual point of D_(3,zeta) must lift to H and must also have the
second HG square root at the same source s, with 0<A(s)/B(s)<1/3.
Arbitrary elliptic points, or points on different hyperelliptic
quotients at different source parameters, do not supply such a seed.

## EQ3. Infinite rank contribution, and a strict lifting gate

Both E and E' have rational points of infinite order. Consequently
the Mordell--Weil rank of Jac(H)(Q) is at least two. In particular
one cannot justify a rank-less-than-genus argument for H.

Proof. Take P=(-2,1) on E and P'=(6,9) on E'. Direct doubling gives

    2P=(25/4,-107/8),       2P'=(33/4,9/8).

For a short integral Weierstrass equation with odd coefficients a,b,
the doubling abscissa is

    x(2Q)=(x^4-2a*x^2-8b*x+a^2)/(4(x^3+a*x+b)).

If v_2(x)=s<0, the unique smallest valuations in the numerator and
denominator are respectively 4s and 2+3s. Thus v_2(x(2Q))=s-2.
Both displayed doubled points start with valuation -2. Their repeated
doublings therefore have distinct abscissas with valuations -2,-4,-6,...,
and each original point has infinite order. Both curves in (EQ2)
have odd a,b, so the argument applies to each. The Q-isogeny of EQ1
and the Mordell--Weil theorem give rank at least one plus one. No
claim that these points generate the full groups is made. QED.

The square gate in EQ2 is genuinely restrictive even on the first
elliptic curve: P lifts, whereas 2P does not, because 4*(25/4)+9=34
is not a rational square. This is an exact example, not an assertion
about all nontrivial multiples of P. Higher descents and simultaneous
square conditions remain available; neither the genus-two curve nor
the two nonidentity unit branches have been proved empty.

## Separate software observations, not proof dependencies

PARI/GP 2.15.4 returned ellrank(E)=[1,1,0,[[-2,1]]] in a diagnostic
call. The two quotient models also returned matching lower and upper
rank values 1. These outputs have not been converted into independent
ordinary upper-bound certificates and are not used above.

The bounded nine-curve search in genus_two_probe.gp, replayed and
checked by replay_genus_two_probe.py, finds 24 affine points across
the nine models for numerator/denominator bounds 1000. All occur at
s=0 or -1, and none gives the actual positive common-source locus.
The wrapper independently exhausts a smaller bound 80 using exact
Fraction arithmetic and integer square roots. This finite information
does not determine all rational points.

API sources actually consulted: the official PARI/GP reference pages
for Hyperelliptic curves (hyperellratpoints) and Elliptic curves
(ellrank); the installed 2.15.4 help for hyperellratpoints was checked.
Standard proof inputs are the reviewed HG divisor norm/pullback
identity, the smooth-projective extension theorem, and the Mordell--
Weil theorem. None is a new Lean formalization in this note.
