# GD primary-source evidence

The following author-hosted primary texts were actually opened during
the independent_route review on 2026-09-07.

- J. S. Milne, Algebraic Number Theory,
  https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf:
  Example 2.41 (printed 39) gives the integral basis and discriminant for
  quadratic fields. Example 3.44 (printed 63) lists the three quadratic
  factorization types and their residue degrees; Example 3.45 (printed
  64) treats Gaussian splitting. They apply to d=-1 and d=3, giving
  discriminants -4 and 12. The displayed norm-Euclidean and fundamental-unit
  calculations in GD supply the additional facts used in these two fields.

- J. S. Milne, Elliptic Curves, second edition,
  https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf:
  Chapter IV opening finite-basis theorem (printed 105; PDF index 109)
  states finite generation over a number field. This is the standard
  input used after the exact quotient has been proved, not a black-box
  rank calculation.

The already reviewed SS geometric input is Milne, Chapter II,
Proposition 1.5 (printed 49), together with Stacks 53.2.2 and 53.2.6.
The exact isogeny chart identities and the complete delta chord-law proof
are given in GD itself. This source record does not assert any generator
completeness or a rational-point classification of SS.
