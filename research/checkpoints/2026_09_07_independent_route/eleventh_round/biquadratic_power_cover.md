# BK1--BK3. The simultaneous Gaussian and Eisenstein power cover

Status: complete ordinary proof; root, critical_bottleneck, and
adversarial_audit independently reviewed BK1--BK3 and passed. This is a new
geometric construction following GE, not a change to the reviewed IL/CI
or earlier DC curves. In particular, this cover is not asserted to be
birational to DC. Larger genus alone gives no exclusion at varying
exponent.

Let K=Q(i,sqrt(-3)), let zeta=(1+sqrt(-3))/2, and choose

    alpha=(sqrt(3)-i)/2,  bar(alpha)=(sqrt(3)+i)/2,
    alpha^2=bar(zeta),  bar(alpha)^2=zeta.

The bar denotes complex conjugation. This is a fixed degree-four field.
For a variable t put

    a0=t^2-1, b0=2t+1, c0=t^2+2t,
    U0=t^2+t+1, A0=a0*b0,
    E=A0+zeta*U0^2, G=A0+i U0*c0.                    (BK1)

The identities c0^2-A0=U0^2 and

    E*bar(E)=G*bar(G)=F(a0,b0)

follow from GE. Here conjugation acts on coefficients, leaving t fixed.

## BK1. Four disjoint quadratic factors

Define

    B1=c0-alpha U0,       B2=c0+alpha U0,
    B3=c0-bar(alpha) U0,  B4=c0+bar(alpha) U0.

Then

    E=B1 B2, bar(E)=B3 B4,
    G=B1 B4, bar(G)=B2 B3.                           (BK2)

Indeed A0=c0^2-U0^2, 1-zeta=bar(zeta),
bar(alpha)-alpha=i, and alpha*bar(alpha)=1.

All four B_j have degree two, each has two simple roots, and the eight
roots are distinct. None is the point at infinity. To prove this, every
B_j is c0-s U0 for s in {alpha,-alpha,bar(alpha),-bar(alpha)}. These
four constants are distinct and none equals one. Its leading
coefficient is 1-s and its discriminant is

    (2-s)^2+4s(1-s)=4-3s^2.

Since s^2 is zeta or bar(zeta), this discriminant is nonzero; its norm
from Q(sqrt(-3)) to Q is 13. Two such quadratics sharing a root would
force U0=c0=0. But U0-c0=1-t, and U0(1)=3, so they have no common
zero, even projectively. The leading coefficients are nonzero, excluding
infinity. This proves the complete branch inventory.

## BK2. Exact degree, component count, and genus

Let g>=2 be an integer and fix constants etaE,etaG in K*. Consider

    Y^g=etaE*E/bar(E),
    Z^g=etaG*G/bar(G).                               (BK3)

Take the smooth projective normalizations of these equations. Work over
an algebraic closure when counting geometric components. Put
delta=gcd(g,2). The normalization of (BK3) has exactly delta geometric
components. Each component has degree g^2/delta over P1_t and genus

    1+(3g^2-4g)/delta.                              (BK4)

In particular, for odd g the curve is geometrically connected, has
degree g^2, and genus 1+3g^2-4g. For even g it has two geometric
components, each of degree g^2/2; ignoring this factor of two would be
incorrect.

Proof. On each pair of roots of B1,B2,B3,B4 the two rational functions
E/bar(E) and G/bar(G) have respective valuation vectors

    (1,1), (1,-1), (-1,-1), (-1,1).                 (BK5)

Thus for exponents u,v modulo g, a multiplicative relation that is a
g-th power requires u+v=0 and u-v=0 modulo g. There is one solution
when g is odd and exactly two when g is even. These are sufficient as
well as necessary: the nontrivial even relation uses u=v=g/2 and

    (E/bar(E))*(G/bar(G))=(B1/B3)^2.

Over the algebraic closure constants have g-th roots. Kummer theory
therefore gives the subgroup size g^2/delta and that same function-field
degree for each component. Equivalently, the full equations have total
generic degree g^2 and split into delta isomorphic conjugate geometric
components, since precisely delta multiplicative relations remain.

There are exactly the eight branch points of BK1. At each, one function
has valuation 1 or -1 and the other also has valuation 1 or -1. Units
have g-th roots in the completed algebraically closed residue field,
so the local extension is generated by one g-th root of a uniformizer.
The inertia order on each component is exactly g. At infinity both
functions have order zero and are units; there is no ramification.
The same applies at all other points. For d=g^2/delta, characteristic-zero
Riemann--Hurwitz gives

    2 genus-2=-2d+8d(1-1/g),

which is (BK4).

For the pure unit constants below, the two even components are also
defined over K. In fact etaE*etaG is in mu6, whose square roots lie in
mu12 contained in K, and the two components are distinguished by

    (YZ)^(g/2)/(B1/B3)=+sqrt(etaE etaG)
                         or -sqrt(etaE etaG).

For arbitrary constants in K* this last arithmetic component assertion
need not hold; only the geometric count above was asserted.

## BK3. Every actual square-first pure-second seed lifts

Let a,b be positive coprime integers with M=U^2 for a positive integer U
and F=Q^g, Q>1, g>=2. There is a K-rational point on one of the at most
six curves (BK3) with etaE in mu3 and etaG in mu2. Its base parameter is

    t=(a+U)/b>1.                                    (BK6)

The point is nonbranch and hence lifts to the normalization. If g is
odd, the Gaussian unit can be absorbed and only the three etaE choices
are needed. If gcd(g,6)=1, both constants can be absorbed: every such
seed lifts to the single curve (BK3) with etaE=etaG=1, of degree g^2
and genus 1+3g^2-4g.

Proof. Since U^2-(a-b)^2=3ab>0, one has U>|a-b| and therefore t>1.
Writing x=a/b, the number t=x+sqrt(x^2+x+1) satisfies
t^2-2xt-x-1=0. Hence a0/b0=x and b0=2t+1>0. With
k=b0/b>0 one obtains

    (a0,b0,c0)=k(a,b,c),    U0=kU,
    E=k^2(ab+U^2 zeta),    G=k^2(ab+i Uc).           (BK7)

The sign in U0=kU is positive since U0=t^2+t+1>0. The exact actual
oriented decompositions in the two quadratic UFDs give

    ab+U^2 zeta=uE*wE^g,     N(wE)=Q,
    ab+i Uc=uG*wG^g,        N(wG)=Q.

Consequently Y=wE/bar(wE), Z=wG/bar(wG) satisfy (BK3), with
etaE=uE^(-2) in mu3 and etaG=uG^(-2) in mu2. The positive rational
factor k^2 cancels from both ratios. None of E,bar(E),G,bar(G) vanishes
at this actual t, as their norms are k^4 F>0. This is therefore a
smooth nonbranch K-point and belongs to the normalization.

For odd g, raising to g is a bijection of mu2, allowing the constant
etaG to be absorbed into Z. If also 3 does not divide g it is a bijection
of mu3, allowing etaE to be absorbed into Y. These are actual changes of
coordinates in K, with no varying cyclotomic extension needed.

## Dependencies and the remaining problem

Actual first square -> rational conic parameter (BK6) -> simultaneous
primitive Gaussian and Eisenstein pure powers -> the explicit finite
unit-twist lift (BK3) -> Kummer relations and eight-branch genus count.
The two coordinate covers are not presumed independent: their exact
relation module was computed, and the even-exponent defect retained.

The curve is a further cover required by actual points, not a
birational re-description of the older DC curve and not a pointwise
inverse for arbitrary K-points. Its additional compatibility may be
useful for a fixed-exponent rational-point calculation. No theorem
here supplies such a calculation or a height bound uniform in g.
For even g the positive seed locus is already excluded by CI2; the
geometric even components are nevertheless present. The odd second
exponent and ramified first branches have not been excluded.

The geometric inputs are the same characteristic-zero Kummer and
Riemann--Hurwitz facts used and source-reviewed in DC/RD. There is no
new claim of Lean formalization or reliance on uncomputed ranks.
