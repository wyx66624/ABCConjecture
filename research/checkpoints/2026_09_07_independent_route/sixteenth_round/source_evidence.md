# Primary source inspection for QS and QH

The author actually opened the following primary documents during this
sixteenth-round work:

- Milne AVc, I Proposition 10.1 (printed42/PDF48): ground-field isogeny
  complement; IV Theorem3.5 and Corollary3.6 (printed141--142/PDF147--148):
  prime-to-residue-characteristic torsion specialization and the good
  reduction criterion for rational Tate modules.
  https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AVc.pdf
- Milne JVs, Theorem11.1, Corollary11.4 and the proof of Corollary12.3:
  curve counts/Jacobian Frobenius and good reduction of the Jacobian of a
  smooth proper curve.
  https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/JVs.pdf
- Balakrishnan--Dogra, Quadratic Chabauty and rational points I,
  arXiv1601.00388: printed3 setup, Section2's base field/good-prime/
  rational-basepoint conventions, Lemmas3.1--3.2 on printed12. The Picard
  number is explicitly over the ground field. Lemma3.2 is the finiteness
  statement for r<g+rho-1. Theorem1.2's explicit height formula has extra
  r=g and p-adic finite-index assumptions; those are not claimed here.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.00388

No secondary search synopsis substitutes for these opened statements.
Finite small-field enumeration and divisor computations in our notes are
separate explicit calculations; the standard geometric inputs remain
ordinary mathematics rather than new Lean formalizations.
