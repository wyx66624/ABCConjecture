\section{Actual private valuations and complete finite depth budgets in Lean}
\label{apd-section}

The finite formalization starts with actual products and actual index sets.
Let $I$ be a finite set, $G$ a commutative monoid, and $a_i\in G$.
Suppose homomorphisms $v_i:G\to(\mathbb Z,+)$ satisfy
\[
 v_i(a_j)=0\quad(j\ne i),\qquad d_i=v_i(a_i)\ne0.
\]
In Lean the additive target is expressed as
\texttt{Multiplicative Int}, so these are ordinary multiplicative
monoid homomorphisms. The integers $d_i$ may have either sign.

\begin{lemma}[Actual multiset injectivity]\label{apd-private}
The map taking a finite multiset of indices to the product of its
actual elements $a_i$ is injective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a multiset $s$, write $c_i(s)$ for its multiplicity at $i$.
Commutativity gives
\[
 \prod_{i\in s}a_i=\prod_{i\in I}a_i^{c_i(s)},\qquad
 v_i\Bigl(\prod_{j\in s}a_j\Bigr)=c_i(s)d_i.
\]
Equal products therefore imply $c_i(s)d_i=c_i(t)d_i$ for every $i$.
Cancel the nonzero integer $d_i$ to obtain equality of all multiplicities,
and hence of the multisets. No tuple-injectivity assumption is needed.
\end{proof}

Give each actual index a natural depth $e_i$, and define
$I_h=\{i\in I:e_i\ge h\}$. Private witnesses restrict to every $I_h$.
Let $H_h$ be a finite commutative monoid with $|H_h|\le3n$, and suppose
$\phi_h:I_h\to H_h$. At a specified positive integer moment $\nu$, assume that equality
of the mapped products of two $\nu$-multisets implies equality of
their original products in $G$. Lemma~\ref{apd-private} then gives
\begin{equation}\label{apd-count}
 \binom{|I_h|+\nu-1}{\nu}\le3n.
\end{equation}
This counts actual multisets with repetitions, including the empty-index
case when $\nu>0$. In the formal library the cardinality is that of
\texttt{Sym} applied to the actual depth subtype.

\begin{proposition}[The finite private-root depth bridge]\label{apd-budget}
Let $n>324$ and $C$ be natural numbers, put $r=\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor+1$,
and assume $e_i\le C$ for every $i$. Suppose the two finite targets
and actual-product rigidity assumptions above are supplied at
$(h,\nu)=(4,2)$ and $(2r,r)$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{apd-excess}
 \sum_{i\in I}(e_i-3)_+\le10n+3C.
\end{equation}
For $w\ge0$ with $Cw\le3nL$, one also has
\begin{equation}\label{apd-weighted}
 w\sum_{i\in I}(e_i-3)_+\le10nw+9nL.
\end{equation}
If $B,s>0$, $w\le2s$ and $L\le2s$, division by $Bns$ bounds
the left side of \eqref{apd-weighted} after normalization by $38/B$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The two-multiset instance of \eqref{apd-count} gives $|I_4|^2\le6n$.
Also $n\le r^2\le4n$. If $|I_{2r}|\ge4$, then
\[
 3n\ge\binom{|I_{2r}|+r-1}{r}
 \ge\binom{r+3}{3}>\frac{r^3}{6}\ge\frac{n^{3/2}}6>3n,
\]
a contradiction. Hence $|I_{2r}|\le3$. For each index separately,
\[
 (e_i-3)_+\le 2r\,\mathbf1_{e_i\ge4}
                  +C\,\mathbf1_{e_i\ge2r}.
\]
Summing counts each actual index once, even when different indices
have the same depth. The first contribution is at most
$2\sqrt{4n}\sqrt{6n}<10n$ and the second at most $3C$.
Multiplication by the nonnegative weight and the stated height bound
prove \eqref{apd-weighted}. Finally its right side is at most $38ns$;
all three factors in the denominator are positive.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The precise formal scope.}
There are 37 new declarations in four modules.
\texttt{PrivateValuation\allowbreak Multisets} contributes eight actual
product, private-valuation, symmetric-product and finite-count theorems.
\texttt{UniformMoment\allowbreak Arithmetic} contributes 15 numerical,
actual-list, complete-layer and normalization theorems.
\texttt{ActualUniform\allowbreak DepthBudget} contributes eight theorems
on restricted private witnesses, actual depth subtypes, finite sums,
and the complete connection in Proposition~\ref{apd-budget}.
The formal weighted sums explicitly perform natural-number truncated
subtraction before casting the complete sum to the reals.

Finally, \texttt{CubicUnit\allowbreak LocalArithmetic} contributes six
theorems on the actual integer sextic in the local obstruction. A
kernel-checked table modulo $27$ implies that an integer square value
forces both homogeneous coordinates to be divisible by three.
It therefore excludes every primitive integer coordinate pair.
The finite table uses \texttt{decide}, with no native computation axiom.

All four sources were freshly compiled together with Lean 4.32.0 and
Mathlib revision \texttt{81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997}.
All 37 explicit axiom queries passed; their union consists only of
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
The fixed Mathlib compiled cache was reused. The ordinary proofs and
independent internal review preceded the corresponding implementation.

The final private-root theorem derives both binomial count premises
from the supplied private witnesses and actual product rigidity.
It does not construct the arithmetic ideal valuations, finite torsion
groups, their lifts, or the height cap. The formal natural depths remain
a supplied function on a finite index type. The ideal, sieve, density,
$\mathbb Q_3$-geometric and elliptic arguments retain their separately
reviewed ordinary-proof status. None of these declarations proves $abc$.
