\section{Thirteenth continuation: dependencies and remaining obstacles}
\label{thirteenth-status}

The analytic and geometric branches have advanced independently.
Uniform determinant control permits every finite moment at the same
lower prime cutoff $8B$. Private norm support supplies an actual
multiplicatively independent domain. The complete layer budget reaches
$Bn(\log n)^{1-\delta}$ on a domain of asymptotic relative size at least
one half. Coloring actual norm support gives the further tradeoff
$(5-\kappa)/8$ at cutoff $Bn^\kappa(\log n)^{1-\delta}$,
for fixed $0<\kappa\le1$. All positive layers of these intervals are paid.
The argument preserves the signed cost beyond their upper endpoint.

The geometric local obstruction excludes the identity-unit curves for
all odd exponents divisible by three, including their projective points.
This is a proved obstruction for that specified branch. The two
nonidentity units remain live. For one cubic nonidentity quotient,
two explicit elliptic maps give a rational product isogeny, an exact
square condition for lifting, and a proved Jacobian rank lower bound of
two. Mordell--Weil is used only in its standard finite-generation form;
see Milne's Theorem~16.7, printed page~69\footnote{J. S. Milne,
\emph{Abelian Varieties},
\url{https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AVc.pdf}.}.
The bounded rational-point search and software rank observations
are distinct from these proofs.

The main unresolved dependencies are the full unbounded signed packet,
roots outside the proved norm-support domains, and the positive
common-source rational locus for the remaining geometric unit classes.
Neither fixed genus at one exponent nor finite searches give a uniform
height bound as the exponent or residual coefficients vary.
The far-tail incidence, norm-smooth complement, higher descent and
moving-residual routes are retained. A local obstruction retires only
its precisely stated branch, and a rank obstruction only the specific
rank-less-than-genus criterion.

The 37 finite formal declarations are compiled and audited separately
from the ordinary analytic and geometric arguments. The proof chain
still has the substantive open dependencies just listed. There is no
complete proof or disproof of $abc$, and no unconditional Lean theorem
asserting it. Subsequent adaptive-precision and owner-concentration
candidates are maintained separately for the next continuation.
