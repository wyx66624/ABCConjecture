\section{Formal arithmetic for direct power tests and denominator classes}
\label{sec:formal-support-power}

The module \texttt{LocalPowerArithmetic.lean} contains fifteen declarations
supporting the preceding ordinary proofs. It imports the existing
Eisenstein pair norm and defines the second norm by that actual norm,
\[
 M_1(a,b)=N\bigl(ab,N(a,b)\bigr),
\]
then proves its quartic expansion. Its principal quantified statement says
that for every positive natural number $L$ and every natural height bound
$T$, there exist positive integers $a,b$ with $a+b>T$ such that
\[
 \gcd(a,b)=\gcd\bigl(ab,N(a,b)\bigr)=1
\]
and, simultaneously for every integer $m\mid L$ and natural exponents
$e,f$,
\[
 m\mid N(a,b)-1^e,\qquad m\mid M_1(a,b)-1^f.
\]
The witness is $a=L(T+1)$, $b=a+1$. Thus this is an actual unbounded
primitive family, with the same seed working for every stated test.
Taking $L$ to be a common multiple gives the direct finite-modulus
obstruction. The formal theorem does not assert that either norm is a
perfect power as an integer or that the seeds have high ABC quality.

Further declarations prove the two consecutive-coordinate polynomial
identities, their exact linearizations at the simple roots modulo seven
and sixty-seven, the coprime derivatives, and the exact arithmetic
supporting the residual depth-one conditions at thirteen and thirty-one.
These are the arithmetic inputs to the ordinary Hensel and CRT argument;
the infinite family of actual Eisenstein power extractions is not itself
claimed as formalized by these declarations.

For the direct-lift denominator, the module checks the integer valuation
implication
\[
 h>0,\quad 0\le d<h,\quad d+hv\ge0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad v\ge0.
\]
It also proves, for natural $h,z$,
\[
 h\mid4z\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \frac{h}{\gcd(h,4)}\mid z,
 \qquad
 4\frac{h}{\gcd(h,4)}=h\frac4{\gcd(h,4)}.
\]
The first equivalence is stated at the nonnegative valuation level in
Lean. The passage through rational valuations, rational power-class
groups, the full lifting bijection, and the higher-genus finiteness
argument remain ordinary proofs with their stated external dependencies.

A fresh standalone Lake build with Lean~4.32.0, warnings treated as
errors, verifies the fifteen new declarations and recompiles all
twenty-nine declarations in the unchanged norm dependency. Every
declaration has a printed-axiom inventory; only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound} occur. The checkpoint
records the exact source hashes and reproducible finite arithmetic
replays. This is scoped formal verification, with no claim to a full
repository build, formalized geometric or probability theorems, or a
solution of ABC.
