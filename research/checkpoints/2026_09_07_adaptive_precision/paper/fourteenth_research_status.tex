\section{Fourteenth continuation: the remaining arithmetic and geometric gates}
\label{fourteenth-status}

Adaptive precision removes the upper-prime restriction from the individual
complete-cost estimate. Two actual largest-depth roots contain the high
layers, and outside them the per-prime cost is smaller by a power of $n$.
For very large primes, simultaneous integer divisibility supplies an
actual arm graph with at most two endpoints per prime, bounded degree
and a finite matching decomposition. Its prime labels and total owner
weight are still uncontrolled at the strength needed for the full signed
gate. The abstract weighted example only limits the displayed finite
inequalities; it is not an arithmetic counterfamily.

An independent Selberg cofactor argument proves that the actual private
norm domain has relative size at least $1/2+\delta_0$ for some absolute
$\delta_0>0$. Its uniform endpoint bounds are retained. Combining this
with the established moment window enlarges the proved domain while
leaving the same far signed term. The remaining smooth-support and
exceptional roots are separate targets, and the full-block mass argument
is never iterated on an arbitrary discarded subset.

The surviving cubic geometric branch now has a reversible same-source
quartic equation over one fixed elliptic curve, exact positive-domain
inequalities and an integral prime-allocation constraint. The two elliptic
quotients are rationally three-isogenous, so the genus-two Jacobian is
isogenous to a square of one elliptic curve. These identities do not
solve the simultaneous rational-point problem. Higher descent and
uniform control of exponents, heights and moving residuals remain live.

At the user's request, the research group has also located the official
open-source Fermat formalization\footnote{Anthropic,
\emph{Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean 4},
\url{https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem}, source commit
\texttt{aa2d8b34692b16c70f699536de0d8e75b9a3e9ef}.}.
It exports the complete natural-number theorem and a guarded derivation
of Mathlib's Fermat statement. Its stated toolchain is Lean 4.33.1 with
Mathlib 4.33.0. In a separate directory, two unmodified official source
files for the vanishing of weight-two cusp forms on $\Gamma_0(2)$
have been freshly compiled with the present Lean 4.32.0 and pinned
Mathlib cache. An actual bridge calls the official solution on Mathlib's
cusp-form type; both explicit axiom queries have only the standard
three axioms. The original sources emit two deprecation warnings.
This verifies that particular dependency's local reuse. The complete
Fermat import and the project's independent full-kernel replays have
not been rerun locally; the published verification records retain
their official-source provenance. The 35 new local declarations
above do not depend on an unverified Fermat import.
Exact applicable established theorems may be reused; excluding pure
equal-exponent powers alone does not estimate the near-power signed tail.

All new ordinary arguments have independent internal review, and the
finite cores have the explicit formal verification just described.
The open global gates remain substantive. No complete proof or disproof
of $abc$, and no gap-free formal proof of either, is claimed.
