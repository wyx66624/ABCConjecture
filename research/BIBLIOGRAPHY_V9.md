# Primary references for version 9

- Riemann--Hurwitz for separable morphisms of smooth projective curves: used in
  the three-point boundary-complexity theorem.
- B. Mazur, *Rational isogenies of prime degree*; M. A. Kenku's completion of
  the rational cyclic-isogeny degree classification: used only for the finite
  degree list in the rational-isogeny amplifier no-go theorem.
- Knudsen--Mumford determinant functor and determinant of cohomology: used in
  the good-place integral determinant theorem.
- Standard relative Picard identification for elliptic curves: the class of a
  degree-zero divisor is the group-law sum of its points.

All uses are theorem-level and independent of IUT or ATS.
