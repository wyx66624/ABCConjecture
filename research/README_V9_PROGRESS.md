# Version 9 progress summary

This branch contains a theorem-first mathematical package. No unconditional
`ABCConjecture` closure is claimed.

## New positive results

- general `k`-power-core inequality and the square--cube horizon;
- good-place unitness for the cyclic-line determinant-of-cohomology complex.

## New no-go theorems

- exact three-point rational-map amplification is impossible beyond the six
  anharmonic degree-one maps;
- rational cyclic isogenies over `Q` provide only boundedly many outputs and
  cannot be a polynomial exceptional-set amplifier.

## Next Lean targets

- power-core arithmetic;
- finite incidence double counting;
- scalar torsion-line cancellation identities;
- a function-field/Riemann--Hurwitz proof of the three-point obstruction;
- the finite-free determinant-unit abstraction.

The global parabolic specialization theorem, the IUT normed source theorem,
and any final proof or disproof remain open.
