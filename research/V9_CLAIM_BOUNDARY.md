# Claim boundary

The results in this branch are unconditional mathematical reductions or
explicit no-go theorems for particular mechanisms. They do not imply the abc
conjecture by themselves.

The following remain unproved:

1. a uniform radical-height bound for the varying conic/cubic core equations;
2. an exceptional-point amplifier satisfying the incidence exponent criterion;
3. the Legendre parabolic arithmetic specialization/maximal-slope theorem;
4. a complete normed IUT/ATS source comparison or an alternative source
   inequality;
5. an infinite fixed-epsilon counterexample family.

No file should be cited as an unconditional proof or disproof of abc until one
of these global routes is closed without importing an equivalent hypothesis.
