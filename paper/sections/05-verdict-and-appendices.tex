
A disproof would require an $\eps_0>0$ and infinitely many primitive triples with
\[
 \log c>(1+\eps_0)\log\rad(abc)+O(1),
\]
or equivalently unbounded quality strictly above $1+\eps_0$.  None of the failures identified above produces such triples.  They are failures of proposed proofs, not counterexamples to the conjecture.

\begin{proposition}[Proof failure does not imply conjecture failure]
The Scholze--Stix scaling obstruction and the gaps in version 6.0 invalidate the corresponding deduction of $abc$, but they imply neither $abc$ nor its negation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
They establish that certain premises do not entail the desired numerical inequality.  No arithmetic triple violating \cref{thm:abc} is constructed.  In classical logic, refuting an argument for a proposition is not a proof of the proposition's negation.
\end{proof}

\section{Conclusion}

The audit rejects the statement that the version-6 manuscript is a complete proof of the $abc$ conjecture.  Its decisive source is assumed, and several additional identifications are stronger than the arguments supplied.

The audit also preserves and strengthens important unconditional work.  In particular, the complete forbidden-set prime theorem shows that the auxiliary-prime problem can be solved with
\[
 \log\ell=O(\log\log c),
\]
without relying on a global exceptional-prime theorem, once the standard two-place Picard--Lefschetz monodromy is used.  This is a genuine advance in the dependency graph, but \cref{counter:blindness} proves that large mod-$\ell$ image alone cannot control the discriminant exponents.

The shortest currently visible route to a complete proof is therefore not ``more prime selection''.  It is a proof of the normed, weight-preserving comparison \cref{hyp:normed-rosetta,crit:repair}, together with an upper estimate for precisely the same locus and a uniform global height passage.  Alternatively, an independent method must produce a new height--conductor inequality strong enough to replace those statements.  No such theorem, and no counterexample to $abc$, is proved in this paper.

\section{Checklist for auditing any future claimed proof}

A future manuscript claiming $abc$ should provide affirmative answers to all of the following.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{A\arabic*.},leftmargin=3.4em]
\item Is every source theorem stated as a theorem rather than a hypothesis, and is its proof independent of $abc$/Szpiro/Vojta?
\item Are all local values embedded in one explicitly normed ambient space?
\item Are the $j^2$ weights visible before and after every comparison?
\item Are tensor norms genuine cross norms for the completed tensor product used?
\item Is the possible-image locus in the lower estimate exactly the locus estimated above?
\item Are Ind1--Ind3 represented by actual maps or relations with the required measure inequalities?
\item Does the auxiliary prime avoid every bad residue characteristic and every q-order?
\item Are all constants uniform in the order $\forall\eps\,\exists C_\eps\,\forall(a,b,c)$?
\item Is the compactly bounded reduction proved rather than silently applied globally?
\item Does the proof still produce a nontrivial coefficient after every normalization is made consistent?
\end{enumerate}

\section{A local no-go family for finite-level representation methods}

The family in \cref{counter:blindness} has a useful extension.  For any finite set of auxiliary primes $S$, choose two positive integers $N,N'$ congruent modulo
\[
 M=\prod_{\ell\in S}\ell.
\]
Then the tame inertia actions on $E_{p^N}[\ell]$ and $E_{p^{N'}}[\ell]$ agree for every $\ell\in S$, while the discriminant orders differ by $|N-N'|$.  Hence no method using only finitely many fixed residual representations can uniformly recover the local height.  A successful representation-theoretic proof must choose levels depending quantitatively on the point or use integral $\ell$-adic data with a uniform global estimate.
