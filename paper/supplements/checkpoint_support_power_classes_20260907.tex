\section{Signed support and higher-power arithmetic classes}
\label{sec:support-power-checkpoint}

The fourth continuation keeps the forward signed-tail problem and the
independent higher-power geometry separate while identifying a precise
connection between their arithmetic supports. On the forward side,
conditioning on the actual supported primes retains the negative
contributions of depths one and two. An exact relative-entropy identity,
with an elementary uniform height error, replaces the unnecessarily strong
positive-excess target by a support-dependent allowance. The required
arithmetic upper bound for that allowance remains open.

On the geometric side, a finite Kummer descent proves finiteness of
primitive positive seeds for every fixed higher exponent and residual
power class. It removes the denominator-class restriction from a direct
affine lift by using integral primitivity. Its actual-profile consequences
force residual support or exponent escape, without supplying a bound
uniform in the exponent. A separate construction realizes arbitrary
specified simultaneous extraction exponents with nonsquare residuals.
A separate consecutive-seed family shows why a fixed finite list of
direct norm-residue tests cannot exclude the whole branch.

The ordinary proofs were independently reviewed before their arithmetic
pieces were formalized. The new Lean module verifies an unbounded actual
primitive family passing all direct tests whose moduli divide a specified
integer, together with polynomial, primitive-coordinate, and denominator
arithmetic. It does not formalize Hensel lifting, the finite Kummer
descent, Faltings's theorem, or the entropy estimates. Neither the actual
signed allowance nor the uniform moving-exponent radical gate has been
proved. Standard $abc$ remains unproved and undisproved here.
