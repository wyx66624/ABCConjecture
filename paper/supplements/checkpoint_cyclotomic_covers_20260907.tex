\section{Actual rank packets and varying-exponent covers}
\label{sec:cyclotomic-covers-checkpoint}

The fifth continuation attacks the arithmetic content left open by the
support-entropy and fixed-class reductions. On a homogeneous actual
Eisenstein orbit, integer cyclotomic factors isolate each prime's first
rank and its later lifting tower. The resulting packet bound has totient
weight, whose sum over the divisors is the exponent itself. It proves a
specified sparse-rank subclass without a divisor-count loss. A separate
actual construction places a deep prime at the maximal rank beyond the
moving cutoff, refuting automatic sparsity of ranks merely containing a
deep prime. It does not determine the sign of the complete rank packet.

The independent geometric direction works in a fixed splitting field
while the extraction exponent varies. Ideal allocations and the unit
lattice give an explicit bound for the number of covers, including the
cost of choosing a moving small residual, and a bound for their coefficient
heights. Some unit power classes necessarily retain a linear height cost.
The number of possible covers supplies no height bound for their individual
points. The actual-point and moving-exponent issues therefore remain open.

The ordinary proofs were independently reviewed before formalization.
Twenty-one new Lean declarations verify a general actual Lucas recurrence
and its full solution, together with exact bad-prime support statements
for every primitive integer second norm. The full cyclotomic, totient,
number-field and geometric arguments remain ordinary proofs with their
stated dependencies. Neither the unrestricted signed tail nor the
two-step radical threshold is resolved; standard $abc$ remains unproved
and undisproved.
