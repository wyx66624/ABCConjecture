\section{Seventh continuation: actual boundary classes and global escape}

Standard ABC remains unproved and undisproved. This continuation combines
several distinct advances while retaining their exact dependencies and
unresolved gates. An actual interval sieve extends the controlled prime
window beyond the length of the root-parameter block on specified exponent
subclasses. It still leaves exceptional roots and primes above that window.
Effective three-chart Diophantine bounds force hypothetical power seeds
to escape every fixed affine slice. Elementary square gaps give explicit
bounds in a narrower class.

The modular route now identifies the specified non-CM boundary descent
using characteristic-zero modularity and its actual conductor before
applying the complete newspace enumeration. Congruence to any coefficient
embedding of this boundary orbit forces a pure power and restricts its
moving prime support; it does not construct such a congruence. An independent
rational two-isogeny descent completely classifies the actual residual
square class thirteen. The integral map and actual square-axis inequalities
have scoped Lean verification; the elliptic group and modular theorems
remain ordinary proofs with explicit external inputs.

The class-thirteen result closes precisely that even-power residual class.
Other residual classes, odd prime exponents, moving affine slices and the
full signed prime tail remain active. Finite local shadows, generic cover
counts and sparse allowed prime support are not promoted to a global
nonexistence argument.
