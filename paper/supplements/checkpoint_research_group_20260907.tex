\section{A parallel research continuation and its verification boundaries}
\label{sec:research-group-20260907}
This continuation separates three research directions and subjects their ordinary
proofs to review by a different research agent before integration. The positive
direction enlarges disjoint exponent blocks to generators that may share the
same oriented prime factors. It provides a uniform angle and small-prime estimate
and an unconditional balance conclusion on the resulting class. The signed
large-prime estimate required to obtain standard $abc$ on that class remains an
explicit hypothesis.

The two independent directions establish precise boundaries. A normalized
infinite family makes the actual relative $abc$ defect under the cubic power
map arbitrarily large; this does not imply that its absolute defect is
unbounded. A geometric argument classifies globally regular maps from a finite
product of three-punctured projective lines to one such line, and identifies a
new boundary divisor when genuinely mixed maps are allowed. Neither result
retires its parent arithmetic or geometric program.

The proofs in the next three sections are ordinary mathematical arguments.
Their companion Lean files cover actual integer-pair exponent reconstruction,
normalized polynomial-root escape and explicitly conditional numerical
amplification, and the integer rectangle identities underlying the geometric
argument. They do not formalize the cited analytic inputs, the complete
geometric classification, the general radical compression lemma, or standard
$abc$. Fresh local compiler and axiom records specify their exact scope in the
checkpoint verification directory. Independent agent review here is distinct
from external peer review.

\paragraph{The next obligations.}
The shared-generator route must still control the signed large-prime tail on
its new class and address profiles outside that class. The geometric successor
must estimate the arithmetic cost of an unavoidable new boundary divisor.
The power route must retain the seed's actual arithmetic credit. Existing
IUT, Pell, Mersenne, packet, and valuation-incidence gates remain open unless a
complete-premise theorem explicitly decides them. Standard $abc$ is neither
proved nor disproved by this continuation.
