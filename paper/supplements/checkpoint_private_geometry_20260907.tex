\section{Private depth and independent elliptic geometry}
\label{sec:private-geometry-checkpoint}

This continuation retains two distinct approaches to the remaining
arithmetic gates.  Exact residual collisions eliminate the common large
factor from a boundary gcd and yield a pointwise bound for all depth that
is repeated at another residual.  A joint finite residue object then gives
an exact large-prime excess law and a first-minimum distribution estimate.
Its full-state entropy transfer has a proved cardinality obstruction.
Passing to the exact excess observable removes that irrelevant loss, but
the resulting entropy is comparable to the positive excess itself; an
independent arithmetic estimate is still required.

The geometric direction gives an infinite primitive positive family whose
second norm is a square, together with an explicit elliptic parametrization.
A different two-isogeny descent proves that the product of the first two
norms is neither a rational square nor three times a rational square.
Thus simultaneous even extraction with square residual norms is impossible,
including moving roots.  Odd exponents and nonsquare residuals remain
available.  A dynamic family of square-root covers has explicitly computed
genus and gives a further fixed-square-class finiteness theorem; it supplies
no uniform height estimate over moving twists.

The ordinary arguments were developed and independently reviewed before
formalization.  Twenty-seven new Lean declarations verify exact residual
cancellation, annotated determinant inequalities, the elementary local
obstructions used in the descent, and actual polynomial identities.  They
do not constitute formal verification of the full elliptic descent or the
probability arguments.  Neither the forward signed-tail gate nor an
unbounded family satisfying the earlier radical disproof condition is
proved in this continuation.  Standard $abc$ remains unproved and undisproved
in this manuscript.
