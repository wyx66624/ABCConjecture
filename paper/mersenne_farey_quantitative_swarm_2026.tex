\section{A quantitative kernel for the Mersenne Farey swarm}
\label{sec:mersenne-farey-quantitative-swarm}

This section turns the finite denominator split of
Section~\ref{sec:mersenne-farey-denominator-entropy} into a kernel-checked
quantitative and subsequential statement.  It also records a sharp
counterexample boundary.  No estimate for the distribution of base-two
super-Wieferich primes is inserted.

Retain the notation
\[
 \mathcal H_T=\sum_{q=1}^{T}\frac1q,
 \qquad C_H=\sum_{r=1}^{H-1}r,
\]
and write $E_{\le T}$ and $E_{>T}$ for the two pieces of the Farey energy.

\begin{proposition}[Harmonic and square prefix bounds]
\label{prop:mersenne-harmonic-square-prefix}
For every $T\ge1$,
\[
 \mathcal H_T\le1+\log T.
\]
If every denominator fibre is contained in $\{1,\ldots,H-1\}$, then
\[
 E_{\le T}\le H^2(1+\log T).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first sum is the classical harmonic number.  Isolating its first term
and comparing the remaining decreasing summand with the integral of $1/x$
gives the first inequality.  The exact denominator split gives
$E_{\le T}\le C_H\mathcal H_T$, while
$C_H=H(H-1)/2\le H^2$.
\end{proof}

The common-index scale itself also admits an unconditional formal bracket.
For $M_n=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,n)$,
\[
 n\log2-\log(n+1)\le\log M_n\le(\log4+4)n.
\]
Indeed, Mathlib identifies $\log M_n$ with Chebyshev's function $\psi(n)$.
The lower bound follows from $2^n\le(n+1)M_n$, and the upper bound is the
explicit elementary Chebyshev estimate for $\psi$.  This proves the correct
linear scale without the prime number theorem; the sharper ratio
$\log M_n/n\to1$ is not claimed.

\begin{theorem}[Cleared quantitative swarm]
\label{thm:mersenne-cleared-quantitative-swarm}
Let $T>0$.  Suppose the prefix fibres lie in
$\{1,\ldots,H-1\}$, all tail numerators are at most $H$, and
\[
 \varepsilon A\le E\le E_{\le T}+E_{>T},
 \qquad H^2(1+\log T)\le\kappa A.
\]
Then
\[
 T(\varepsilon-\kappa)A\le N_{>T}H.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:mersenne-harmonic-square-prefix} bounds the prefix by
$\kappa A$.  The tail must therefore carry at least
$(\varepsilon-\kappa)A$.  Since every tail row contributes at most $H/T$,
multiplication by $T$ proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

For $X\ge0$, put
\[
 \mathcal W_3(X)=\{p\le X:p\text{ prime and }p^3\mid2^{p-1}-1\}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Finite exact-order row transport]
\label{prop:mersenne-finite-row-super-wieferich}
Fix a common index $m$ and a finite set $S$ of actual exact-order endpoint
rows.  If every $x\in S$ satisfies
\[
 p_x\text{ prime},\qquad p_x^3\mid2^{d_x}-1,
 \qquad p_x\le X,
\]
then
\[
 |S|\le|\mathcal W_3(X)|.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Exact order gives $d_x=\operatorname{ord}_{p_x}(2)$, so the depth transport
theorem yields $p_x^3\mid2^{p_x-1}-1$.  Hence the prime-coordinate map lands
in $\mathcal W_3(X)$.  The same exact-order identity makes that coordinate
map injective on endpoint rows.  Restricting the injection to $S$ proves the
finite cardinality inequality.
\end{proof}

This statement closes the finite row-to-prime bookkeeping only for rows
whose primality, exact-order and depth premises have been proved.  It neither
turns arbitrary Farey rows into actual endpoint rows nor estimates the size
of $\mathcal W_3(X)$.

For a predicate $P_m$, write $\operatorname{Freq}(P_m)$ when
$\forall N\,\exists m\ge N:P_m$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact failure witness for little-oh]
\label{prop:mersenne-not-little-o-frequent}
If $f,g:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R_{\ge0}$, then
\[
 f\ne o(g)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists\varepsilon>0:\operatorname{Freq}(\varepsilon g(m)<f(m)).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The definition of $f=o(g)$ says that for every positive $\varepsilon$ the
inequality $|f(m)|\le\varepsilon|g(m)|$ holds eventually.  Nonnegativity
removes the absolute values.  Negating the two quantifiers gives precisely
the displayed frequent strict reverse inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Frequent quantitative swarm]
\label{cor:mersenne-frequent-quantitative-swarm}
Suppose $E_m,A_m\ge0$, $E\ne o(A)$, and the finite packet conditions hold
eventually.  If for every $\delta>0$ one eventually has
\[
 H_m^2(1+\log T_m)\le\frac{\delta}{2}A_m,
\]
then for some fixed $\varepsilon>0$,
\[
 \operatorname{Freq}\left(
 T_m\frac{\varepsilon}{2}A_m\le N_{>T_m}(m)H_m
 \right).
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:mersenne-not-little-o-frequent} supplies one fixed
$\varepsilon>0$ and a frequent set on which
$\varepsilon A_m<E_m$.  Intersect it with the eventual structural set and
apply Theorem~\ref{thm:mersenne-cleared-quantitative-swarm} with
$\kappa=\varepsilon/2$.
\end{proof}

The coefficient of the exact prefix estimate cannot be uniformly reduced.
Indeed, take the full fibres $R_q=\{1,\ldots,H-1\}$.  Then
\[
 E_{\le T}=C_H\mathcal H_T.
\]
For $T=1$ and $H=2$, all three quantities equal one.  Hence any proposed
constant $c<1$ satisfying
$E_{\le T}\le cC_H\mathcal H_T$ for every admissible packet would force
$1\le c$, a contradiction.  This full-premise example deletes only that
strict universal improvement.  It leaves the arithmetic route intact:
future gain must use primality, exact order, depth, or cross-fibre
correlation.

The Lean module
\texttt{MersenneFareyQuantitativeSwarm20260903} proves all statements in
this section at their displayed quantifiers.  Its theorem-level dependency
union is only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound}.  The critical upper bound
\[
 \limsup_{x\to\infty}
 \frac{\log\max\{1,W_3(x)\}}{\log\log x}\le\frac12
\]
remains an open arithmetic input, so neither the Mersenne endpoint nor the
abc conjecture is proved here.
