\section{An admissible log-volume model and an explicit integer bridge}
\label{sec:iut-admissible-volume-integer-bridge}

The empty-set obstruction in
Section~\ref{sec:iut-current-concrete-implication} identifies a defect in one
total real-valued interface, rather than in Haar scaling.  We give an explicit
extensional repair and then state the exact comparison data that transfer the
abstract general-position inequality to the repository's integer target.

Fix $s:X\to X$ and $\delta\in\mathbb R$.  An admissible preimage log-volume
consists of an inhabited region type $\mathcal R$, an injective carrier map
$C:\mathcal R\to\mathcal P(X)$ with $C(U)\ne\varnothing$, a map
$P:\mathcal R\to\mathcal R$, and $V:\mathcal R\to\mathbb R$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:admissible-volume-interface}
 C(PU)=s^{-1}C(U),\qquad V(PU)=V(U)+\delta.
\end{equation}
Injectivity prevents assigning several volumes to the same underlying set;
the typed operation $P$ supplies the required closure proof.

\begin{theorem}[Valuation-ball model]
\label{thm:padic-admissible-volume-model}
For every rational prime $p$, the interface
\eqref{eq:admissible-volume-interface} with $X=\mathbb Q_p$,
$s(x)=px$, and $\delta=\log p$ is inhabited.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $k\in\mathbb Z$, let
\[
 B_k=\{x\in\mathbb Q_p:|x|_p\le p^{-k}\},\qquad
 P(k)=k-1,\qquad V(k)=-k\log p.
\]
Every ball contains zero.  Since $|p|_p=p^{-1}$,
$px\in B_k$ if and only if $x\in B_{k-1}$, proving carrier closure.
The inclusions among these balls recover the integer index, so the carrier
map is injective.  Finally
$V(k-1)=-k\log p+\log p=V(k)+\log p$.
\end{proof}

The shift law also imposes an exact consistency condition.  Induction gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:admissible-volume-iterate}
                      V(P^nU)=V(U)+n\delta.
\end{equation}
If $\delta\ne0$, no positive iterate can fix $U$; if $P^mU=P^nU$, applying
$V$ and cancelling $\delta$ gives $m=n$.  Every orbit is therefore infinite.
This supplies full-premise contradictions to proposed repairs with a fixed
region, a positive-period cycle, or only finitely many regions.  The
valuation lattice passes all three tests.  It is a model only of this local
scaling layer, not a construction of LANA's tensor packets or of IUT.

We next make the abstract-to-integer bridge non-tautological.  Let $T$ be a
height theory, let $P_{abc}$ be the set of positive primitive triples, and put
\[
 H(a,b,c)=\log\max(a,b,c),\qquad
 R(a,b,c)=\log\rad(abc).
\]
A uniform tripod comparison consists of an encoding
$\iota:P_{abc}\to T.\mathrm{Pt}(T.\mathrm{tripod})$, membership of every
$\iota(t)$ in the degree-at-most-one set, and constants $A_H,A_R\in\mathbb R$
such that
\begin{align}
 H(t)&\le h_{\rm can}(\iota(t))+A_H,
 \label{eq:iut-height-comparison}\\
 \log\mathrm{Diff}(\iota(t))+\log\mathrm{Cond}(\iota(t))
 &\le R(t)+A_R.
 \label{eq:iut-radical-comparison}
\end{align}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-discrepancy transfer]
\label{thm:statementI-integer-transfer}
If $T.\mathrm{StatementI}$ and a uniform tripod comparison are supplied,
then the repository's exact \texttt{ABCConjecture} holds.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix $\epsilon>0$.  Apply $T.\mathrm{StatementI}$ to the tripod, degree bound
one, and $\epsilon$.  It supplies $C_\epsilon$ such that every encoded point
satisfies
\[
 h_{\rm can}(\iota(t))\le(1+\epsilon)
  (\log\mathrm{Diff}(\iota(t))+\log\mathrm{Cond}(\iota(t)))+C_\epsilon.
\]
Since $1+\epsilon>0$, the two comparisons give
\[
 H(t)\le(1+\epsilon)R(t)+
   C_\epsilon+A_H+(1+\epsilon)A_R.
\]
The final three terms form one constant independent of $t$, which is exactly
the required quantifier order.
\end{proof}

Each field is logically necessary at this level.  For example, on
$\mathbb N$, $h=k=R=0$ and $H(n)=n$ satisfy the source inequality and radical
comparison but refute every uniform target without the height comparison.
Taking $h=k=H=n$ and $R=0$ does the same without the radical comparison.
Restricting the source inequality to the empty set shows why degree
membership is necessary, and replacing $A_H$ by the point-dependent error
$A_H(n)=n$ destroys uniformity.  These are full-premise countermodels within
this bare sequence-transfer schema to the corresponding weakened bridges.
They are not abc counterexamples and do not retire the IUT/same-pilot research
route.  Constructing the intended degree-one
encoding and proving \eqref{eq:iut-height-comparison}--
\eqref{eq:iut-radical-comparison} remain open arithmetic work.
