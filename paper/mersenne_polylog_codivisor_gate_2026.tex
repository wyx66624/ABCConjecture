\section{Fixed-polylogarithmic co-divisors at the Mersenne endpoint}
\label{sec:mersenne-polylog-codivisor}

The squared-loglog localization in
Corollary~\ref{cor:localized-exact-mersenne-gate} can be sharpened without
assuming a stronger concentration theorem.  The sharpening proved here is a
family of fixed windows with a quantified outer limit.  The present moment
argument does not show that the complement of one fixed window is negligible.

Retain $a_d=\log E_d$ and put
\[
 B_\epsilon(m)=
   \sum_{\substack{d\mid m\\a_d>\epsilon\varphi(d)}}\varphi(d),
 \qquad \Lambda_m=\log\log(3m).
\]
For a fixed positive integer $k$, define
\[
 B^{\mathrm{poly}}_{\epsilon,k}(m)=
 \sum_{\substack{d\mid m\\
       \log(m/d)<k\Lambda_m\\a_d>\epsilon\varphi(d)}}\varphi(d).
\]
Writing $q=m/d$, its support is the genuinely polylogarithmic range
\[
             q<\exp(k\Lambda_m)=\{\log(3m)\}^{k}.
\]

Let
\[
 \Delta(m)=\frac1m\sum_{d\mid m}\varphi(d)\log\frac md.
\]
The exact moment formula in
Section~\ref{sec:mersenne-totient-concentration}, together with the
elementary Chebyshev bound, supplies constants $C_0>0$ and $m_0$ such that
 $\Delta(m)\le C_0\Lambda_m$ for $m\ge m_0$.  Finite weighted Markov therefore
gives, for every fixed $k\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fixed-polylog-far-tail}
 \sum_{\substack{d\mid m\\k\Lambda_m\le\log(m/d)}}\varphi(d)
 \le \frac{C_0}{k}m.
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:polylog-exceptional-approximation}
 0\le B_\epsilon(m)-B^{\mathrm{poly}}_{\epsilon,k}(m)
 \le\frac{C_0}{k}m.
\end{equation}
The right side is uniform in $m$, but it is not $o(m)$ for one fixed $k$.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-polylogarithmic co-divisor gate]
\label{thm:fixed-polylog-mersenne-gate}
For the actual canonical Mersenne blocks,
\[
 \log W_m=o(m)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{for every $\epsilon>0$ and every fixed $k\ge1$,}\quad
 B^{\mathrm{poly}}_{\epsilon,k}(m)=o(m).
\]
The same equivalence holds when $k$ ranges over all fixed positive real
numbers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cyclotomic bound
$0\le a_d\le(\log3)\varphi(d)$ and the exceptional-mass criterion give
\[
 \log W_m=o(m)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 B_\epsilon(m)=o(m)\quad\hbox{for every }\epsilon>0.
\]
The forward implication follows by restricting a nonnegative sum.  For the
reverse implication, fix $\epsilon>0$ and a requested normalized error
$\eta>0$.  First choose one integer $k>2C_0/\eta$.  This $k$ is now fixed,
so the hypothesis eventually gives
$B^{\mathrm{poly}}_{\epsilon,k}(m)<\eta m/2$.  Equation
\eqref{eq:polylog-exceptional-approximation} then gives
$B_\epsilon(m)<\eta m$.  Letting $\eta$ vary proves the required little-oh
statement.  Integer windows imply the real-window formulation, while the
converse follows by specialization.
\end{proof}

The order of quantifiers in the proof is essential:
\[
 \forall\eta>0\ \exists k=k(\eta)\ \forall m\ge m_0(\eta,k).
\]
An abstract primorial divisor model shows that, for general
totient-bounded masses, the far tail for a single fixed $k$ need not be
$o(m)$.  Thus the Markov and moment estimates alone cannot replace the
theorem by a one-window assertion.  This model is not the actual Mersenne
mass $\log E_d$ and does not refute a future Mersenne-specific one-window
strengthening.

There is a useful dichotomy in the negation.  If the endpoint fails, then
for some fixed $\epsilon,\eta,k>0$ and an unbounded sequence $m_j$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:polylog-failure-packet}
 \sum_{\substack{q\mid m_j\\q<\{\log(3m_j)\}^{k}\\
   \log E_{m_j/q}>\epsilon\varphi(m_j/q)}}
 \frac{\varphi(m_j/q)}{m_j}\ge\eta.
\end{equation}
Let $\mathcal Q_j$ be the set of co-divisors counted in
\eqref{eq:polylog-failure-packet}, and let $M_j$ be its largest summand.
After passage to a subsequence, exactly one of the following pressure
regimes remains.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $M_j\ge\tau>0$, then
$\varphi(m_j/q)/m_j\le1/q$ forces $q\le1/\tau$; a further subsequence fixes
$q=q_0$.  With $d_j=m_j/q_0$ one obtains
\[
 \frac{\varphi(d_j)}{d_j}\ge q_0\tau,\qquad
 \frac{\log E_{d_j}}{d_j}>\epsilon q_0\tau.
\]
This is the atomic, fixed-co-divisor obstruction and still contains the
prime-index problem.
\item If $M_j\to0$, the packet contains at least $\eta/M_j\to\infty$
exceptional co-divisors, and
\[
             \sum_{q\in\mathcal Q_j}\log E_{m_j/q}
             >\epsilon\eta m_j.
\]
This is the diffuse multi-fibre obstruction.
\end{enumerate}
The transition, extreme-order, and deep-lift decomposition of
Section~\ref{sec:mersenne-near-diagonal-triage} applies inside the same
fixed-polylogarithmic packet.

The prime-index test confirms that no core difficulty has been hidden: for
$m=\ell$ prime, $q=1$ lies in every window with weight
$(\ell-1)/\ell$, so the theorem still requires $\log E_\ell=o(\ell)$.
The certified occurrences $1093\mid E_{364}$ and $3511\mid E_{1755}$
refute small universal pointwise constants but are finite and do not refute
the eventual gate.  Conversely, the sparse primorial model from the earlier
totient audit satisfies every fixed-$k$ gate while its pointwise normalized
blocks need not converge to zero.  These tests verify both directions of the
quantifier boundary and produce no full-premise counterexample to the actual
Mersenne endpoint.

The Lean companion proves the exact exceptional-set partition, the finite
$C_0/k$ estimate, a general uniform-in-fixed-index transfer theorem, and the
displayed equivalence for the repository's actual
\texttt{mersennePowerLoss}.  All moment and cyclotomic estimates are reused
from previously checked theorems; no distribution hypothesis enters the
kernel.
