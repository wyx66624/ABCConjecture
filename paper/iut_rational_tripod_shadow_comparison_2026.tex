\section{The exact rational-tripod shadow and its semantic boundary}
\label{sec:iut-rational-tripod-shadow}

The comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:iut-admissible-volume-integer-bridge} has an exact arithmetic
realization on the open rational tripod
\[
                 U(\Q)=\{x\in\Q:0<x<1\}.
\]
For a positive primitive triple $t=(a,b,c)$, put $\lambda(t)=a/c$.  Conversely,
if $x=m/n$ is reduced in $U(\Q)$, set $t(x)=(m,n-m,n)$.  These maps are
inverse.  With $H_{\rm tr}$ the rational Weil height and $N_{\rm tr}$ the
truncated counting function for the three marked points $0,1,\infty$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rational-tripod-zero-error}
 H_{\rm tr}(\lambda(t))=\log c=\log\max(a,b,c),\qquad
 N_{\rm tr}(\lambda(t))=\log\rad(abc).
\end{equation}
The first identity follows because $a/c$ is reduced and $0<a<c$; the second
follows because the supports of $a/c$ and $1-a/c=b/c$ have union exactly the
prime support of $abc$.

Define a one-curve shadow height theory $T_{\rm rat}$.  Its point set is
$U(\Q)$; its degree-at-most-zero locus is empty and every point has exact
degree one; and
\[
 h_{\rm can}=H_{\rm tr},\qquad \log\mathrm{Diff}=0,
 \qquad \log\mathrm{Cond}=N_{\rm tr}.
\]
The remaining structural fields have elementary witnesses.  The identity
map on $U(\Q)$ therefore gives a degree-one comparison, while
\eqref{eq:rational-tripod-zero-error} gives both normalization errors equal
to zero.

\begin{theorem}[Exact shadow boundary]
\label{thm:rational-tripod-shadow-iff-abc}
Statement~I for $T_{\rm rat}$ is equivalent to the standard integer
$abc$ conjecture:
\[
            T_{\rm rat}.\mathrm{StatementI}
            \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
            \mathrm{ABCConjecture}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The forward implication is the bounded-discrepancy transfer of
Section~\ref{sec:iut-admissible-volume-integer-bridge}, with both errors zero.
For the reverse implication, integer $abc$ transfers through the inverse
reduced triple $t(x)$ to the uniform rational-tripod inequality.  Since the
shadow has one curve, the degree-zero case is empty and every positive degree
bound contains all of $U(\Q)$, that inequality is exactly its Statement~I.
\end{proof}

For an arbitrary height theory $T$, an open-rational tripod comparison is a
map from $U(\Q)$ to the degree-at-most-one points of the distinguished tripod,
together with uniform height and radical errors.  Composition with
$t\mapsto a/c$ turns this into the integer comparison package.  Composition
with $x\mapsto t(x)$ gives the converse, with the same constants.  Thus the
rational and integer semantic comparison packages have exactly the same
logical strength.

Three full-premise models delimit what the current bare structural fields can
prove.  First, a one-point theory with every degree locus empty satisfies
Statement~I vacuously but admits no comparison from the nonempty set $U(\Q)$.
Second, on the actual rational points take
\[
 h_{\rm can}=0,
 \quad\log\mathrm{Diff}=0,
 \quad\log\mathrm{Cond}=N_{\rm tr}.
\]
Statement~I, degree membership, and the radical identity all hold, but no
uniform height error exists because $H_{\rm tr}(1/(n+1))=\log(n+1)$ is
unbounded.  Third, take
\[
 h_{\rm can}=H_{\rm tr},
 \quad\log\mathrm{Diff}=e^{2H_{\rm tr}},
 \quad\log\mathrm{Cond}=N_{\rm tr}.
\]
Statement~I follows from $e^{2H}\ge1+2H\ge H$ for $H\ge0$, and the height
comparison is exact, but a uniform radical error would bound
$e^{2H_{\rm tr}}$, again impossible.

These are counterexamples to automatic degree realization and to automatic
uniform normalization from the present abstract fields.  They are not
counterexamples to the intended arithmetic height theory, IUT, Statement~I
for that theory, or $abc$.  The positive route remains the source-level
construction of the rational point, its degree-one membership, and the two
triple-independent normalization constants in the intended objects.  The
Lean companion constructs the shadow, proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:rational-tripod-shadow-iff-abc}, and verifies all three
pressure models without placeholders or new axioms.
