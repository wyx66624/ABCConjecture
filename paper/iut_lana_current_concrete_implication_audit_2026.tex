\section{Audit of the current concrete IUT implication}
\label{sec:iut-current-concrete-implication}

After the preceding packet work was completed, the public LANA IUT
repository added a conditional implication strand.  We audited commit
\href{https://github.com/lana-agents/iut/tree/6e963070c73c5defd1012320deccc777e2555d22}
{\texttt{6e963070}}.  Its main declaration has
the schematic type
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lana-concrete-conditional-type}
\begin{split}
 &\mathsf P(T)\to(\forall K,d,\ \mathsf C_{22}(T,K,d))\to\\
 &\quad(\forall K,d,\ \mathsf E(K,d))\to
 \mathsf{Cheb}\to\mathsf{PNT}\to\\
 &\quad(\forall D,LT,TL,QI,\ \mathsf C_{312}(D,LT,TL,QI))\to\\
 &\quad \texttt{ABC}(T),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathsf P(T)$ abbreviates \texttt{T.ProofPackage},
$\mathsf C_{22}(T,K,d)$ abbreviates \texttt{Corollary22Inputs}$(T,K,d)$,
and $\mathsf{Cheb},\mathsf{PNT}$ denote the two stated prime bounds.  The
remaining abbreviations are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf E(K,d)&:=
   \texttt{ConcreteThetaDataExistence}(I(K,d)),\\
 \mathsf C_{312}(D,LT,TL,QI)&:=
   \texttt{Corollary312Variant}\bigl(\\[-2pt]
 &\hspace{35mm}\texttt{concreteVariantData}(D,LT,TL,QI)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $LT:\texttt{LocalTheory}(D.K_t)$ and
$TL:\texttt{ThetaLocalData}(D,LT)$.  Moreover,
\[
                \texttt{ABC}(T):=T.\texttt{StatementI}.
\]
Thus \eqref{eq:lana-concrete-conditional-type} is a proved conditional
implication, not a construction of its inputs and not a closed proof of the
integer statement \eqref{eq:abc}.

The proof graph is nevertheless useful.  Concrete theta-data existence is
converted to theta-data existence restricted by a predicate; its invariants,
certificate and local estimate enter Theorem~1.10; the assumed Corollary
3.12 variant and the prime bounds yield Corollary~2.2; finite exceptional
sets are absorbed to prove $T.\texttt{StatementII}$; finally the
\texttt{genl} proof package proves
\[
        T.\texttt{StatementII}\Longrightarrow T.\texttt{StatementI}.
\]
Every source assumption remains visible in the type.

The new concrete interface has, however, a complete elementary
counterexample.  Its local log-volume field requires, for every finite label
type $\iota$, every prime $p$, every place tuple $c$, and every set $U$ in
the tensor packet,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:latest-local-theory-total-shift}
 V\bigl((x\mapsto px)^{-1}U\bigr)=V(U)+\log p,
 \qquad V:\mathcal P(A)\longrightarrow\R.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Empty-set obstruction to the current \texttt{LocalTheory}]
\label{thm:latest-local-theory-empty}
At the audited commit, \texttt{LocalTheory}$(K)$ is empty for every number
field $K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Take the empty finite label type, its unique place tuple, $p=2$, and
$U=\varnothing$.  The preimage of the empty set under every function is
empty, so \eqref{eq:latest-local-theory-total-shift} becomes
\[
                        V(\varnothing)=V(\varnothing)+\log2.
\]
As $V(\varnothing)$ is a finite real number, subtraction gives
$\log2=0$, contradicting $\log2>0$.
\end{proof}

This counterexample satisfies every premise of the displayed volume law.
It therefore rejects that exact total-real-valued specification.  It does
not reject the Haar scaling identity on nonempty measurable sets of finite
positive measure.  The upstream change that adds nonemptiness to the
packet-product combination law does not repair
\eqref{eq:latest-local-theory-total-shift}, where $U$ remains unrestricted.
Each nonvacuous witness requested by
\texttt{ConcreteThetaDataExistence} contains a term of
\texttt{LocalTheory}; consequently the advertised concrete branch cannot
currently be instantiated.  The definition is implication-valued and may
still hold vacuously in a degenerate model with no qualifying input point,
which is not the intended theta-data construction.

There is a second independent boundary.  The repository target
\texttt{ABCConjecture} is the explicit integer inequality
\eqref{eq:abc}, whereas $T.\texttt{StatementI}$ is an abstract
bounded-discrepancy inequality for hyperbolic curves.  No checked
declaration in the audited upstream snapshot proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:missing-statementI-integer-bridge}
                 T.\texttt{StatementI}\Longrightarrow
                 \texttt{ABCConjecture}.
\end{equation}
A proof of \eqref{eq:missing-statementI-integer-bridge} must construct the
intended height theory, send every primitive positive triple to a
degree-one tripod point, compare the canonical height with
$\log\max(a,b,c)$, compare log-different plus log-conductor with
$\log\rad(abc)$, and unpack bounded discrepancy into one uniform additive
constant.

We therefore retain the reproducible pin
\texttt{ddaddc274281adb5674d647e24fa478745ac6d40}.  A direct update would
also change packet summands from fields to commutative rings, integral
subrings to sets, and nonzero hull scales to units.  The refined-factor
signature then needs an explicit equivalence of each tensor packet with a
finite product of fields.  A successful compilation after an immediate pin
change would not cure the empty-set contradiction.

The constructive continuation is precise:
\begin{enumerate}
\item restrict logarithmic volume to a proof-carrying domain of measurable
finite positive-volume regions, and prove closure under the scaling
preimages;
\item construct an inhabited local theory on that corrected domain;
\item provide the finite-product-of-fields decomposition needed by the
refined zero-aware coordinates;
\item construct the compatible $2\ell$-th Tate-parameter roots and the
remaining concrete theta data;
\item prove the actual Corollary 3.12 statement; and
\item prove the integer bridge \eqref{eq:missing-statementI-integer-bridge}.
\end{enumerate}
Difficulty of these steps does not eliminate the corrected IUT route.

The companion Lean audit proves the universal empty-set theorem for any
real-valued set-volume with the unrestricted prime-preimage law, specializes
the already pinned public no-go, and introduces the honest proposition
\[
 \texttt{StatementIToIntegerABCBridge}(T):=
 T.\texttt{StatementI}\to\texttt{ABCConjecture}.
\]
It proves only that a supplied term of this bridge consumes a supplied term
of $T.\texttt{StatementI}$.  No bridge or IUT premise is populated by a
hidden axiom.
