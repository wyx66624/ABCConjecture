\begin{abstract}
We attach an oriented exterior two-cell to the prime-divisor triangle of a
primitive abc point.  We prove its positive mixed-area formula, affine and
$S_3$ covariance, and the image under $\bigwedge^2 d$ of the standard Bloch
five-term boundary.
Its quadratic specialization exactly peels a square cell into two
linear-entry cells.
An infinite family of primitive Pythagorean squares rigorously refutes two
natural single-cell radical-truncation gates.  Separating coherent Veronese
thickness from primitive-base residual thickness, together with an automatic
filling-boundary inequality, leaves two calibrated five-term cost estimates
whose status is open.  No proof or disproof of the abc conjecture is claimed.
\end{abstract}
