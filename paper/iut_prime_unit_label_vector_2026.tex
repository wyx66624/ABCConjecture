\section{A faithful prime-unit-label vector bridge}
\label{sec:iut-prime-unit-label-vector}

The scalar holonomy ledger in Section~\ref{sec:holonomy-density-depth}
cannot reconstruct a pilot: two different positive normalized labelled
packets may have the same one-dimensional weighted value.  We therefore
retain the rational prime, complete unit part, and fixed label until after
the object comparison.  IUT~III \cite{IUT3} distinguishes unit/coric data and
mono-analytic log-shells before the final log-volume operation.  The pinned
Project LANA container \cite{LANA,LANAlean} likewise retains the rational
place fibre and procession labels in a packet component.  These sources
motivate the coordinate type; they do not prove that the actual multiradial
transport preserves the coordinates below.

Fix a rational prime $p$.  For $x\in\Q^\times$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-coordinate}
 e_p(x)=v_p(x),\qquad
 u_p(x)=\frac{x}{p^{e_p(x)}},\qquad
 C_p(x)=(e_p(x),u_p(x)).
\end{equation}
Negative powers have their ordinary field meaning.

\begin{theorem}[Exact prime-unit reconstruction]
\label{thm:pul-local-reconstruction}
For every $x\in\Q^\times$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-reconstruct}
 v_p(u_p(x))=0,\qquad p^{e_p(x)}u_p(x)=x.
\end{equation}
Consequently $C_p$ is injective.  The identical assertions hold on the
actual complete local field $\mathbb Q_p^\times$ with its normalized
additive valuation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Valuation additivity and $v_p(p)=1$ give
\[
 v_p(u_p(x))=v_p(x)-e_p(x)v_p(p)=0.
\]
The second equality in \eqref{eq:pul-reconstruct} is cancellation of the
same nonzero field power used in the definition.  Equal coordinate pairs
therefore have equal reconstruction expressions.  This proves injectivity.
The proof uses only the corresponding field and valuation identities in
$\mathbb Q_p$ and is unchanged there.
\end{proof}

There is also a valuation-free field-summand template.  If $K$ is any field,
$\pi\in K^\times$, and $e:K^\times\to\Z$ is any function, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-field-scale}
 u_{\pi,e}(x)=\frac{x}{\pi^{e(x)}}\ne0,\qquad
 \pi^{e(x)}u_{\pi,e}(x)=x.
\end{equation}
Thus $x\mapsto(e(x),u_{\pi,e}(x))$ is injective.  When $e$ is a normalized
discrete valuation and $\pi$ a uniformizer, the first calculation in
Theorem~\ref{thm:pul-local-reconstruction} additionally proves that the
second coordinate is a genuine unit.  Hence the reconstruction mechanism
applies separately to every actual field summand once that summand and its
transport have been constructed.

Let $L$ be a label set and let a packet be a map
$P:L\to\Q^\times$.  Define its complete labelled signature by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-labelled-signature}
             \Sigma_p(P)=\bigl(C_p(P(i))\bigr)_{i\in L}.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Labelled and region reconstruction]
\label{thm:pul-labelled-region}
The map $\Sigma_p$ is injective.  More generally, let $X$ be a carrier with
a family of exponent and unit coordinates whose joint value is faithful at
each point, and let $\Sigma$ be the corresponding labelled signature.
For packet regions $A,B\subseteq(L\to X)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-image-bridge}
                  \Sigma(A)\subseteq\Sigma(B)
                  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad A\subseteq B.
\end{equation}
If $\mu$ is a monotone finite real-valued region functional, then
$\mu(B)\le T$ and \eqref{eq:pul-image-bridge} imply $\mu(A)\le T$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equality of two signatures gives equality of both local coordinates at every
fixed label.  Theorem~\ref{thm:pul-local-reconstruction}, or the assumed
pointwise faithfulness, gives equality of the packet entries and then of the
packets.

For the region statement, take $P\in A$.  Image containment supplies
$Q\in B$ with $\Sigma(Q)=\Sigma(P)$.  Injectivity gives $Q=P$, hence
$P\in B$.  Monotonicity of $\mu$ and transitivity of the real order give the
last assertion.
\end{proof}

This is a non-circular route from vector data to a scalar bound.  It does not
assume equality of the regions or equality of their volumes.  It requires an
independently proved containment of complete signature images, followed by a
monotonicity theorem on the correct finite-positive-volume domain.

The unit coordinate also completes the holonomy ledger.  Suppose a labelled
transport $P\to Q$ has exponent shifts $\delta_i\in\Z$ and unit twists
$\tau_i\in\Q$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pul-vector-transport}
 e_p(Q(i))=e_p(P(i))+\delta_i,\qquad
 u_p(Q(i))=u_p(P(i))\tau_i.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Pointed vector holonomy]
\label{prop:pul-vector-holonomy}
Every twist in \eqref{eq:pul-vector-transport} is nonzero and has
$p$-adic valuation zero.  Transports compose labelwise by
\[
       (\delta,\tau)\circ(\epsilon,\upsilon)
       =(\delta+\epsilon,\tau\upsilon).
\]
If the labelled transport is a pointed loop, $Q=P$, then
$\delta_i=0$ and $\tau_i=1$ for every fixed label $i$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both unit parts in the second equation of
\eqref{eq:pul-vector-transport} are nonzero, so $\tau_i\ne0$.  Applying
$v_p$ and Theorem~\ref{thm:pul-local-reconstruction} gives
$0=0+v_p(\tau_i)$.  Substitution of two consecutive transport equations
gives the composition law.  If $Q=P$, cancellation in the exponent equation
gives $\delta_i=0$, and cancellation of the nonzero unit part gives
$\tau_i=1$.
\end{proof}

Each of the three retained layers is necessary for this particular
reconstruction theorem.

\begin{proposition}[Complete counterexamples to three weakened interfaces]
\label{prop:pul-three-counterexamples}
The following data satisfy all the indicated weakened hypotheses.
\begin{enumerate}
\item At $p=5$, the one-label packets $P=1$, $Q=2$, with weight one,
have the same exponent zero, the same fixed label, and the same positive
normalized weight, but are unequal.
\item At $p=5$, the one-label packets $P=1$, $Q=6$, with weight one,
have equal exponent zero and equal first unit residue modulo $5$, but are
unequal.
\item At $p=5$, take $P=(1,2)$, $Q=(2,1)$ and weights $(1/2,1/2)$.
Their multisets of complete coordinates agree after transposition.  At the
fixed labels the exponent shifts vanish and the unit twists are $(2,1/2)$,
whose product is one, but the labelled packets differ and neither twist is
one.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The integers $1,2,6$ are $5$-adic units, and $1\equiv6\pmod5$.
The stated weights are positive and sum to one.  In the two-label example,
transposition exchanges the two complete coordinates, while at fixed labels
the quotient unit twists are $2/1$ and $1/2$.  Their product is one and each
is nontrivial.  Every asserted inequality is immediate.
\end{proof}

The first example closes only exponent-only reconstruction; the second closes
only first-residue truncation; the third closes only unordered reconstruction
and aggregate unit-holonomy closure.  The third example does not refute a
transport that records and returns an explicit label permutation.

The active IUT target is therefore a source-level theorem on the actual common
carrier: through the theta link, log-Kummer correction, determinant
normalization, and every required Ind1--Ind3 branch, the globally synchronized
output must preserve the faithful prime-unit coordinate at every required
place and every fixed label, or at least contain the input signature image.
Theorem~\ref{thm:pul-labelled-region} would then transfer the output bound
back to the native pilot before taking scalar log-volume.  No such global
preservation theorem is proved here, and none of the finite counterexamples
disproves it.  The IUT route remains active.
