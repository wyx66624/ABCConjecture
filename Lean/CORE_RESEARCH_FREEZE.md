## Corrected local theta geometry

The previously studied substitution `r^ell = q`, `u = v^ell` produces an
angular/cyclotomic kernel `mu_ell`.  On the radial skeleton the induced map has
degree one.  It therefore cannot be the graph-direction
`Z/ell Z` cover required by Definition 2.5.  This skeleton-degree invariant is
a counterexample to that proposed identification, so that identification is
retired.

The corrected first local proposition uses the subgroup generated by
`q^ell`.  For odd `ell`, iterating theta automorphy gives

```text
theta(q^ell u)
  = q^(-ell*(ell+1)/2) * u^(-ell) * theta(u)
  = (q^(-(ell+1)/2) * u^(-1))^ell * theta(u).
```

Thus the Kummer locus `y^ell = theta(u)` is equivariant for the lifted
`q^ell` translation, while `<q>/<q^ell>` is the required cyclic graph quotient.
The exact mathematical proof and the Lean theorem names for the algebraic
equivariance are recorded in `CORE_PROOF_NOTEBOOK.md`; that part is formalized
in `IUTThreeClosures/TateThetaOddGraphDescent.lean`.  The abstract cyclic
